## Shri Bhaja Govindam (Mohamudgara)
**Complete Text, Word-by-Word Meaning, Translation & Significance**

---

### 1. Introduction & Spiritual Significance

'Bhaja Govindam' (also known as 'Mohamudgara' — the hammer that shatters delusion) is one of the most famous and awakening philosophical hymns in Advaita Vedanta. Legend has it that Adi Shankaracharya, while walking in Varanasi (Kashi), saw an old scholar intensely memorizing Sanskrit grammar rules ('Dukrinkarane') on his deathbed. Overcome with compassion for the man's ignorance and worldly attachment, Shankaracharya spontaneously composed this hymn. The first 12 verses were composed by Shankaracharya himself, the next 14 by his disciples, and the final 5 verses were a concluding blessing by the Guru.

* **Composer:** Adi Guru Shankaracharya and his 14 disciples
* **Presiding Deity:** Lord Govinda (Sri Krishna / Supreme Brahman)
* **Purpose (Viniyoga):** To shatter worldly delusions, cultivate deep dispassion (Vairagya), and awaken the seeker to self-realization and liberation (Moksha).

---

### 2. Sanskrit Verses, Word-by-Word Meaning & English Translation

#### ॥ Verse 1 ॥
भज गोविन्दं भज गोविन्दं गोविन्दं भज मूढमते ।
सम्प्राप्ते सन्निहिते काले न हि न हि रक्षति डुकृञ्करणे ॥ १॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Bhaja Govindaṃ** = Worship Govinda
* **Mūḍhamate** = O foolish mind!
* **Samprāpte sannihite kāle** = When the appointed time (of death) arrives
* **Na hi na hi rakṣati** = It will certainly not save you
* **Ḍukṛñkaraṇe** = The rules of grammar (mundane scholarly knowledge).

**Translation:**
Worship Govinda, worship Govinda, worship Govinda, O foolish mind! When the time of death approaches, the rules of grammar (mundane knowledge) will certainly not save you.

---
#### ॥ Verse 2 ॥
मूढ जहीहि धनागमतृष्णां कुरु सद्बुद्धिं मनसि वितृष्णाम् ।
यल्लभसे निजकर्मोपात्तं वित्तं तेन विनोदय चित्तम् ॥ २॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Mūḍha jahīhi dhanāgamatṛṣṇāṃ** = O fool, abandon the intense thirst for amassing wealth
* **Kuru sadbuddhiṃ manasi vitṛṣṇām** = Cultivate good thoughts in your mind, free from passion
* **Yallabhase nijakarmopāttaṃ** = Whatever you obtain through your own honest actions/past karma
* **Vittaṃ tena vinodaya cittam** = With that wealth, entertain/satisfy your mind.

**Translation:**
O fool! Abandon the intense thirst to amass wealth. Cultivate a pure, desireless intellect in your mind. Be content and keep your mind happy with whatever wealth you obtain as a result of your own honest karma.

---
#### ॥ Verse 3 ॥
नारीस्तनभरनाभीदेशं दृष्ट्वा मा गा मोहावेशम् ।
एतन्मांसवसादि विकारं मनसि विचिन्तय वारं वारम् ॥ ३॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Nārīstanabharanābhīdeśaṃ** = The physical beauty of a woman (breasts and navel region)
* **Dṛṣṭvā mā gā mohāveśam** = Having seen, do not fall prey to frenzied delusion/lust
* **Etanmāṃsavasādi vikāraṃ** = This is merely a modification of flesh, fat, etc.
* **Manasi vicintaya vāraṃ vāram** = Contemplate this truth in your mind again and again.

**Translation:**
Do not fall into the frenzy of lust and delusion upon seeing the physical beauty of a woman. Contemplate again and again in your mind that this body is nothing but a mere modification of flesh, fat, and blood.

---
#### ॥ Verse 4 ॥
नलिनीदलगतजलमतितरलं तद्वज्जीवितमतिशयचपलम् ।
विद्धि व्याध्यभिमानग्रस्तं लोकं शोकहतं च समस्तम् ॥ ४॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Nalinīdalagatajalam ati-taralaṃ** = Just as a water drop on a lotus leaf is extremely unstable
* **Tadvajjīvitamatiśayacapalam** = In the exact same way, this life is exceedingly fleeting/unstable
* **Viddhi vyādhyabhimānagrastaṃ** = Know this to be afflicted by disease and ego
* **Lokaṃ śokahataṃ ca samastam** = And the entire world is struck by grief.

**Translation:**
Just as a drop of water resting on a lotus leaf is extremely unstable and tremulous, so too is human life exceedingly fleeting. Understand that this entire world is consumed by disease, ego, and grief.

---
#### ॥ Verse 5 ॥
यावद्वित्तोपार्जनसक्तः तावन्निजपरिवारो रक्तः ।
पश्चाज्जीवति जर्जरदेहे वार्तां कोऽपि न पृच्छति गेहे ॥ ५॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Yāvad-vittopārjana-saktaḥ** = As long as one is capable of earning wealth
* **Tāvan-nijaparivāro raktaḥ** = Till then, one's own family is attached/affectionate
* **Paścājjīvati jarjaradehe** = Later, when one lives with a decrepit/old body
* **Vārtāṃ ko'pi na pṛcchati gehe** = No one in the house even inquires about their welfare.

**Translation:**
As long as a man is capable of earning wealth, his family remains deeply attached to him. But later, when his body becomes old and decrepit, no one in the house even bothers to ask about his well-being.

---
#### ॥ Verse 6 ॥
यावत्पवनो निवसति देहे तावत्पृच्छति कुशलं गेहे ।
गतवति वायौ देहापाये भार्या बिभ्यति तस्मिन्काये ॥ ६॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Yāvatpavano nivasati dehe** = As long as the breath (wind) resides in the body
* **Tāvatpṛcchati kuśalaṃ gehe** = Till then, people in the house ask about your welfare
* **Gatavati vāyau dehāpāye** = When the breath departs and the body perishes
* **Bhāryā bibhyati tasminkāye** = Even the wife is terrified of that very body.

**Translation:**
As long as breath remains in the body, family members inquire about your welfare. But the moment the breath departs and the body becomes a corpse, even one's own wife is frightened of that very body.

---
#### ॥ Verse 7 ॥
बालस्तावत्क्रीडासक्तः तरुणस्तावत्तरुणीसक्तः ।
वृद्धस्तावच्चिन्तासक्तः परे ब्रह्मणि कोऽपि न सक्तः ॥ ७॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Bālastāvat krīḍāsaktaḥ** = In childhood, one is attached to play
* **Taruṇastāvat taruṇīsaktaḥ** = In youth, one is attached to young women/lust
* **Vṛddhastāvac cintāsaktaḥ** = In old age, one is attached to (drowning in) anxiety
* **Pare brahmaṇi ko'pi na saktaḥ** = But alas, no one is attached to the Supreme Brahman!

**Translation:**
In childhood, one is absorbed in play; in youth, one is engrossed in the pursuit of sensual pleasures; and in old age, one is consumed by anxieties. Alas! No one seeks to be absorbed in the Supreme Brahman.

---
#### ॥ Verse 8 ॥
का ते कान्ता कस्ते पुत्रः संसारोऽयमतीव विचित्रः ।
कस्य त्वं कः कुत आयातः तत्त्वं चिन्तय तदिदं भ्रातः ॥ ८॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Kā te kāntā kaste putraḥ** = Who is your wife? Who is your son?
* **Saṃsāro'yam-atīva vicitraḥ** = This worldly existence is exceedingly strange/mysterious
* **Kasya tvaṃ kaḥ kuta āyātaḥ** = Whose are you? Who are you? Where have you come from?
* **Tattvaṃ cintaya tadidaṃ bhrātaḥ** = O brother, ponder over this ultimate truth!

**Translation:**
Who is your wife? Who is your son? This worldly illusion is exceedingly mysterious. Who do you really belong to? Who are you? Where have you come from? O brother, deeply contemplate this ultimate truth.

---
#### ॥ Verse 9 ॥
सत्सङ्गत्वे निस्सङ्गत्वं निस्सङ्गत्वे निर्मोहत्वम् ।
निर्मोहत्वे निश्चलतत्त्वं निश्चलतत्त्वे जीवन्मुक्तिः ॥ ९॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Satsaṅgatve nissaṅgatvaṃ** = Through the company of the good/truth comes detachment
* **Nissaṅgatve nirmohatvam** = Through detachment comes freedom from delusion
* **Nirmohatve niścalatattvaṃ** = Through freedom from delusion comes steadiness of mind
* **Niścalatattve jīvanmuktiḥ** = Through a steady mind comes liberation while living.

**Translation:**
The company of the wise leads to detachment from the world; detachment destroys delusion; the destruction of delusion leads to a steadfast, unwavering mind; and a steadfast mind leads to liberation even while living in this body (Jivanmukti).

---
#### ॥ Verse 10 ॥
वयसि गते कः कामविकारः शुष्के नीरे कः कासारः ।
क्षीणे वित्ते कः परिवारो ज्ञाते तत्त्वे कः संसारः ॥ १०॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Vayasi gate kaḥ kāmavikāraḥ** = When youth has passed, where is the lustful passion?
* **Śuṣke nīre kaḥ kāsāraḥ** = When the water dries up, where is the lake?
* **Kṣīṇe vitte kaḥ parivāro** = When wealth is exhausted, where is the family/entourage?
* **Jñāte tattve kaḥ saṃsāraḥ** = When the Supreme Truth is realized, where is the worldly bondage?

**Translation:**
When youth has faded, what meaning does lust hold? When the water dries up, what is the meaning of a lake? When wealth is depleted, where is the entourage of relatives? Similarly, when the Ultimate Truth is realized, where is this worldly bondage?

---
#### ॥ Verse 11 ॥
मा कुरु धनजनयौवनगर्वं हरति निमेषात्कालः सर्वम् ।
मायामयमिदमखिलं हित्वा ब्रह्मपदं त्वं प्रविश विदित्वा ॥ ११॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Mā kuru dhana-jana-yauvana-garvaṃ** = Do not boast of your wealth, people (power), and youth
* **Harati nimeṣātkālaḥ sarvam** = Time (Death) snatches all these away in the blink of an eye
* **Māyāmayam-idamakhiḷaṃ hitvā** = Having abandoned this entire illusionary world
* **Brahmapadaṃ tvaṃ praviśa viditvā** = Having realized the Truth, enter the state of Brahman.

**Translation:**
Do not be arrogant about your wealth, followers, and youth, for Time snatches them all away in the blink of an eye. Realize that this entire universe is an illusion; abandon it mentally, and enter the supreme state of Brahman.

---
#### ॥ Verse 12 ॥
दिनयामिन्यौ सायं प्रातः शिशिरवसन्तौ पुनरायातः ।
कालः क्रीडति गच्छत्यायुस्तदपि न मुञ्चत्याशावायुः ॥ १२॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Dina-yāminyau sāyaṃ prātaḥ** = Day and night, dusk and dawn
* **Śiśira-vasantau punarāyātaḥ** = Winter and spring come again and again
* **Kālaḥ krīḍati gacchatyāyuḥ** = Time plays on, and life ebbs away
* **Tadapi na muñcatyāśāvāyuḥ** = Yet, the storm of desires never leaves a person.

**Translation:**
Day and night, dusk and dawn, winter and spring come and go repeatedly. Time plays its game, and one's lifespan continuously diminishes. Yet, the gust of endless desires and hopes never leaves the human mind.

---
#### ॥ Verse 13 ॥
का ते कान्ता धनगतचिन्ता वातुल किं तव नास्ति नियन्ता ।
त्रिजगति सज्जनसङ्गतिरेका भवति भवार्णवतरणे नौका ॥ १३॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Kā te kāntā dhanagatacintā** = Who is your wife? Why this obsession with wealth?
* **Vātula kiṃ tava nāsti niyantā** = O distracted one! Is there no Supreme Controller for you?
* **Trijagati sajjanasaṅgatirekā** = In all the three worlds, the company of the wise alone
* **Bhavati bhavārṇavataraṇe naukā** = Is the boat to cross over the ocean of worldly existence.

**Translation:**
Who is your wife? Why are you so obsessed with accumulating wealth? O distracted soul, is there no guiding Lord for you? Know that in all three worlds, only the association of the wise and pure-hearted (Satsang) acts as a boat to cross this terrifying ocean of worldly existence.

---
#### ॥ Verse 14 ॥
जटिलो मुण्डी लुञ्छितकेशः काषायाम्बरबहुकृतवेषः ।
पश्यन्नपि च न पश्यति मूढो उदरनिमित्तं बहुकृतवेषः ॥ १४॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Jaṭilo muṇḍī luñchitakeśaḥ** = One with matted locks, one with a shaven head, one who plucks out his hair
* **Kāṣāyāmbara-bahukṛtaveṣaḥ** = One who wears ochre/saffron robes in various disguises
* **Paśyannapi ca na paśyati mūḍho** = This fool, even while seeing the truth, fails to see it
* **Udaranimittaṃ bahukṛtaveṣaḥ** = All these various disguises are merely for the sake of filling the belly.

**Translation:**
Some grow matted locks, some shave their heads, some pluck out their hair, and some wear saffron robes. The foolish man, though seeing the ultimate truth of death, chooses to ignore it. All these elaborate ascetic disguises are merely hypocritical means to fill one's stomach.

---
#### ॥ Verse 15 ॥
अङ्गं गलितं पलितं मुण्डं दशनविहीनं जातं तुण्डम् ।
वृद्धो याति गृहीत्वा दण्डं तदपि न मुञ्चत्याशापिण्डम् ॥ १५॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Aṅgaṃ galitaṃ palitaṃ muṇḍaṃ** = The limbs have withered, the head has turned grey
* **Daśanavihīnaṃ jātaṃ tuṇḍam** = The mouth has become toothless
* **Vṛddho yāti gṛhītvā daṇḍaṃ** = The old man walks leaning heavily on his walking stick
* **Tadapi na muñcatyāśāpiṇḍam** = Even then, he does not let go of the bundle of his desires.

**Translation:**
His body has grown decrepit, his hair has turned entirely white, and his mouth is completely toothless. The old man totters around leaning on a walking stick, yet, what a tragedy—he still refuses to let go of the heavy bundle of his worldly desires!

---
#### ॥ Verse 16 ॥
अग्रे वह्निः पृष्ठे भानुः रात्रौ चुबुकसमर्पितजानुः ।
करतलभिक्षस्तरुतलवासस्तदपि न मुञ्चत्याशापाशः ॥ १६॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Agre vahniḥ pṛṣṭhe bhānuḥ** = Warming himself with fire in front and the sun behind
* **Rātrau cubukasamarpitajānuḥ** = At night, sitting curled up with his chin tucked into his knees (due to cold)
* **Karatalabhikṣas-tarutalavāsas-** = Eating alms from his bare palms, sleeping under the trees
* **Tadapi na muñcatyāśāpāśaḥ** = Even then, the noose of desires does not leave him.

**Translation:**
He warms himself with fire in the front and the sun at his back; at night, shivering, he sits with his chin tucked into his knees. He begs for food in his palms and lives under trees. Yet, despite being in such a destitute state, he cannot free himself from the binding noose of worldly desires.

---
#### ॥ Verse 17 ॥
कुरुते गङ्गासागरगमनं व्रतपरिपालनमथवा दानम् ।
ज्ञानविहीनः सर्वमतेन मुक्तिं न भजति जन्मशतेन ॥ १७॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Kurute gaṅgāsāgaragamanaṃ** = One may go on a pilgrimage to Gangasagar
* **Vrataparipālanamathavā dānam** = Or strictly observe fasts, or give away massive wealth in charity
* **Jñānavihīnaḥ sarvamatena** = Yet, devoid of Self-knowledge, according to all scriptures/scholars
* **Muktiṃ na bhajati janmaśatena** = He cannot attain liberation even after a hundred lifetimes.

**Translation:**
A person may undertake grand pilgrimages to Gangasagar, strictly observe austere vows, or donate immense wealth. But it is the unanimous verdict of all scriptures that without true Self-knowledge (Atma-Jnana), one cannot attain liberation even after a hundred births.

---
#### ॥ Verse 18 ॥
सुरमन्दिरतरुमूलनिवासः शय्या भूतलमिजिनं वासः ।
सर्वपरिग्रहभोगत्यागः कस्य सुखं न करोति विरागः ॥ १८॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Suramandira-tarumūla-nivāsaḥ** = Residing in temples or at the roots of trees
* **Śayyā bhūtalam-ijinaṃ vāsaḥ** = Sleeping on the bare ground, wearing deer skin as clothing
* **Sarvaparigraha-bhogatyāgaḥ** = Completely renouncing all possessions and worldly enjoyments
* **Kasya sukhaṃ na karoti virāgaḥ** = To whom will such pure dispassion (Vairagya) not bring immense happiness?

**Translation:**
Living in temples or beneath trees, sleeping on the bare earth, wearing simple animal skins, and completely renouncing all worldly possessions and sensual enjoyments—whom will such pristine, desireless dispassion not fill with supreme peace and joy?

---
#### ॥ Verse 19 ॥
योगरतो वा भोगरतो वा सङ्गरतो वा सङ्गविहीनः ।
यस्य ब्रह्मणि रमते चित्तं नन्दति नन्दति नन्दत्येव ॥ १९॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Yogarato vā bhogarato vā** = Let one be engaged in Yoga (asceticism) or Bhoga (worldly enjoyment)
* **Saṅgarato vā saṅgavihīnaḥ** = Let one be amidst company or in absolute solitude
* **Yasya brahmaṇi ramate cittaṃ** = He whose mind constantly revels in the Supreme Brahman
* **Nandati nandati nandatyeva** = He alone enjoys, he alone enjoys, he alone truly enjoys!

**Translation:**
Whether a person is absorbed in ascetic Yoga or engaged in worldly activities, whether he lives in the company of people or in complete solitude—he whose mind is deeply anchored and revels in the Supreme Brahman is the one who truly rejoices, rejoices, and alone rejoices!

---
#### ॥ Verse 20 ॥
भगवद्गीता किञ्चिदधीता गङ्गाजललवकणिका पीता ।
सकृदपि येन मुरारिसमर्चा क्रियते तस्य यमेन न चर्चा ॥ २०॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Bhagavadgītā kiñcidadhītā** = He who has studied the Bhagavad Gita even a little
* **Gaṅgājala-lavakaṇikā pītā** = He who has drunk even a single drop of Ganga water with devotion
* **Sakṛdapi yena murārisamarcā** = He who has worshipped Lord Murari (Krishna) genuinely even once
* **Kriyate tasya yamena na carcā** = With him, Yama (the Lord of Death) has no discussion/argument.

**Translation:**
He who has studied the Bhagavad Gita even a little bit, who has sipped even a tiny drop of the sacred Ganga water with pure faith, and who has genuinely worshipped Lord Krishna even once in his life—the Lord of Death (Yamaraja) holds no sway over him.

---
#### ॥ Verse 21 ॥
पुनरपि जननं पुनरपि मरणं पुनरपि जननीजठरे शयनम् ।
इह संसारे बहुदुस्तारे कृपयाऽपारे पाहि मुरारे ॥ २१॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Punarapi jananaṃ punarapi maraṇaṃ** = Born again, dead again
* **Punarapi jananījaṭhare śayanam** = And repeatedly resting (suffering) in the mother's womb
* **Iha saṃsāre bahudustāre** = In this cycle of Samsara, which is extremely difficult to cross
* **Kṛpayā'pāre pāhi murāre** = O Lord Murari! Out of Your boundless compassion, please protect/rescue me.

**Translation:**
Born again, dead again, and doomed to suffer repeatedly in the mother's womb—such is the cycle of life. This ocean of Samsara is incredibly difficult to cross. O Lord Murari (Krishna), out of Your boundless compassion, please rescue and protect me!

---
#### ॥ Verse 22 ॥
रथ्याचर्पटविरचितकन्थः पुण्यापुण्यविवर्जितपन्थः ।
योगी योगनियोजितचित्तो रमते बालोन्मत्तवदेव ॥ २२॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Rathyācarpaṭa-viracitakanthaḥ** = One who wears garments made of rags cast off on the streets
* **Puṇyāpuṇya-vivarjitapanthaḥ** = Who walks the path that is beyond the dualities of merit (punya) and sin (apunya)
* **Yogī yoganiyojitacitto** = That Yogi, whose mind is perfectly fixed in Yoga (union with the Divine)
* **Ramate bālonmattavadeva** = Revels in his own bliss, behaving like a child or a madman.

**Translation:**
Wearing clothes pieced together from discarded street rags, walking a path entirely free from the dualities of merit and sin, the true Yogi—whose mind is deeply united with the Supreme—revels in sheer spiritual ecstasy, appearing to the world just like a carefree child or a madman.

---
#### ॥ Verse 23 ॥
कस्त्वं कोऽहं कुत आयातः का मे जननी को मे तातः ।
इति परिभावय सर्वमसारं विश्वं त्यक्त्वा स्वप्नविचारम् ॥ २३॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Kastvaṃ ko'haṃ kuta āyātaḥ** = Who are you? Who am I? From where have we come?
* **Kā me jananī ko me tātaḥ** = Who is my mother? Who is my father?
* **Iti paribhāvaya sarvamasāraṃ** = Inquire thus, and realize that all this is essence-less/meaningless
* **Viśvaṃ tyaktvā svapnavicāram** = And abandon this entire world, knowing it to be a mere dream.

**Translation:**
Who are you? Who am I? Where have we come from? Who is my mother? Who is my father? Ponder deeply upon these questions. Realize that this entire worldly existence is hollow and meaningless. Treat the universe as a mere dream and abandon your attachment to it.

---
#### ॥ Verse 24 ॥
त्वयि मयि चान्यत्रैको विष्णुर्व्यर्थं कुप्यसि मय्यसहिष्णुः ।
भव समचित्तः सर्वत्र त्वं वाञ्छस्यचिराद्यदि विष्णुत्वम् ॥ २४॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Tvayi mayi cānyatraiko viṣṇur-** = In you, in me, and everywhere else, there is only one Vishnu (Supreme Lord)
* **Vyarthaṃ kupyasi mayyasahiṣṇuḥ** = You become intolerant and angry with me completely in vain
* **Bhava samacittaḥ sarvatra tvaṃ** = Cultivate an equal, balanced vision everywhere/towards everyone
* **Vāñchasyacirādyadi viṣṇutvam** = If you desire to quickly attain 'Vishnutva' (the state of the Supreme/Moksha).

**Translation:**
In you, in me, and pervading everywhere, there is only one Supreme Lord (Vishnu). Why do you become intolerant and angry with me or others in vain? If you wish to swiftly attain the supreme state of liberation (Vishnutva), you must cultivate an equal mind and see the same Divine in everyone.

---
#### ॥ Verse 25 ॥
शत्रौ मित्रे पुत्रे बन्धौ मा कुरु यत्नं विग्रहसन्धौ ।
सर्वस्मिन्नपि पश्यात्मानं सर्वत्रोत्सृज भेदाज्ञानम् ॥ २५॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Śatrau mitre putre bandhau** = Towards friend, enemy, son, or relative
* **Mā kuru yatnaṃ vigrahasandhau** = Do not strive to create strife/conflict (vigraha) or unnatural attachment (sandhi)
* **Sarvasminnapi paśyātmānaṃ** = See your own Self (Atman) in everything and everyone
* **Sarvatrotṣṛja bhedājñānam** = And discard everywhere the ignorance that creates a sense of separation/duality.

**Translation:**
Do not waste your energy in building unnatural attachments or fighting vain conflicts with your enemies, friends, sons, or relatives. See your own Atman (Self) in all beings, and completely discard the ignorance that makes you perceive differences and duality.

---
#### ॥ Verse 26 ॥
कामं क्रोधं लोभं मोहं त्यक्त्वात्मानं भावय कोऽहम् ।
आत्मज्ञानविहीना मूढास्ते पच्यन्ते नरकनिगूढाः ॥ २६॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Kāmaṃ krodhaṃ lobhaṃ mohaṃ** = Lust, anger, greed, and delusion
* **Tyaktvātmānaṃ bhāvaya ko'ham** = Having abandoned these, reflect upon your true Self, asking "Who am I?"
* **Ātmajñānavihīnā mūḍhās-** = Those fools who are devoid of Self-knowledge
* **Te pacyante narakanigūḍhāḥ** = They fall into deep, hidden hells and are tormented there.

**Translation:**
Cast away lust, anger, greed, and delusion. Reflect deeply on your true nature and ask yourself, "Who am I?" Those foolish individuals who lack Self-knowledge remain trapped in the cycle of birth and death, suffering endlessly in the deep hells of material existence.

---
#### ॥ Verse 27 ॥
गेयं गीतानामसहस्रं ध्येयं श्रीपतिरूपमजस्रम् ।
नेयं सज्जनसङ्गे चित्तं देयं दीनजनाय च वित्तम् ॥ २७॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Geyaṃ gītānāmasahasraṃ** = One should sing/chant the Bhagavad Gita and the Thousand Names (Vishnu Sahasranama)
* **Dhyeyaṃ śrīpatirūpamajasram** = One should constantly/uninterruptedly meditate on the form of the Lord of Lakshmi (Vishnu)
* **Neyaṃ sajjanasaṅge cittaṃ** = The mind should be led into the company of the good and wise (Satsang)
* **Deyaṃ dīnajanāya ca vittam** = And wealth should be distributed in charity to the poor and needy.

**Translation:**
Sing the holy verses of the Bhagavad Gita and chant the Vishnu Sahasranama. Constantly meditate upon the divine form of Lord Sri Hari. Steer your mind toward the company of the noble and wise, and distribute your wealth in charity to the poor and distressed.

---
#### ॥ Verse 28 ॥
सुखतः क्रियते रामाभोगः पश्चाद्धन्त शरीरे रोगः ।
यद्यपि लोके मरणं शरणं तदपि न मुञ्चति पापाचरणम् ॥ २८॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Sukhataḥ kriyate rāmābhogaḥ** = Seeking pleasure, man indulges in carnal enjoyments with women
* **Paścāddhanta śarīre rogaḥ** = But alas! Later, his body becomes afflicted with terrible diseases
* **Yadyapi loke maraṇaṃ śaraṇaṃ** = Even though it is certain that death is the ultimate destination in this world
* **Tadapi na muñcati pāpācaraṇam** = Even then, he does not give up his sinful behaviors/conduct.

**Translation:**
Driven by the desire for fleeting pleasure, a person indulges heavily in carnal enjoyments. But alas, as a result, the body eventually succumbs to severe diseases. Despite knowing perfectly well that death is the ultimate destination for everyone, man stubbornly refuses to abandon his sinful ways.

---
#### ॥ Verse 29 ॥
अर्थमनर्थं भावय नित्यं नास्ति ततः सुखलेशः सत्यम् ।
पुत्रादपि धनभाजां भीतिः सर्वत्रैषा विहिता रीतिः ॥ २९॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Arthamanarthaṃ bhāvaya nityaṃ** = Constantly consider wealth (Artha) to be the root of disaster (Anartha)
* **Nāsti tataḥ sukhaleśaḥ satyam** = It is the absolute truth that there is not even a trace of true happiness in it
* **Putrādapi dhanabhājāṃ bhītiḥ** = The wealthy live in fear even from their own sons
* **Sarvatraiṣā vihitā rītiḥ** = This is the established rule/way of the world everywhere.

**Translation:**
Constantly reflect that amassing wealth is the root of all disaster; it is the absolute truth that money yields not even a drop of true inner peace. The rich live in constant paranoia and fear, sometimes even from their own children. This is the established way of the world everywhere.

---
#### ॥ Verse 30 ॥
प्राणायामं प्रत्याहारं नित्यानित्यविवेकविचारम् ।
जाप्यसमेतसमाधिविधानं कुर्ववधानं महदवधानम् ॥ ३०॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Prāṇāyāmaṃ pratyāhāraṃ** = Control of the life-breath (Pranayama) and withdrawal of the senses (Pratyahara)
* **Nityānitya-viveka-vicāram** = Discerning inquiry into what is eternal (truth) and what is temporary (illusion)
* **Jāpyasametasamādhividhānaṃ** = The practice of chanting mantras coupled with deep meditation (Samadhi)
* **Kurvavadhānaṃ mahadavadhānam** = Perform these with immense care, attention, and diligence.

**Translation:**
Practice control of your breath (Pranayama), withdraw your senses from worldly objects (Pratyahara), and constantly exercise your intellect to discriminate between the eternal and the transient. Engage in the chanting of the Lord's name combined with deep meditation, and perform all these practices with extreme care and utmost attention.

---
#### ॥ Verse 31 ॥
गुरुचरणाम्बुजनिर्भरभक्तः संसारादचिराद्भव मुक्तः ।
सेन्द्रियमानसनियमादेवं द्रक्ष्यसि निजहृदयस्थं देवम् ॥ ३१॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Guru-caraṇāmbuja-nirbhara-bhaktaḥ** = Becoming fully devoted and surrendered to the lotus feet of the Guru
* **Saṃsārād-acirādbhava muktaḥ** = May you swiftly be liberated from this cycle of Samsara
* **Sendriyamānasa-niyamād-evaṃ** = Through such strict discipline/control of the senses and the mind
* **Drakṣyasi nijahṛdayasthaṃ devam** = You will behold the Supreme Lord residing right within your own heart.

**Translation:**
By dedicating yourself with unshakeable devotion to the lotus feet of your Guru, may you swiftly break free from this painful wheel of Samsara. By strictly disciplining your wandering mind and senses in this way, you shall witness the Supreme Lord shining radiantly within your very own heart.

---
#### ॥ Shanti Patha ॥
Here ends the Bhaja Govindam (Mohamudgara Stotram) composed by Adi Shankaracharya.
Om Shantih Shantih Shantih ॥

---

### 3. Benefits of Recitation (Phalashruti)

'Bhaja Govindam' is not merely a hymn of praise; it is a profound mirror reflecting the bitter truths of human existence. Regular study and chanting of these verses shatter the false ego born of wealth, youth, power, and lust. It cultivates deep Vairagya (dispassion), clarifying that all worldly relationships are inherently transactional. This stotram grants immense mental peace, motivates the seeker on the path of authentic devotion to Lord Govinda, and ultimately makes one worthy of self-realization and Moksha (liberation).
