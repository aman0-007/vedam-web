## Shri Bhaja Govindam (Mohamudgara)
**Complete Text, Word-by-Word Meaning, Translation & Significance**

---

### 1. Introduction & Spiritual Significance

'Bhaja Govindam' (also known as 'Mohamudgara') is a highly renowned and awakening hymn composed by the founder of Advaita Vedanta, Adi Guru Shankaracharya. 'Mohamudgara' translates to—the hammer (mudgara) that shatters delusion/attachment (moha).

According to legend, while in Kashi (Varanasi), Adi Shankaracharya saw an old scholar intensely memorizing the rules of Sanskrit grammar ('Dukrinkarane') even as he was nearing death. Observing the old man's ignorance and worldly attachment, a teaching emerged from Shankaracharya's heart out of pure compassion, which became famous as the 'Bhaja Govindam' stotram.

The structure of this stotram is as follows:
* **Dvadasamanjarika (Verses 1 to 12):** These initial 12 verses were composed directly by **Adi Guru Shankaracharya** himself.
* **Chaturdashamanjarika (Verses 13 to 26):** Following this, **14 prominent disciples** of Shankaracharya (Padmapada, Totaka, Hastamalaka, Subodha, Vartikakara, etc.) composed one verse each, expressing their sense of dispassion (Vairagya).
* **Pancharatnika (Verses 27 to 31):** Finally, **Adi Guru Shankaracharya** concluded the stotram by offering these 5 final verses of instruction.

* **Composer:** Adi Guru Shankaracharya and his 14 disciples
* **Presiding Deity:** Lord Govinda (Sri Krishna / Supreme Brahman)
* **Purpose (Viniyoga):** For the destruction of worldly delusions, the attainment of dispassion (Vairagya), and the awakening of Self-knowledge (Moksha).

### 2. Sanskrit Verses, Word-by-Word Meaning & English Translation

#### ॥ Dvadasamanjarika (Composed by Adi Shankaracharya) ॥

#### ॥ Verse 1 ॥
भज गोविन्दं भज गोविन्दं गोविन्दं भज मूढमते ।
सम्प्राप्ते सन्निहिते काले न हि न हि रक्षति डुकृञ्करणे ॥ १॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Bhaja Govindaṃ** = Worship Govinda (Govinda, the Supreme Lord who is the knower of the senses)
* **Mūḍhamate** = O foolish mind! (who considers this fleeting world to be true)
* **Samprāpte sannihite kāle** = When the time of death arrives very near
* **Na hi na hi rakṣati** = Will certainly not save you
* **Ḍukṛñkaraṇe** = The grammatical formula 'Dukrin Karane' (mundane scholarly/bookish knowledge).

**Translation:**
O foolish-minded man! Worship Govinda, worship Govinda, chant only the name of Govinda. When the time of death approaches very near, this knowledge of grammar (worldly education and memorized formulas) will absolutely not protect you. Only the name of the Lord can save you.

---
#### ॥ Verse 2 ॥
मूढ जहीहि धनागमतृष्णां कुरु सद्बुद्धिं मनसि वितृष्णाम् ।
यल्लभसे निजकर्मोपात्तं वित्तं तेन विनोदय चित्तम् ॥ २॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Mūḍha jahīhi dhanāgamatṛṣṇāṃ** = O fool (mudha)! Abandon (jahihi) this intense thirst for amassing wealth
* **Kuru sadbuddhiṃ manasi vitṛṣṇām** = Cultivate a pure, righteous intellect (sadbuddhim) in your mind (manasi), completely free from passions (vitrishnam)
* **Yallabhase nijakarmopāttaṃ** = Whatever you obtain easily as a result of your past karma or present honest labor
* **Vittaṃ tena vinodaya cittam** = With that wealth (vittam), keep your mind entertained and content (vinodaya).

**Translation:**
O fool! Abandon this blind race and intense thirst for accumulating wealth. Eradicate all passions from your mind and cultivate a pure, righteous intellect. Be content with whatever honest wealth you obtain naturally as a result of your own karma, and keep your mind happy with that alone.

---
#### ॥ Verse 3 ॥
नारीस्तनभरनाभीदेशं दृष्ट्वा मा गा मोहावेशम् ।
एतन्मांसवसादि विकारं मनसि विचिन्तय वारं वारम् ॥ ३॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Nārīstanabharanābhīdeśaṃ** = The physical beauty of a woman (breasts and navel region)
* **Dṛṣṭvā mā gā mohāveśam** = Having seen (drishtva), do not fall (ma ga) into the frenzy of lust and delusion (mohawesham)
* **Etanmāṃsavasādi vikāraṃ** = This body is merely a modification (vikaram) of flesh, fat, blood, etc.
* **Manasi vicintaya vāraṃ vāram** = Contemplate this truth in your mind (manasi) again and again (varam varam).

**Translation:**
Do not fall into the frenzy of lust and delusion upon seeing the external physical beauty of a woman. Understand this truth deeply that this body is, in reality, nothing but a mere modification/dummy of flesh, fat, blood, and bones; contemplate this reality in your mind again and again so that your delusion is shattered.

---
#### ॥ Verse 4 ॥
नलिनीदलगतजलमतितरलं तद्वज्जीवितमतिशयचपलम् ।
विद्धि व्याध्यभिमानग्रस्तं लोकं शोकहतं च समस्तम् ॥ ४॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Nalinīdalagatajalam ati-taralaṃ** = Just as a drop of water on a lotus leaf (nalinidala) is extremely unstable (ati-taralam)
* **Tadvajjīvitamatiśayacapalam** = In the exact same way (tadvat), this human life is exceedingly fleeting and unstable
* **Viddhi vyādhyabhimānagrastaṃ** = Know this well (viddhi) that this world is afflicted by disease (vyadhi) and ego (abhimana)
* **Lokaṃ śokahataṃ ca samastam** = And this entire (samastam) world is struck by grief (shoka).

**Translation:**
Just as a drop of water resting on a lotus leaf inside a pond is extremely unstable and can fall with a single gust of wind, in the exact same way, this life is exceedingly fleeting. Know this well that this entire world is consumed by disease, ego, and grief; therefore, it is futile to search for happiness in it.

---
#### ॥ Verse 5 ॥
यावद्वित्तोपार्जनसक्तः तावन्निजपरिवारो रक्तः ।
पश्चाज्जीवति जर्जरदेहे वार्तां कोऽपि न पृच्छति गेहे ॥ ५॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Yāvad-vittopārjana-saktaḥ** = As long as a man is capable of earning wealth
* **Tāvan-nijaparivāro raktaḥ** = Only till then, his own family holds affection/attachment (raktah) for him
* **Paścājjīvati jarjaradehe** = Later (paschat), when the body becomes old and decrepit
* **Vārtāṃ ko'pi na pṛcchati gehe** = Then no one in the house (gehe) even asks about his welfare.

**Translation:**
As long as a man earns and is capable of accumulating wealth, only then does his family respect him and show affection. But in old age, when the body stops working and becomes decrepit, no one in that very house even comes to ask about his well-being.

---
#### ॥ Verse 6 ॥
यावत्पवनो निवसति देहे तावत्पृच्छति कुशलं गेहे ।
गतवति वायौ देहापाये भार्या बिभ्यति तस्मिन्काये ॥ ६॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Yāvatpavano nivasati dehe** = As long as the breath/wind (pavana) resides in this body (dehe)
* **Tāvatpṛcchati kuśalaṃ gehe** = Till then, people in the house ask about your welfare
* **Gatavati vāyau dehāpāye** = When the vital breath departs and the body becomes lifeless
* **Bhāryā bibhyati tasminkāye** = Then even his own wife (bharya) is terrified (bibhyati) of that dead body (kaye).

**Translation:**
As long as the breaths are flowing in this body, family members inquire about your welfare and show respect. But the moment the vital breath departs and this body becomes a lifeless corpse, even the wife whom he loved the most becomes afraid to go near that dead body.

---
#### ॥ Verse 7 ॥
बालस्तावत्क्रीडासक्तः तरुणस्तावत्तरुणीसक्तः ।
वृद्धस्तावच्चिन्तासक्तः परे ब्रह्मणि कोऽपि न सक्तः ॥ ७॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Bālastāvat krīḍāsaktaḥ** = In childhood, man is attached to play (krida)
* **Taruṇastāvat taruṇīsaktaḥ** = In youth, he is attached to women (taruni) and lust
* **Vṛddhastāvac cintāsaktaḥ** = In old age, he is drowning in anxieties
* **Pare brahmaṇi ko'pi na saktaḥ** = But no one is attached to (absorbed in) the Supreme Brahman (God).

**Translation:**
Life passes by without one even realizing it—in childhood, man is absorbed in play; in youth, he is entangled in lust and physical attraction; and when old age arrives, he is surrounded by past memories and future anxieties. The irony, however, is that amidst all this, no one sets their mind on devotion to the Supreme Brahman (God).

---
#### ॥ Verse 8 ॥
का ते कान्ता कस्ते पुत्रः संसारोऽयमतीव विचित्रः ।
कस्य त्वं कः कुत आयातः तत्त्वं चिन्तय तदिदं भ्रातः ॥ ८॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Kā te kāntā kaste putraḥ** = Who is your wife? And who is your son?
* **Saṃsāro'yam-atīva vicitraḥ** = This world is exceedingly strange and illusory
* **Kasya tvaṃ kaḥ kuta āyātaḥ** = Whose are you really? Who are you? Where have you come from?
* **Tattvaṃ cintaya tadidaṃ bhrātaḥ** = O brother! Ponder deeply over these absolute truths.

**Translation:**
Consider those whom you claim as your own—who is your wife and who is your son? This worldly illusion is exceedingly mysterious and based purely on selfishness. Who do you truly belong to? Who are you as a soul, and where will you go after death? O brother! Contemplate deeply upon this ultimate truth and the final reality of life.

---
#### ॥ Verse 9 ॥
सत्सङ्गत्वे निस्सङ्गत्वं निस्सङ्गत्वे निर्मोहत्वम् ।
निर्मोहत्वे निश्चलतत्त्वं निश्चलतत्त्वे जीवन्मुक्तिः ॥ ९॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Satsaṅgatve nissaṅgatvaṃ** = Through Satsang (company of the wise) comes detachment/dispassion (nissangatvam)
* **Nissaṅgatve nirmohatvam** = Through detachment comes the destruction of delusion/ignorance (nirmohatvam)
* **Nirmohatve niścalatattvaṃ** = When delusion is destroyed, the mind becomes steady and unwavering (nishchalatattvam)
* **Niścalatattve jīvanmuktiḥ** = And when the mind becomes steady thus, one attains liberation while living (Jivanmukti).

**Translation:**
When a person engages in Satsang (the company of the wise and truthful), detachment from worldly objects arises within him. Detachment destroys ignorance and delusion. When delusion is eradicated, the mind becomes steadfastly anchored in the Divine, and in this unwavering state of mind, one attains liberation (Moksha) even while living.

---
#### ॥ Verse 10 ॥
वयसि गते कः कामविकारः शुष्के नीरे कः कासारः ।
क्षीणे वित्ते कः परिवारो ज्ञाते तत्त्वे कः संसारः ॥ १०॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Vayasi gate kaḥ kāmavikāraḥ** = When youth has passed, where is the lustful passion?
* **Śuṣke nīre kaḥ kāsāraḥ** = When the water dries up, where is the lake (kasara)?
* **Kṣīṇe vitte kaḥ parivāro** = When wealth is depleted, where is the family? (i.e., selfishness ends)
* **Jñāte tattve kaḥ saṃsāraḥ** = And when the Supreme Truth (Brahma-Jnana) is realized, where is the world?

**Translation:**
When youth fades away, what justification remains for lustful passions? When the water itself dries up, what is the meaning of a lake? When wealth is exhausted, what becomes of the selfish attachment of the family? Exactly in the same way, when the Ultimate Truth (Self-knowledge) is realized, what existence remains of this worldly bondage and illusion?

---
#### ॥ Verse 11 ॥
मा कुरु धनजनयौवनगर्वं हरति निमेषात्कालः सर्वम् ।
मायामयमिदमखिलं हित्वा ब्रह्मपदं त्वं प्रविश विदित्वा ॥ ११॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Mā kuru dhana-jana-yauvana-garvaṃ** = Do not be arrogant about your wealth, followers/power, and youth
* **Harati nimeṣātkālaḥ sarvam** = Time (Death) snatches all these away in the blink of an eye
* **Māyāmayam-idamakhiḷaṃ hitvā** = Having mentally abandoned this entire illusory (unreal) world
* **Brahmapadaṃ tvaṃ praviśa viditvā** = Realizing the Truth, enter the state of Brahman (Moksha).

**Translation:**
Do not hold vain arrogance regarding your vast wealth, power, family, and youth, because Time destroys all of them in a mere fraction of a second. Knowing this entire world to be illusory, mentally abandon your attachment to it, recognize the Ultimate Truth, and attain the state of the Supreme Brahman.

---
#### ॥ Verse 12 ॥
दिनयामिन्यौ सायं प्रातः शिशिरवसन्तौ पुनरायातः ।
कालः क्रीडति गच्छत्यायुस्तदपि न मुञ्चत्याशावायुः ॥ १२॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Dina-yāminyau sāyaṃ prātaḥ** = Day and night (yamini), evening and morning
* **Śiśira-vasantau punarāyātaḥ** = Winter and spring seasons come and go repeatedly
* **Kālaḥ krīḍati gacchatyāyuḥ** = Time is playing, and life/age is continuously passing away
* **Tadapi na muñcatyāśāvāyuḥ** = Even then, the storm of desires/hopes does not leave a person.

**Translation:**
Day and night, morning and evening, winter and spring come and go repeatedly like an endless cycle. Time is playing its game with us, and our lifespan is constantly decreasing; yet, the storm of endless desires and hopes never settles, and man remains trapped in illusion.

---

#### ॥ Chaturdashamanjarika (Composed by 14 Disciples) ॥

#### ॥ Verse 13 (Padmapadacharya) ॥
का ते कान्ता धनगतचिन्ता वातुल किं तव नास्ति नियन्ता ।
त्रिजगति सज्जनसङ्गतिरेका भवति भवार्णवतरणे नौका ॥ १३॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Kā te kāntā dhanagatacintā** = Who is your wife? Why this obsession with wealth?
* **Vātula kiṃ tava nāsti niyantā** = O distracted/lost one (vatula)! Is there no Supreme Controller for you?
* **Trijagati sajjanasaṅgatirekā** = In all the three worlds, the company of the wise alone
* **Bhavati bhavārṇavataraṇe naukā** = Is the boat to cross over this ocean of worldly existence.

**Translation (Instruction by Padmapadacharya):**
Who is your wife? Why are you drowning in the vain obsession of wealth? O misguided man! Is there no God guiding you? Remember, in all the three worlds, only the association of the wise and pure-hearted (Satsang) acts as the sole boat that can ferry you across this terrifying ocean of worldly existence.

---
#### ॥ Verse 14 (Totakacharya) ॥
जटिलो मुण्डी लुञ्छितकेशः काषायाम्बरबहुकृतवेषः ।
पश्यन्नपि च न पश्यति मूढो उदरनिमित्तं बहुकृतवेषः ॥ १४॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Jaṭilo muṇḍī luñchitakeśaḥ** = One with matted locks, one with a shaven head, one who plucks out his hair
* **Kāṣāyāmbara-bahukṛtaveṣaḥ** = One who wears ochre/saffron robes in various disguises
* **Paśyannapi ca na paśyati mūḍho** = This fool, even while seeing the truth of death, fails to see it
* **Udaranimittaṃ bahukṛtaveṣaḥ** = All these various disguises are merely for the sake of filling the belly.

**Translation (Instruction by Totakacharya):**
Some ascetics grow matted locks, some shave their heads, some pluck out their hair, and some don saffron robes. This foolish creature, despite seeing the ultimate truth of life, ignores it. Without genuine dispassion, all these external garbs and hypocrisies are created merely for the sake of filling one's stomach.

---
#### ॥ Verse 15 (Hastamalakacharya) ॥
अङ्गं गलितं पलितं मुण्डं दशनविहीनं जातं तुण्डम् ।
वृद्धो याति गृहीत्वा दण्डं तदपि न मुञ्चत्याशापिण्डम् ॥ १५॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Aṅgaṃ galitaṃ palitaṃ muṇḍaṃ** = The limbs have become decrepit, the hair on the head has turned white
* **Daśanavihīnaṃ jātaṃ tuṇḍam** = The mouth has become toothless
* **Vṛddho yāti gṛhītvā daṇḍaṃ** = The old man walks leaning heavily on his walking stick
* **Tadapi na muñcatyāśāpiṇḍam** = Even then, he does not let go of the bundle of his desires.

**Translation (Instruction by Hastamalakacharya):**
The body's limbs have loosened, the hair on the head has turned completely white, not a single tooth remains in the mouth, and the old man is forced to walk leaning on a stick; but look at the irony, even after becoming so decrepit, he is unable to let go of the heavy burden of his desires and hopes.

---
#### ॥ Verse 16 (Subodhacharya) ॥
अग्रे वह्निः पृष्ठे भानुः रात्रौ चुबुकसमर्पितजानुः ।
करतलभिक्षस्तरुतलवासस्तदपि न मुञ्चत्याशापाशः ॥ १६॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Agre vahniḥ pṛṣṭhe bhānuḥ** = Fire in front and the sun's heat at the back (to escape the cold)
* **Rātrau cubukasamarpitajānuḥ** = At night, sitting curled up with his chin tucked into his knees
* **Karatalabhikṣas-tarutalavāsas-** = Eating alms from his bare palms, sleeping under the trees
* **Tadapi na muñcatyāśāpāśaḥ** = Even then, the noose/trap of desires does not leave him.

**Translation (Instruction by Subodhacharya):**
To escape the cold, he warms himself with a fire in front and takes the sun's heat on his back; at night, he shivers and sits with his chin tucked into his knees. He begs for food in his hands and sleeps beneath trees; yet, even in such a destitute and ascetic state, he cannot break free from the binding noose of worldly desires (Maya).

---
#### ॥ Verse 17 (Vartikakaracharya - Sureshwaracharya) ॥
कुरुते गङ्गासागरगमनं व्रतपरिपालनमथवा दानम् ।
ज्ञानविहीनः सर्वमतेन मुक्तिं न भजति जन्मशतेन ॥ १७॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Kurute gaṅgāsāgaragamanaṃ** = One may undertake a pilgrimage to Gangasagar
* **Vrataparipālanamathavā dānam** = Or strictly observe fasts, or give away heavy charity
* **Jñānavihīnaḥ sarvamatena** = But according to all scriptures/scholars, a person devoid of Self-knowledge
* **Muktiṃ na bhajati janmaśatena** = Cannot attain liberation even after a hundred lifetimes.

**Translation (Instruction by Sureshwaracharya):**
A person may undertake a holy pilgrimage to Gangasagar, rigorously observe strict vows, or donate immense wealth; but it is the unanimous verdict of all scriptures and scholars that without true 'Self-knowledge' (Brahma-Jnana), one cannot attain liberation (Moksha) even after taking a hundred births.

---
#### ॥ Verse 18 (Nityanandacharya) ॥
सुरमन्दिरतरुमूलनिवासः शय्या भूतलमिजिनं वासः ।
सर्वपरिग्रहभोगत्यागः कस्य सुखं न करोति विरागः ॥ १८॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Suramandira-tarumūla-nivāsaḥ** = Residing in temples or at the roots of trees
* **Śayyā bhūtalam-ijinaṃ vāsaḥ** = Having the earth as a bed and wearing deer skin as clothing
* **Sarvaparigraha-bhogatyāgaḥ** = Who has renounced all possessions and worldly enjoyments
* **Kasya sukhaṃ na karoti virāgaḥ** = To whom will such dispassion not bring happiness?

**Translation (Instruction by Nityanandacharya):**
Living in temples or beneath trees, sleeping on the bare earth, wearing simple deer skin (or plain clothes), and completely renouncing all types of worldly possessions and sensual enjoyments—whom will such pure and unblemished dispassion not bless with supreme happiness and peace? (Meaning, true happiness lies only in Vairagya).

---
#### ॥ Verse 19 (Anandagiryacharya) ॥
योगरतो वा भोगरतो वा सङ्गरतो वा सङ्गविहीनः ।
यस्य ब्रह्मणि रमते चित्तं नन्दति नन्दति नन्दत्येव ॥ १९॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Yogarato vā bhogarato vā** = Whether one is engaged in Yoga or in worldly enjoyments
* **Saṅgarato vā saṅgavihīnaḥ** = Whether one lives in society (company) or in absolute solitude
* **Yasya brahmaṇi ramate cittaṃ** = He whose mind constantly revels solely in the Supreme Brahman
* **Nandati nandati nandatyeva** = He alone rejoices, he alone rejoices, he alone truly rejoices!

**Translation (Instruction by Anandagiryacharya):**
Whether a person is absorbed in ascetic Yoga practices or engaged in worldly enjoyments, whether he lives amidst crowds or in complete solitude; it does not matter. The person whose mind is deeply absorbed in the Supreme Brahman (God), only he attains supreme bliss; only he finds true joy.

---
#### ॥ Verse 20 (Dridhabhaktacharya) ॥
भगवद्गीता किञ्चिदधीता गङ्गाजललवकणिका पीता ।
सकृदपि येन मुरारिसमर्चा क्रियते तस्य यमेन न चर्चा ॥ २०॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Bhagavadgītā kiñcidadhītā** = He who has studied the Bhagavad Gita even a little
* **Gaṅgājala-lavakaṇikā pītā** = He who has drunk even a tiny drop of Ganga water with devotion
* **Sakṛdapi yena murārisamarcā** = He who has genuinely worshipped Lord Murari (Krishna) even once in life
* **Kriyate tasya yamena na carcā** = Yama (the Lord of Death) has no discussion/inquiry with him.

**Translation (Instruction by Dridhabhaktacharya):**
The person who has studied the Bhagavad Gita even a little bit, who has reverently consumed even a single drop of sacred Ganga water, and who has worshipped Lord Krishna (Murari) with a true heart even once in his life; at the time of death, Yamaraja does not question him (he is freed from the fear of the messengers of death).

---
#### ॥ Verse 21 (Nityanathacharya) ॥
पुनरपि जननं पुनरपि मरणं पुनरपि जननीजठरे शयनम् ।
इह संसारे बहुदुस्तारे कृपयाऽपारे पाहि मुरारे ॥ २१॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Punarapi jananaṃ punarapi maraṇaṃ** = Born again, dead again
* **Punarapi jananījaṭhare śayanam** = And repeatedly sleeping (suffering) in the mother's womb
* **Iha saṃsāre bahudustāre** = In this world, which is extremely difficult to cross
* **Kṛpayā'pāre pāhi murāre** = O Lord Murari! Out of Your boundless compassion, please protect me.

**Translation (Instruction by Nityanathacharya):**
Taking birth again and again, dying again and again, and repeatedly entering the mother's womb to suffer—this is the endless cycle of life. O Lord Murari (Krishna)! This ocean of the world is extremely difficult to cross; please, out of Your boundless compassion, protect me and liberate me from this cycle of birth and death.

---
#### ॥ Verse 22 (Yoganandacharya) ॥
रथ्याचर्पटविरचितकन्थः पुण्यापुण्यविवर्जितपन्थः ।
योगी योगनियोजितचित्तो रमते बालोन्मत्तवदेव ॥ २२॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Rathyācarpaṭa-viracitakanthaḥ** = One who wears a garment made from rags cast off on the streets
* **Puṇyāpuṇya-vivarjitapanthaḥ** = Who is free from the dual paths of merit (punya) and sin (apunya)
* **Yogī yoganiyojitacitto** = That Yogi, whose mind is perfectly fixed in Yoga (the Supreme)
* **Ramate bālonmattavadeva** = Revels in bliss just like a child or a madman.

**Translation (Instruction by Yoganandacharya):**
Wearing clothes pieced together from torn rags found on the streets, and having transcended the dualities of merit and sin; such a Yogi, whose mind is completely absorbed in the Divine, roams the world indifferent to societal norms, reveling in his own supreme bliss like an innocent child or a madman.

---
#### ॥ Verse 23 (Surendranathacharya) ॥
कस्त्वं कोऽहं कुत आयातः का मे जननी को मे तातः ।
इति परिभावय सर्वमसारं विश्वं त्यक्त्वा स्वप्नविचारम् ॥ २३॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Kastvaṃ ko'haṃ kuta āyātaḥ** = Who are you? Who am I? From where have we come?
* **Kā me jananī ko me tātaḥ** = Who is my mother? Who is my father?
* **Iti paribhāvaya sarvamasāraṃ** = Ponder thus, and realize that all this is essence-less
* **Viśvaṃ tyaktvā svapnavicāram** = And abandon the attachment to this world, considering it merely a dream.

**Translation (Instruction by Surendranathacharya):**
Who are you? Who am I? Where have we all come from? Who is my mother and who is my father? Contemplate deeply upon this and realize that all this worldly existence is hollow and meaningless. Treat this entire universe merely as a dream and abandon your attachment to it.

---
#### ॥ Verse 24 (Medhatithyacharya) ॥
त्वयि मयि चान्यत्रैको विष्णुर्व्यर्थं कुप्यसि मय्यसहिष्णुः ।
भव समचित्तः सर्वत्र त्वं वाञ्छस्यचिराद्यदि विष्णुत्वम् ॥ २४॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Tvayi mayi cānyatraiko viṣṇur-** = In you, in me, and everywhere else, there is only one Vishnu (Supreme)
* **Vyarthaṃ kupyasi mayyasahiṣṇuḥ** = You become intolerant and get angry with me completely in vain
* **Bhava samacittaḥ sarvatra tvaṃ** = Maintain an equal, balanced vision towards everyone
* **Vāñchasyacirādyadi viṣṇutvam** = If you desire to quickly attain 'Vishnutva' (the state of God/Moksha).

**Translation (Instruction by Medhatithyacharya):**
The same Supreme Lord (Vishnu) resides within you, within me, and throughout this entire creation. Then why do you become intolerant and express anger towards me or others in vain? If you wish to swiftly attain liberation (Vishnutva), you must learn to maintain an equal vision and equanimity towards everyone.

---
#### ॥ Verse 25 (Bharativamshacharya) ॥
शत्रौ मित्रे पुत्रे बन्धौ मा कुरु यत्नं विग्रहसन्धौ ।
सर्वस्मिन्नपि पश्यात्मानं सर्वत्रोत्सृज भेदाज्ञानम् ॥ २५॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Śatrau mitre putre bandhau** = Towards an enemy, friend, son, or relative
* **Mā kuru yatnaṃ vigrahasandhau** = Do not make vain efforts for conflict (vigraha) or attachment (sandhi)
* **Sarvasminnapi paśyātmānaṃ** = See only your own Self in all beings
* **Sarvatrotṣṛja bhedājñānam** = And abandon the ignorance of perceiving differences everywhere.

**Translation (Instruction by Bharativamshacharya):**
Do not waste your efforts in vain disputes or excessive attachments with enemies, friends, sons, or relatives. Behold your own Soul (Atman) within all living beings, and completely discard the ignorance of the 'I' and 'You' duality from everywhere.

---
#### ॥ Verse 26 (Sumatindranathacharya) ॥
कामं क्रोधं लोभं मोहं त्यक्त्वात्मानं भावय कोऽहम् ।
आत्मज्ञानविहीना मूढास्ते पच्यन्ते नरकनिगूढाः ॥ २६॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Kāmaṃ krodhaṃ lobhaṃ mohaṃ** = Lust, anger, greed, and delusion
* **Tyaktvātmānaṃ bhāvaya ko'ham** = Abandoning these, reflect upon your Soul, asking "Who am I?"
* **Ātmajñānavihīnā mūḍhās-** = Those fools who are devoid of Self-knowledge
* **Te pacyante narakanigūḍhāḥ** = They fall into deep hells and are tormented there.

**Translation (Instruction by Sumatindranathacharya):**
Abandon the four great enemies—lust, anger, greed, and delusion—and reflect within yourself, "Who am I truly?" Those foolish individuals who lack Self-knowledge remain trapped in the cycle of birth and death, suffering endlessly in the terrible agonies of hell.

---

#### ॥ Pancharatnika (Shankaracharya's Final Instruction) ॥

#### ॥ Verse 27 ॥
गेयं गीतानामसहस्रं ध्येयं श्रीपतिरूपमजस्रम् ।
नेयं सज्जनसङ्गे चित्तं देयं दीनजनाय च वित्तम् ॥ २७॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Geyaṃ gītānāmasahasraṃ** = One should sing/recite the Bhagavad Gita and the Vishnu Sahasranama
* **Dhyeyaṃ śrīpatirūpamajasram** = One should constantly meditate upon the form of Lord Shri Hari (Vishnu)
* **Neyaṃ sajjanasaṅge cittaṃ** = One should direct their mind towards the company of the good (Satsang)
* **Deyaṃ dīnajanāya ca vittam** = And one should donate their wealth to the poor and needy.

**Translation:**
A person should regularly recite the Bhagavad Gita and the Vishnu Sahasranama. One should constantly meditate upon the supreme form of Lord Sri Hari (Vishnu). One must steer their mind towards Satsang (the company of noble people) and donate a portion of their wealth to serve the poor and the distressed.

---
#### ॥ Verse 28 ॥
सुखतः क्रियते रामाभोगः पश्चाद्धन्त शरीरे रोगः ।
यद्यपि लोके मरणं शरणं तदपि न मुञ्चति पापाचरणम् ॥ २८॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Sukhataḥ kriyate rāmābhogaḥ** = Seeking pleasure, man indulges in carnal enjoyments
* **Paścāddhanta śarīre rogaḥ** = But alas! Later, his body becomes afflicted with terrible diseases
* **Yadyapi loke maraṇaṃ śaraṇaṃ** = Even though it is certain that death is the ultimate destination in this world
* **Tadapi na muñcati pāpācaraṇam** = Even then, he does not give up his sinful conduct.

**Translation:**
For the sake of momentary pleasure, a person indulges deeply in lust and worldly enjoyments, but as a consequence, terrible diseases arise in the body later on. Despite knowing perfectly well that death is inevitable for everyone at the end, man refuses to abandon his sinful behaviors.

---
#### ॥ Verse 29 ॥
अर्थमनर्थं भावय नित्यं नास्ति ततः सुखलेशः सत्यम् ।
पुत्रादपि धनभाजां भीतिः सर्वत्रैषा विहिता रीतिः ॥ २९॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Arthamanarthaṃ bhāvaya nityaṃ** = Constantly consider wealth (Artha) as the root of disaster (Anartha)
* **Nāsti tataḥ sukhaleśaḥ satyam** = It is the absolute truth that it does not yield even a fraction of true happiness
* **Putrādapi dhanabhājāṃ bhītiḥ** = The wealthy live in fear even from their own sons
* **Sarvatraiṣā vihitā rītiḥ** = This is the established rule/way of the world everywhere.

**Translation:**
Always consider wealth and property to be the root cause of disaster; it is the ultimate truth that immense wealth does not provide even an ounce of true peace or happiness. The truth is that wealthy people harbor fear for their lives even from their own sons; this is the prevalent way of the world everywhere.

---
#### ॥ Verse 30 ॥
प्राणायामं प्रत्याहारं नित्यानित्यविवेकविचारम् ।
जाप्यसमेतसमाधिविधानं कुर्ववधानं महदवधानम् ॥ ३०॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Prāṇāyāmaṃ pratyāhāraṃ** = Control of breath (Pranayama) and withdrawing senses from objects (Pratyahara)
* **Nityānitya-viveka-vicāram** = Discerning inquiry between the eternal (truth) and the transient (unreal)
* **Jāpyasametasamādhividhānaṃ** = The practice of entering Samadhi (deep meditation) along with chanting the Lord's name
* **Kurvavadhānaṃ mahadavadhānam** = Perform these practices with extreme care and absolute attention.

**Translation:**
Practice Pranayama (breath control), Pratyahara (withdrawing the senses from worldly objects), and constantly exercise discernment to distinguish between what is eternal and what is transient. Engage in deep meditation (Samadhi) while continuously chanting the name of the Lord, and practice all this with extreme care and complete focus.

---
#### ॥ Verse 31 ॥
गुरुचरणाम्बुजनिर्भरभक्तः संसारादचिराद्भव मुक्तः ।
सेन्द्रियमानसनियमादेवं द्रक्ष्यसि निजहृदयस्थं देवम् ॥ ३१॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Guru-caraṇāmbuja-nirbhara-bhaktaḥ** = Becoming fully and exclusively devoted to the lotus feet of the Guru
* **Saṃsārād-acirādbhava muktaḥ** = May you swiftly be liberated from the bondage of this Samsara
* **Sendriyamānasa-niyamād-evaṃ** = Only by disciplining the senses and the mind in this way
* **Drakṣyasi nijahṛdayasthaṃ devam** = Will you be able to behold the Supreme Lord residing in your own heart.

**Translation:**
Become completely and exclusively devoted to the lotus feet of your true Guru; through this alone, you will swiftly be liberated from this ocean of worldly existence (the cycle of birth and death). Discipline your mind and your senses; by doing so, you shall directly witness the Supreme Lord residing within your very own heart.

---
#### ॥ Shanti Patha ॥
Here ends the Bhaja Govindam (Mohamudgara Stotram) composed by Adi Shankaracharya.
Om Shantih Shantih Shantih ॥

---

### 3. Benefits of Recitation (Phalashruti)

'Bhaja Govindam' is not merely a stotram; it is a profound mirror reflecting the bitter truths of human existence. Regular study of this text destroys the false ego born out of wealth, youth, lust, and status. This hymn cultivates deep dispassion (Vairagya) in the heart and clarifies that all worldly relationships are based on selfishness. Its recitation grants the seeker mental peace, motivates them onto the path of true devotion to Lord Govinda, and ultimately makes them worthy of attaining Moksha (liberation).
