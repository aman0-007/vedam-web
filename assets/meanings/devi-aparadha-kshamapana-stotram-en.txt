## ॥ Devyaparadhakshamapana Stotram ॥
**Complete Text, Word-by-Word Meaning, Translation & Significance**

---

### 1. Introduction & Spiritual Significance

The 'Devyaparadhakshamapana Stotram' is an intensely moving and deeply devotional hymn composed by Adi Guru Shankaracharya, dedicated to the Supreme Mother of the Universe, Goddess Bhavani (Durga). When a spiritual seeker realizes their ignorance regarding the complex rules of rituals, mantras, and worship, and acknowledges the countless mistakes (aparadha) committed in life, they completely surrender to the Mother through this beautiful prayer.

The core philosophy of this stotram is encapsulated in its world-renowned refrain: **"Kuputro jayeta kvachidapi kumata na bhavati"** (A son may turn out to be a bad son, but a mother can never be a bad mother). It is highly recommended to chant this hymn at the conclusion of any puja or ritual to seek forgiveness for all known and unknown errors.

* **Composer:** Adi Guru Shankaracharya
* **Presiding Deity:** Jagadamba (Goddess Bhavani / Parvati / Durga)
* **Purpose (Viniyoga):** To seek forgiveness for errors committed during worship, to attain the uncaused grace of the Divine Mother, and to find absolute refuge in Her during times of crisis.

---

### 2. Sanskrit Verses, Word-by-Word Meaning & English Translation

#### ॥ Verse 1 ॥
न मन्त्रं नो यन्त्रं तदपि च न जाने स्तुतिमहो
न चाह्वानं ध्यानं तदपि च न जाने स्तुतिकथाः ।
न जाने मुद्रास्ते तदपि च न जाने विलपनं
परं जाने मातस्त्वदनुसरणं क्लेशहरणम् ॥ १॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Na mantraṃ no yantraṃ** = Neither do I know the Mantras (sacred chants), nor the Yantras (mystical diagrams)
* **Tadapi ca na jāne stutimaho** = And alas! I do not even know how to sing Your praises (Stuti)
* **Na cāhvānaṃ dhyānaṃ** = Neither do I know how to invoke You (Ahvana), nor how to meditate upon You (Dhyana)
* **Tadapi ca na jāne stutikathāḥ** = And I do not even know the stories of Your glory
* **Na jāne mudrāste** = I do not know Your sacred hand-gestures (Mudras of worship)
* **Tadapi ca na jāne vilapanaṃ** = And I do not even know how to wail/plead before You
* **Paraṃ jāne mātastvadanusaraṇaṃ kleśaharaṇam** = But I know only this, O Mother: that merely following/surrendering to You takes away all afflictions (Klesha).

**Translation:**
O Mother! I do not know Your mantras, nor Your yantras, nor do I know how to sing Your praises. I do not know how to invoke You, how to meditate upon You, or how to recite the stories of Your glory. I do not know the sacred mudras of worship, nor do I even know how to properly cry and plead before You. But I know only one thing, O Mother: that taking refuge in You destroys all the sorrows and afflictions of life.

---

#### ॥ Verse 2 ॥
विधेरज्ञानेन द्रविणविरहेणालसतया
विधेयाशक्यत्वात्तव चरणयोर्या च्युतिरभूत् ।
तदेतत् क्षन्तव्यं जननि सकलोद्धारिणि शिवे
कुपुत्रो जायेत क्वचिदपि कुमाता न भवति ॥ २॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Vidherajñānena** = Due to my ignorance of the prescribed rules of worship
* **Draviṇaviraheṇālasatayā** = Due to the lack of wealth (Dravina), or due to sheer laziness
* **Vidheyāśakyatvāt-** = Or due to my physical inability to perform the required duties
* **Tava caraṇayoryā cyutirabhūt** = Whatever lapses/failings have occurred in the service of Your lotus feet
* **Tadetat kṣantavyaṃ janani** = All that must be forgiven, O Mother!
* **Sakaloddhāriṇi śive** = O Savior of all, O Auspicious One (Shivaa)!
* **Kuputro jāyeta kvacidapi kumātā na bhavati** = Because a bad son may be born somewhere, but a bad mother can never exist.

**Translation:**
O Mother, Savior of all, O Auspicious One! Whether due to my ignorance of the rituals, lack of wealth, pure laziness, or sheer physical inability, whatever mistakes and omissions have occurred in serving Your lotus feet—please forgive them all. Because in this world, a son may turn out to be wicked, but a mother can never be a bad mother.

---

#### ॥ Verse 3 ॥
पृथिव्यां पुत्रास्ते जननि बहवः सन्ति सरलाः
परं तेषां मध्ये विरलतरलोऽहं तव सुतः ।
मदीयोऽयं त्यागः समुचितमिदं नो तव शिवे
कुपुत्रो जायेत क्वचिदपि कुमाता न भवति ॥ ३॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Pṛthivyāṃ putrāste janani** = On this earth, You have many sons, O Mother
* **Bahavaḥ santi saralāḥ** = Who are very simple, sincere, and good
* **Paraṃ teṣāṃ madhye** = But among all of them
* **Viralataralo'haṃ tava sutaḥ** = I am Your rare, extremely fickle and wayward son
* **Madīyo'yaṃ tyāgaḥ** = Abandoning me like this
* **Samucitamidaṃ no tava śive** = Is not proper/fitting for You, O Shivaa!
* **Kuputro jāyeta...** = A bad son may be born, but there is never a bad mother.

**Translation:**
O Mother! On this earth, You have countless sons who are sincere, simple, and devoted. But among all of them, I am a rare, highly fickle, and wayward son. Despite this, O Shivaa, it is not proper for You to abandon me; because a son may turn out to be wicked, but a mother can never be a bad mother.

---

#### ॥ Verse 4 ॥
जगन्मातर्मातस्तव चरणसेवा न रचिता
न वा दत्तं देवि द्रविणमपि भूयस्तव मया ।
तथापि त्वं स्नेहं मयि निरुपमं यत्प्रकुरुषे
कुपुत्रो जायेत क्वचिदपि कुमाता न भवति ॥ ४॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Jaganmātarmātas-** = O Mother of the Universe! O Mother!
* **Tava caraṇasevā na racitā** = I have never performed service at Your feet
* **Na vā dattaṃ devi draviṇamapi bhūyastava mayā** = Nor have I offered abundant wealth/gifts to You, O Goddess
* **Tathāpi tvaṃ snehaṃ mayi nirupamaṃ yatprakuruṣe** = Yet, the unparalleled affection that You continue to bestow upon me
* **Kuputro jāyeta...** = Is because a bad son may be born, but a mother can never be a bad mother.

**Translation:**
O Mother of the Universe! I have never truly served Your lotus feet, nor have I ever offered You abundant wealth or precious gifts. Yet, You continue to shower Your unparalleled, causeless affection upon me. The only reason for this is that a son may become wicked, but a mother can never be a bad mother.

---

#### ॥ Verse 5 ॥
परित्यक्ता देवा विविधविधसेवाकुलतया
मया पञ्चाशीतेरधिकमपनीते तु वयसि ।
इदानीं चेन्मातस्तव यदि कृपा नापि भविता
निरालम्बो लम्बोदरजननि कं यामि शरणम् ॥ ५॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Parityaktā devā vividhavidhasevākulatayā** = I abandoned other gods, being overwhelmed by the complex varieties of their required worship
* **Mayā pañcāśīteradhikamapanīte tu vayasi** = And now that my age has crossed over eighty-five years (i.e., advanced age)
* **Idānīṃ cenmātastava yadi kṛpā nāpi bhavitā** = If even at this stage, O Mother, Your grace does not fall upon me
* **Nirālambo lambodarajanani kaṃ yāmi śaraṇam** = Completely destitute/helpless, O Mother of Ganesha (Lambodara), whose refuge shall I seek?

**Translation:**
O Mother! Overwhelmed and exhausted by the complex rituals required to serve them, I abandoned the worship of other gods. Now, I have crossed eighty-five years of age (reached the twilight of my life). O Mother of Lord Ganesha! If You do not bestow Your grace upon me even at this helpless stage, then destitute and without support, whose refuge shall I seek?

---

#### ॥ Verse 6 ॥
श्वपाको जल्पाको भवति मधुपाकोपमगिरा
निरातङ्को रङ्को विहरति चिरं कोटिकनकैः ।
तवापर्णे कर्णे विशति मनुवर्णे फलमिदं
जनः को जानीते जननि जपनीयं जपविधौ ॥ ६॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Śvapāko jalpāko bhavati** = An outcaste/dog-eater (shvapaka) becomes a highly eloquent speaker (jalpaka)
* **Madhupākopamagirā** = With a voice as sweet as honey
* **Nirātaṅko raṅko viharati ciraṃ koṭikanakaiḥ** = A destitute beggar (ranka) roams fearlessly for a long time with millions of gold coins
* **Tavāparṇe karṇe viśati manuvarṇe phalamidaṃ** = O Aparna! If even a single syllable of Your mantra just enters the ear, this is the fruit
* **Janaḥ ko jānīte janani japanīyaṃ japavidhau** = O Mother! Who can possibly know the boundless merit gained by the one who actually chants it with proper rituals?

**Translation:**
O Aparna (Goddess Parvati)! If even a single syllable of Your mantra simply falls into one's ears, an outcaste transforms into an eloquent speaker with words as sweet as honey, and a destitute beggar fearlessly enjoys millions of gold coins. O Mother! When merely hearing Your mantra yields such fruits, who can possibly comprehend the infinite reward of the one who formally and devotedly chants it according to the sacred rules?

---

#### ॥ Verse 7 ॥
चिताभस्मालेपो गरलमशनं दिक्पटधरो
जटाधारी कण्ठे भुजगपतिहारी पशुपतिः ।
कपाली भूतेशो भजति जगदीशैकपदवीं
भवानि त्वत्पाणिग्रहणपरिपाटीफलमिदम् ॥ ७॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Citābhasmālepo** = He who smears Himself with the ashes from cremation grounds
* **Garalamaśanaṃ** = Whose food is deadly poison (Halahala)
* **Dikpaṭadharo** = Who wears the directions as His clothes (naked/Digambara)
* **Jaṭādhārī kaṇṭhe bhujagapatihārī paśupatiḥ** = Who wears matted hair, a garland of the king of serpents around His neck, and is the Lord of Animals (Pashupati)
* **Kapālī bhūteśo** = Who carries a skull (Kapali) and is the Lord of ghosts/spirits (Bhutesha)
* **Bhajati jagadīśaikapadavīṃ** = Enjoys the singular, supreme title of 'Jagadisha' (Lord of the Universe)
* **Bhavāni tvatpāṇigrahaṇaparipāṭīphalamidam** = O Bhavani! This is entirely the fruit of the event of taking Your hand in marriage.

**Translation:**
O Bhavani! He who smears Himself with cremation ashes, who consumes deadly poison, who remains naked, wears matted hair and a garland of serpents, who holds a skull in His hand and is the Lord of ghosts—that Lord Pashupati (Shiva) enjoys the singular, supreme title of 'Jagadisha' (The Lord of the Universe). In truth, this glorious status is merely the fruit of having taken Your hand in marriage!

---

#### ॥ Verse 8 ॥
न मोक्षस्याकाङ्क्षा भवविभववाञ्छापि च न मे
न विज्ञानापेक्षा शशिमुखि सुखेच्छापि न पुनः ।
अतस्त्वां संयाचे जननि जननं यातु मम वै
मृडानी रुद्राणी शिव शिव भवानीति जपतः ॥ ८॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Na mokṣasyākāṅkṣā** = I have no desire for liberation (Moksha)
* **Bhavavibhavavāñchāpi ca na me** = Nor do I have any craving for worldly wealth/glory (bhava-vibhava)
* **Na vijñānāpekṣā** = I do not seek any special sciences or knowledge
* **Śaśimukhi sukhecchāpi na punaḥ** = And O moon-faced Mother! I do not even desire happiness
* **Atastvāṃ saṃyāce janani** = Therefore, O Mother, I beg only this of You
* **Jananaṃ yātu mama vai** = May my life/time strictly pass
* **Mṛḍānī rudrāṇī śiva śiva bhavānīti japataḥ** = While continuously chanting: "Mridani, Rudrani, Shiva, Shiva, Bhavani."

**Translation:**
O Moon-faced Mother! I harbor no desire for liberation (Moksha), nor do I crave any worldly wealth or glory. I seek no specialized knowledge, nor do I even desire personal happiness. O Mother! I beg only this from You: May my entire life pass simply chanting Your holy names—"O Mridani, O Rudrani, O Shivaa, O Bhavani."

---

#### ॥ Verse 9 ॥
नाराधितासि विधिना विविधोपचारैः
किं रुक्षचिन्तनपरैर्न कृतं वचोभिः ।
श्यामे त्वमेव यदि किञ्चन मय्यनाथे
धत्से कृपामुचितमम्ब परं तवैव ॥ ९॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Nārādhitāsi vidhinā vividhopacāraiḥ** = I have never worshipped You according to the rules with various offerings
* **Kiṃ rukṣacintanaparairna kṛtaṃ vacobhiḥ** = What offenses have I not committed through my harsh thoughts and cruel words!
* **Śyāme tvameva yadi kiñcana mayyanāthe** = O Shyama! Even so, if You Yourself show even a little
* **Dhatse kṛpāmucitamamba paraṃ tavaiva** = Mercy/grace towards this orphan, O Mother, it is only befitting of Your supreme greatness.

**Translation:**
O Mother! I have never worshipped You properly with the prescribed rituals or diverse offerings. On the contrary, what sins have I not committed through my harsh thoughts and cruel words! Yet, O Shyama (Dark-complexioned Goddess), if You still bestow even a fraction of Your grace upon this helpless orphan, it is only befitting of Your supreme, boundless compassion.

---

#### ॥ Verse 10 ॥
आपत्सु मग्नः स्मरणं त्वदीयं
करोमि दुर्गे करुणार्णवेशि ।
नैतच्छठत्वं मम भावयेथाः
क्षुधातृषार्ता जननीं स्मरन्ति ॥ १०॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Āpatsu magnaḥ smaraṇaṃ tvadīyaṃ** = Submerged in calamities, Your remembrance
* **Karomi durge karuṇārṇaveśi** = I do, O Durga, O Supreme Goddess of the ocean of compassion!
* **Naitacchaṭhatvaṃ mama bhāvayethāḥ** = Please do not consider this as my deceitfulness or selfishness
* **Kṣudhātṛṣārtā jananīṃ smaranti** = For children tormented by hunger and thirst naturally remember their mother.

**Translation:**
O Durga! O Sovereign of the ocean of compassion! I only remember You when I am deeply submerged in calamities and distress. Please do not view this as my selfishness or deceit. It is but natural, for when children are tormented by agonizing hunger and thirst, they instinctively cry out for their mother.

---

#### ॥ Verse 11 ॥
जगदम्ब विचित्रमत्र किं
परिपूर्णा करुणास्ति चेन्मयि ।
अपराधपरम्परापरं
न हि माता समुपेक्षते सुतम् ॥ ११॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Jagadamba vicitramatra kiṃ** = O Mother of the Universe! What is surprising in this?
* **Paripūrṇā karuṇāsti cenmayi** = If Your complete compassion remains on me
* **Aparādhaparamparāparaṃ** = One who is filled with an endless series of offenses
* **Na hi mātā samUpekṣate sutam** = A mother never abandons/neglects her son.

**Translation:**
O Mother of the Universe! What is so surprising if You continue to shower Your complete and perfect compassion upon me? Even if a son commits an endless series of offenses and is entirely absorbed in them, a true mother never abandons or forsakes her child.

---

#### ॥ Verse 12 ॥
मत्समः पातकी नास्ति पापघ्नी त्वत्समा न हि ।
एवं ज्ञात्वा महादेवि यथायोग्यं तथा कुरु ॥ १२॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Matsamaḥ pātakī nāsti** = There is no sinner equal to me
* **Pāpaghnī tvatsamā na hi** = And there is no destroyer of sins equal to You
* **Evaṃ jñātvā mahādevi** = Knowing this, O Mahadevi (Great Goddess)!
* **Yathāyogyaṃ tathā kuru** = Do whatever You deem fit and proper.

**Translation:**
O Mother! There is no sinner in this world equal to me, and there is no destroyer of sins equal to You. Knowing this perfectly well, O Mahadevi, I surrender entirely to You—do with me whatever You deem proper.

---

### 3. Benefits of Recitation (Phalashruti)

The 'Devyaparadhakshamapana Stotram' effectively dissolves the seeker's ego, leading to a state of absolute, childlike surrender at the lotus feet of the Divine Mother. Chanting this hymn at the end of Navaratri pujas, Chandi Path, or daily worship absolves the devotee of all the doshas (blemishes) incurred due to mispronunciation of mantras, skipping of steps, or lack of focus during the ritual. Reciting it removes the heavy burden of guilt from the mind, ensuring that the seeker receives Mother Bhavani's supreme peace, protection, and boundless forgiveness.
