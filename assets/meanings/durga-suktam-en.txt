## Śrī Durgāsūktam (Mahānārāyaṇa Upaniṣad)
**Complete Text, Word-by-Word Meaning, and Spiritual Significance**

---

### 1. Introduction & Spiritual Significance

The Durga Suktam is a highly revered Vedic hymn from the Mahanarayana Upanishad (Anuvaka 2) of the Taittiriya Aranyaka (Krishna Yajurveda). 

While in later Puranic literature the name *'Durga'* refers specifically to the Divine Mother, in Vedic philosophy *'Durga'* denotes *"That Divine Power which takes us across all difficult passages, obstacles, and the ocean of Samsara."* In this Suktam, Agni (Jatavedas — the All-Knowing Fire) is invoked as the radiant embodiment of Goddess Durga to burn away our inner impurities and ferry us across all adversities just as a sturdy ship ferries travelers across a rough ocean.

* **Rishi:** Vedic Seers (Seers of Agni Jatavedas & Divine Protection)
* **Devata:** Agni Jatavedas & Goddess Durga (The Fire-Lustred Divine Mother)
* **Chandas:** Trishtubh & Gayatri (for the Katyayana Gayatri)
* **Purpose:** Overcoming obstacles, spiritual protection, dispelling fear, and ultimate peace.

---

### 2. Sanskrit Mantras, Word-by-Word & English Meanings

#### ॥ MANTRA 1 ॥
ॐ जा॒तवे॑दसे सुनवाम॒ सोम॑ मरातीय॒तो निद॑हाति॒ वेदः॑ ।
स नः॑ पर्ष॒दति॑ दु॒र्गाणि॒ विश्वा॑ ना॒वेव॒ सिन्धुं॑ दुरि॒तात्य॒ग्निः ॥ १॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Om Jātavedase** = To Agni Jātavedas (the All-Knowing Fire who knows all born beings)
* **Sunavāma Somam** = We press and offer the Soma juice (offering of devotion)
* **Arātīyataḥ** = Of those who are hostile / our inner and outer enemies
* **Vedaḥ** = The wealth / strength / possessions
* **Nidahāti** = May He consume and burn away entirely
* **Saḥ Naḥ** = May He (that Agni) take us
* **Parṣad-ati** = Ferry across / deliver safely beyond
* **Durgāṇi Viśvā** = All difficult passages, perils, and obstacles
* **Nāvā-iva Sindhum** = Just as a boat across a rough ocean
* **Duritā Ati Agniḥ** = May Agni carry us across all sins and calamities.

**Line-by-Line Meaning:**
We offer the sacred Soma (our devotion and offerings) to Agni Jātavedas, the All-Knowing Divine Fire. May He consume and burn away the wealth, power, and malice of all our hostile enemies (both external foes and internal impurities like anger and ego). Just as a sturdy ship ferries travelers safely across a turbulent sea, may Agni deliver us across all obstacles, perils, and sins.

---

#### ॥ MANTRA 2 ॥
ताम॒ग्निव॑र्णां तप॑सा ज्वल॒न्तीं वै॑रोच॒नीं क॑र्मफ॒लेषु॒ जुष्टा᳚म् ।
दु॒र्गां दे॒वीग्ं शर॑णम॒हं प्रप॑द्ये सु॒तर॑सि तरसे॒ नमः॑ ॥ २॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Tām Agni-varṇām** = Her who is of the lustrous color of Fire
* **Tapasā Jvalantīm** = Who is blazing with the power of Tapas (austere knowledge)
* **Vairocanīm** = Daughter of the Supreme Light / Radiance of the Sun (Virochana)
* **Karma-phaleṣu Juṣṭām** = Who is sought after for / resides in the fruits of actions
* **Durgām Devīm** = In Goddess Durgā, the remover of perils
* **Śaraṇam Ahaṃ Prapadye** = I take absolute refuge
* **Su-tarasi** = O Goddess who ferries us across easily!
* **Tarase Namaḥ** = Salutations to Your saving, swift ferrying power!

**Line-by-Line Meaning:**
I take absolute refuge in Goddess Durgā, who is radiant like blazing fire, shining brilliantly through Her Tapas (spiritual austerity), who belongs to the Supreme Solar Radiance, and who graciously bestows the rightful fruits of all actions. O Goddess who effortlessly ferries us across the ocean of Samsara! I bow to Your swift and compassionate saving power.

---

#### ॥ MANTRA 3 ॥
अग्ने॒ त्वं पा॑रया॒ नव्यो॑ अ॒स्मान्थ्स्व॒स्तिभि॒रति॑ दु॒र्गाणि॒ विश्वा᳚ ।
पूश्च॑ पृ॒थ्वी ब॑हु॒ला न॑ उ॒र्वी भवा॑ तो॒काय॒ तन॑याय॒ शंयोः ॥ ३॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Agne Tvaṃ** = O Agni (Divine Fire), You
* **Pārayā** = Ferry us across / deliver us
* **Navyaḥ** = Ever-new / worthy of praise
* **Asmān Svastibhiḥ** = Us, by auspicious and blessed means
* **Ati Durgāṇi Viśvā** = Across all difficult obstacles and perils
* **Pūḥ-ca Pṛthvī** = May this earth be like an impenetrable stronghold (city)
* **Bahulā Naḥ Urvī** = Broad, spacious, and prosperous for us
* **Bhavā Tokāya Tanayāya** = Be for our children (toka) and grandchildren (tanaya)
* **Śaṃ-yoḥ** = A source of happiness (śam) and freedom from illness/suffering (yoḥ).

**Line-by-Line Meaning:**
O ever-praiseworthy Agni! By Your auspicious and blessed grace, lead us safely across all difficult obstacles and tribulations. May this earth and our dwelling place be as secure as an impenetrable fortress, spacious and bountiful for us. May You be the bestower of supreme peace, health, and prosperity to our children and grandchildren.

---

#### ॥ MANTRA 4 ॥
विश्वा॑नि नो दु॒र्गहा॑ जातवेदः॒ सिन्धुं॒ न ना॒वा दु॑रि॒ताऽति॑पर्षि ।
अग्ने॑ अत्रि॒वन्मन॑सा गृणा॒नो᳚ऽस्माकं॑ बोध्यवि॒ता त॒नूना᳚म् ॥ ४॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Viśvāni Naḥ** = All our
* **Durga-hā** = O Destroyer of difficult obstacles!
* **Jātavedaḥ** = O All-Knowing Agni!
* **Sindhuṃ Na Nāvā** = Just as one crosses a river/sea by boat
* **Duritā Ati-parṣi** = Ferry us across all sins and calamities
* **Agne Atri-vat** = O Agni! Just as You did for Sage Atri
* **Manasā Gṛṇānaḥ** = Welcoming our hymns with a mindful heart
* **Asmākaṃ Bodhi** = Be attentive and watchful over us
* **Avitā Tanūnām** = And become the protector of our bodies (and souls).

**Line-by-Line Meaning:**
O All-Knowing Agni, Destroyer of all obstacles! Just as a boatman carries passengers across the sea, deliver us across all our sins, sorrows, and difficulties. O Agni! Just as You listened to Sage Atri's prayers and protected him, receive our heartfelt hymns with pleasure and remain ever watchful as the protector of our bodies and lives.

---

#### ॥ MANTRA 5 ॥
पृ॒त॒ना॒ जित॒ग्ं॒ सह॑मानमु॒ग्रम॒ग्निग्ं हु॑वेम पर॒माथ्स॒धस्था᳚त् ।
स नः॑ पर्ष॒दति॑ दु॒र्गाणि॒ विश्वा॒ क्षाम॑द्दे॒वो अति॑ दुरि॒ताऽत्य॒ग्निः ॥ ५॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Pṛtanā-jitam** = The conqueror of hostile armies (and inner negative forces)
* **Sahamānam Ugram** = Victorious, overwhelming, and fierce
* **Agniṃ Huvema** = We invoke Agni
* **Paramāt Sadhasthāt** = From His supreme abode (highest heaven / sanctuary of heart)
* **Saḥ Naḥ Parṣad-ati** = May He carry us safely across
* **Durgāṇi Viśvā** = All difficult passages and afflictions
* **Kṣāmat Devaḥ** = May the shining God burn away our imperfections
* **Ati Duritā Ati-Agniḥ** = May Agni deliver us beyond all sins and evils.

**Line-by-Line Meaning:**
We invoke from His highest realm the fierce, victorious, and unconquerable Agni, who vanquishes all hostile armies and inner demons. May that radiant Divine Fire consume our sins to ashes and carry us safely across all the difficult passages and perils of life.

---

#### ॥ MANTRA 6 ॥
प्र॒त्नोषि॑ क॒मीड्यो॑ अध्व॒रेषु॑ स॒नाच्च॒ होता॒ नव्य॑श्च॒ सत्सि॑ ।
स्वाञ् चा᳚ग्ने त॒नुवं॑ पि॒प्रय॑स्वा॒स्मभ्यं॑ च॒ सौभ॑ग॒माय॑जस्व ॥ ६॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Pratnoṣi** = You expand / diffuse (joy and the sacrifice)
* **Kam Īḍyaḥ** = You are bliss-embodied and worthy of worship
* **Adhvareṣu** = In all sacred sacrifices
* **Sanāt Ca Hotā** = You are the eternal Hotar (invoker of gods)
* **Navyaḥ Ca Satsi** = And You take Your seat with ever-fresh enthusiasm
* **Svāñ-Ca Agne Tanuvaṃ** = O Agni! Your own divine body (flame)
* **Piprayasva** = Gladden / make happy and prosperous
* **Asmabhyaṃ Ca** = And for us
* **Saubhagam Āyajasva** = Procure supreme good fortune through sacrifice.

**Line-by-Line Meaning:**
O Agni! You are worthy of praise in all sacred rituals and You expand bliss throughout the universe. You are the eternal priest (Hotar) from ancient times, yet You take Your seat with ever-new vigor. O Lord! Gladden and strengthen Your own divine form, and bring about supreme good fortune, prosperity, and auspiciousness for all of us.

---

#### ॥ MANTRA 7 ॥
गोभि॒र्जुष्ट॑म॒युजो॒ निषि॑क्तं॒ तवे᳚न्द्र विष्णो॒रनु॒सञ्च॑रेम ।
नाक॑स्य पृ॒ष्ठम॒भि सं॒वसा॑नो॒ वैष्ण॑वीं लो॒क इ॒ह मा॑दयन्ताम् ॥ ७॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Gobhiḥ Juṣṭām** = Pleasing to the senses and praised by Vedic words
* **Ayujaḥ Niṣiktam** = Inaccessible to the non-yogis / pervading everywhere
* **Tava Indra Viṣṇoḥ** = Of Yours, O Indra (Lord of Power) and O Vishnu (All-Pervading)
* **Anu-sañcarema** = May we follow and walk along Your path
* **Nākasya Pṛṣṭham** = On the highest summit of heaven (free from sorrow)
* **Abhi Saṃvasānaḥ** = Dwelling together in perfection
* **Vaiṣṇavīṃ Loke** = In the realm sacred to Lord Vishnu
* **Iha Mādayantām** = May we delight and experience spiritual ecstasy here.

**Line-by-Line Meaning:**
O Indra and O All-Pervading Vishnu! May we follow Your divine path—a path honored by pure senses and sacred Vedic speech, which is unattainable by those lacking spiritual discipline. Dwelling together upon the loftiest heights of the sorrowless heaven, may we rejoice and experience supreme bliss in this sacred realm of Lord Vishnu.

---

#### ॥ MANTRA 8 (KATYAYANA GAYATRI / DURGA GAYATRI) ॥
ॐ का॒त्या॒य॒नाय॑ वि॒द्महे॑ कन्यकु॒मारि॑ धीमहि ।
तन्नो॑ दुर्गिः प्रचो॒दया᳚त् ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Om Kātyāyanāya Vidmahe** = We realize / meditate upon Her worshipped by Sage Kātyāyana
* **Kanyākumāri Dhīmahi** = We meditate upon the ever-pure Maiden Goddess (Kanyākumārī)
* **Tat Naḥ Durgiḥ** = May Goddess Durgi (Durgā)
* **Pracodayāt** = Awaken, illuminate, and inspire our intellect.

**Line-by-Line Meaning:**
We realize and commune with Goddess Kātyāyanī; we meditate deeply upon the ever-pure, virginal Divine Mother Kanyākumārī. May Goddess Durgā illuminate our intellect and inspire us toward truth, righteous action, and spiritual liberation.

---

### 3. Benefits of Recitation (Phalashruti)

Regular recitation of the Durga Suktam dispels fear, anxiety, and internal negativity. By the grace of Agni Jatavedas and Goddess Durga, the seeker is granted spiritual protection, courage, and resilience against all adversities. It cleanses the mind of impurities and ensures a safe passage through the trials of life toward liberation.
