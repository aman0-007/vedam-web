## Śrī Dūrvāsūktam (Taittirīya Āraṇyaka / Mahānārāyaṇa Upaniṣad)
**Complete Text, Word-by-Word Meaning, and Spiritual Significance**

---

### 1. Introduction & Spiritual Significance

The Durva Suktam is a highly revered Vedic hymn from the Taittiriya Aranyaka (Mahanarayana Upanishad) and classical Vedic ritual texts, chanted to glorify the sacred Durva grass and Goddess Vasundhara (Mother Earth).

In Vedic philosophy, Durva grass represents immortality, resilience, spiritual vitality, and unbroken continuity. Just as Durva spreads continuously through its network of roots and shoots even under harsh conditions, the seeker invokes Goddess Durva and Mother Earth to purify all sins, dispel bad dreams, and multiply prosperity, lineage, and spiritual wisdom by hundreds and thousands.

* **Rishi:** Vedic Seers (Seers of Goddess Durva and Mother Earth)
* **Devata:** Goddess Durva and Mother Earth (Vasundhara)
* **Chandas:** Anushtubh and Vedic meters
* **Purpose:** Removal of sins and bad dreams, longevity, prosperity, and divine protection.

---

### 2. Sanskrit Mantras, Word-by-Word & English Meanings

#### ॥ MANTRA 1 ॥
स॒ह॒स्र॒पर॑मा दे॒वी॒ श॒तमू॑ला श॒ताङ्कु॑रा ।
सर्वग्ं॑ हरतु॑ मे पा॒पं॒ दू॒र्वा दुः॑स्वप्न॒नाशि॑नी ॥ १॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Sahasra-paramā** = Supreme with thousands of virtues and powers
* **Devī** = O Divine Goddess (Durva)
* **Śata-mūlā** = Having hundreds of roots
* **Śatāṅkurā** = And having hundreds of shoots and sprouts
* **Sarvam Haratu Me Pāpam** = May She destroy all my sins
* **Dūrvā** = The sacred Durva grass
* **Duḥsvapna-nāśinī** = The destroyer of bad dreams and inauspicious influences.

**Line-by-Line Meaning:**
O Divine Durva Goddess, supreme with thousands of virtues, spreading with hundreds of roots and hundreds of shoots! May the sacred Durva, the destroyer of bad dreams and negative influences, wash away all my sins and impurities.

---

#### ॥ MANTRA 2 ॥
काण्डा᳚त्काण्डात्प्र॒रोह॑न्ती परु॑षः परुष॒स्परि॑ ।
ए॒वानो॑ दूर्वे॒ प्रत॑नु स॒हस्रे॑ण श॒तेन॑ च ॥ २॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Kāṇḍāt-kāṇḍāt** = From every stem and internode
* **Prarohantī** = Sprouting and growing forth
* **Paruṣaḥ Paruṣaḥ Pari** = And spreading from every joint and node all around
* **Evā** = In this very manner
* **Naḥ** = Us / our lineage and prosperity
* **Dūrve** = O Goddess Durva!
* **Pratanu** = Extend and propagate
* **Sahasreṇa Śatena Ca** = By hundreds and by thousands.

**Line-by-Line Meaning:**
Sprouting forth from every stem and growing endlessly from every joint and node all around—O Goddess Durva! In the exact same manner, extend and multiply us (our lineage, wisdom, and prosperity) by hundreds and by thousands.

---

#### ॥ MANTRA 3 ॥
या श॒तेन॑ प्रत॒नोषि॑ स॒हस्रे॑ण वि॒रोह॑सि ।
तस्या᳚स्ते देवीष्टके वि॒धेम॑ ह॒विषा॑ व॒यम् ॥ ३॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Yā** = You who
* **Śatena** = By hundreds
* **Pratanoṣi** = Extend and spread
* **Sahasreṇa** = And by thousands
* **Virohasi** = Sprout and grow abundantly
* **Tasyāḥ Te** = To You of such nature
* **Devi Iṣṭake** = O Divine, adorable Goddess (sacred altar deity of sacrifice)!
* **Vidhema Haviṣā Vayam** = We worship and offer oblations with devotion.

**Line-by-Line Meaning:**
You who expand by hundreds and grow abundantly by thousands—O adorable Divine Goddess! To You of such infinite vitality, we offer our sacred oblations and worship with devotion.

---

#### ॥ MANTRA 4 ॥
अश्वा॑क्रान्ते र॑थक्रा॒न्ते॒ वि॒ष्णुक्रा᳚न्ते व॒सुन्ध॑रा ।
शिरसा॑ धार॑यिष्या॒मि॒ र॒क्ष॒स्व मां᳚ पदे॒पदे ॥ ४॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Aśvakrānte** = O Earth traversed by sacred horses
* **Rathakrānte** = O Earth traversed by chariots
* **Viṣṇukrānte** = O Earth measured/stepped upon by Lord Vishnu (as Vamana)
* **Vasundharā** = O Goddess Earth, the bearer of all wealth and treasures!
* **Śirasā Dhārayiṣyāmi** = I hold You (Your sacred dust and Durva) upon my head
* **Rakṣasva Mām** = Protect me
* **Pade Pade** = At every step (at every moment and place).

**Line-by-Line Meaning:**
O Goddess Earth (Vasundhara), traversed by sacred horses, crossed by chariots, and sanctified by the strides of Lord Vishnu! I reverently bear Your sacred clay and Durva grass upon my head; protect me at every single step of my life.

---

#### ॐ शान्तिः॒ शान्तिः॒ शान्तिः॑ ॥
May there be complete peace from physical, divine, and internal suffering.

---

### 3. Benefits of Recitation (Phalashruti)

Regular chanting of the Durva Suktam dispels negative thoughts, bad dreams, and accumulated sins. By the grace of Goddess Durva and Mother Earth, the seeker is blessed with lasting health, unbroken lineage, prosperity, and divine protection at every step of life.
