## ॥ Shri Ganapati Atharvashirsham ॥
**Complete Text, Word-by-Word Meaning, Translation & Significance**

---

### 1. Introduction & Spiritual Significance

The 'Shri Ganapati Atharvashirsham' is a highly revered and sacred Upanishad from the Atharva Veda. It is the ultimate and most authentic Vedic hymn dedicated to Lord Ganesha. In this text, Lord Ganapati is not merely viewed as a deity, but is established as the Supreme Brahman (the absolute creator, preserver, and destroyer of the universe).

In the Sanatana tradition, this Atharvashirsham is chanted with Vedic intonations (Svaras) and used for Abhishekam (ritual bathing) before beginning any auspicious task, commencing education, or seeking the removal of severe obstacles. It brilliantly explains the profound secret of Ganesha's seed mantra 'Gam' (*Ganesha Vidya*) and includes the powerful Ganesha Gayatri Mantra.

* **Veda:** Atharva Veda
* **Presiding Deity:** Lord Shri Ganapati (as the Supreme Brahman)
* **Sage (Rishi):** Sage Ganaka
* **Purpose (Viniyoga):** For the destruction of all obstacles, attainment of supreme intellect and wisdom, eradication of ignorance, and the realization of the Supreme Brahman (Moksha).

### 2. Sanskrit Verses with Svaras, Word-by-Word Meaning & Translation

#### ॥ Shanti Mantra (Peace Invocation) ॥
ॐ भ॒द्रं कर्णे॑भिः शृणु॒याम॑ देवाः । भ॒द्रं प॑श्येमा॒क्षभि॒र्यज॑त्राः । 
स्थि॒रैरङ्गै᳚स्तुष्टु॒वाग्ं स॑स्त॒नूभिः॑ । व्यशे॑म दे॒वहि॑तं॒ यदायुः॑ । 
स्व॒स्ति न॒ इन्द्रो॑ वृ॒द्धश्र॑वाः । स्व॒स्ति नः॑ पू॒षा वि॒श्ववे॑दाः । 
स्व॒स्ति न॒स्तार्क्ष्यो॒ अरि॑ष्टनेमिः । स्व॒स्ति नो॒ बृह॒स्पति॑र्दधातु ॥
ॐ शान्तिः॒ शान्तिः॒ शान्तिः॑ ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Bhadraṃ karṇebhiḥ śṛṇuyāma devāḥ** = O Gods, may we hear only what is auspicious (bhadram) with our ears
* **Bhadraṃ paśyemākṣabhiryajatrāḥ** = O protectors of sacrifices, may we see only what is auspicious with our eyes
* **Sthirairaṅgaistuṣṭuvāgṃ sastanūbhiḥ** = With steady/healthy limbs and bodies, while praising You
* **Vyaśema devahitaṃ yadāyuḥ** = May we enjoy the full span of life allotted to us by the divine
* **Svasti na indro vṛddhaśravāḥ** = May Lord Indra of great fame bestow auspiciousness upon us
* **Svasti naḥ pūṣā viśvavedāḥ** = May Pushan (the Sun God), the knower of all, bestow auspiciousness upon us
* **Svasti nastārkṣyo ariṣṭanemiḥ** = May Tarkshya (Garuda), the destroyer of evils, bestow auspiciousness upon us
* **Svasti no bṛhaspatirdadhātu** = And may Brihaspati (the preceptor of gods) establish auspiciousness for us.

**Translation:**
O Gods! May we hear only auspicious things with our ears, and may we see only auspicious things with our eyes. May we praise You with steady limbs and healthy bodies, and thus enjoy the complete lifespan allotted to us by the divine. May Lord Indra of magnificent glory, the omniscient Sun God (Pushan), Lord Garuda who eradicates all evils, and Lord Brihaspati bestow absolute welfare and auspiciousness upon us.

#### ॥ Mantra 1 (The Supreme Brahman Form) ॥
ॐ नम॑स्ते ग॒णप॑तये । त्वमे॒व प्र॒त्यक्षं॒ तत्त्व॑मसि । 
त्वमे॒व के॒वलं॒ कर्ता॑ऽसि । त्वमे॒व के॒वलं॒ धर्ता॑ऽसि । 
त्वमे॒व के॒वलं॒ हर्ता॑ऽसि । त्वमेव सर्वं खल्विदं॑ ब्रह्मा॒ऽसि । 
त्वं साक्षादात्मा॑ऽसि नि॒त्यम् ॥ १॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Om namaste gaṇapataye** = Om, salutations to You, O Lord Ganapati
* **Tvameva pratyakṣaṃ tattvamasi** = You alone are the visible, manifest Supreme Truth (Tattva)
* **Tvameva kevalaṃ kartā'si** = You alone are the sole Creator
* **Tvameva kevalaṃ dhartā'si** = You alone are the sole Preserver/Sustainer
* **Tvameva kevalaṃ hartā'si** = You alone are the sole Destroyer
* **Tvameva sarvaṃ khalvidaṃ brahmā'si** = You alone are entirely this entire universe; You are Brahman
* **Tvaṃ sākṣādātmā'si nityam** = You are the eternal, manifest Atman (Supreme Soul).

**Translation:**
Om. Salutations to You, O Lord Ganapati. You alone are the visible and direct embodiment of the Supreme Truth. You alone are the sole creator, the sole preserver, and the sole destroyer of this universe. Verily, You alone are the Supreme Brahman encompassing this entire universe. You are the eternal, manifest Supreme Soul (Atman).

---

#### ॥ Mantra 2 (Declaration of Truth) ॥
ऋ॑तं व॒च्मि । स॑त्यं व॒च्मि ॥ २॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning & Translation:**
* **Ṛtaṃ vacmi** = I speak the divine, universal law (Ritam)
* **Satyaṃ vacmi** = I speak the absolute truth.
(Meaning: Whatever glory I am declaring about You, O Lord, is the eternal and absolute truth).

#### ॥ Mantra 3 (Prayer for Protection) ॥
अ॒व त्वं॒ माम् । अव॑ व॒क्तारम्᳚ । अव॑ श्रो॒तारम्᳚ । अव॑ दा॒तारम्᳚ । 
अव॑ धा॒तार᳚म् । अवानूचानम॑वशि॒ष्यम् । अव॑ प॒श्चात्ता᳚त् । अव॑ पु॒रस्ता᳚त् । 
अवोत्त॒रात्ता᳚त् । अव॑ दक्षि॒णात्ता᳚त् । अव॑ चो॒र्ध्वात्ता᳚त् । अवाध॒रात्ता᳚त् । 
सर्वतो मां पाहि पाहि॑ सम॒न्तात् ॥ ३॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Ava tvaṃ mām** = Please protect (ava) me
* **Ava vaktāram, ava śrotāram** = Protect the speaker (who chants this), protect the listener
* **Ava dātāram, ava dhātāram** = Protect the giver (of knowledge), protect the receiver/sustainer
* **Avānūcānamavaśiṣyam** = Protect the learned teacher, protect the disciple
* **Ava paścāttāt... avādharāttāt** = Protect from behind, from the front, from the north, from the south, from above, and from below
* **Sarvato māṃ pāhi pāhi samantāt** = O Lord! Protect me, protect me from all sides and in all ways.

**Translation:**
O Lord Ganapati, please protect me. Protect the one who speaks this hymn, and protect the one who listens to it. Protect the one who imparts knowledge, and protect the one who receives and sustains it. Protect the teacher, and protect the disciple. Protect me from the back, from the front, from the north, from the south, from above, and from below. O Lord! Protect me completely from all directions.

#### ॥ Mantra 4 (Philosophical Nature) ॥
त्वं वाङ्मय॑स्त्वं चिन्म॒यः । त्वमानन्दमय॑स्त्वं ब्रह्म॒मयः । 
त्वं सच्चिदानन्दाऽद्वि॑तीयो॒ऽसि । त्वं प्र॒त्यक्षं॒ ब्रह्मा॑ऽसि । 
त्वं ज्ञानमयो विज्ञान॑मयो॒ऽसि ॥ ४॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Tvaṃ vāṅmayastvaṃ cinmayaḥ** = You are the essence of speech/words, You are the essence of pure consciousness
* **Tvamānandamayastvaṃ brahmamayaḥ** = You are the essence of absolute bliss, You are the essence of Brahman
* **Tvaṃ saccidānandā'dvitīyo'si** = You are the non-dual (without a second) embodiment of Existence, Consciousness, and Bliss (Sat-Chit-Ananda)
* **Tvaṃ pratyakṣaṃ brahmā'si** = You are the visibly manifest Supreme Brahman
* **Tvaṃ jñānamayo vijñānamayo'si** = You are the embodiment of worldly knowledge and supreme spiritual wisdom.

**Translation:**
O Lord Ganapati! You are the very essence of spoken word, and You are pure consciousness itself. You are absolute bliss, and You are the Supreme Brahman. You are the non-dual reality of Truth, Consciousness, and Bliss (Sat-Chit-Ananda). You are the visible, manifest Brahman. You are the ultimate embodiment of all material knowledge and transcendental spiritual wisdom.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 5 (Cosmic Form) ॥
सर्वं जगदिदं त्व॑त्तो जा॒यते । सर्वं जगदिदं त्व॑त्तस्ति॒ष्ठति । 
सर्वं जगदिदं त्वयि लय॑मेष्य॒ति । सर्वं जगदिदं त्वयि॑ प्रत्ये॒ति ।
त्वं भूमिरापोऽनलोऽनि॑लो न॒भः । त्वं चत्वारि वा᳚क्पदा॒नि ॥ ५॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Sarvaṃ jagadidaṃ tvatto jāyate** = This entire universe is born from You
* **Sarvaṃ jagadidaṃ tvattastiṣṭhati** = This entire universe is sustained/supported by You
* **Sarvaṃ jagadidaṃ tvayi layameṣyati** = This entire universe will dissolve into You
* **Sarvaṃ jagadidaṃ tvayi pratyeti** = This entire universe ultimately returns to You
* **Tvaṃ bhūmirāpo'nalo'nilo nabhaḥ** = You are earth, water, fire, air, and ether (space)
* **Tvaṃ catvāri vākpadāni** = You are the four levels of speech (Para, Pashyanti, Madhyama, and Vaikhari).

**Translation:**
O Supreme Lord! This entire universe originates from You. This entire universe is sustained by Your power. This entire universe will ultimately dissolve into You, and everything will return to You. You are the five great elements: earth, water, fire, air, and ether. You are the four levels of cosmic speech (Para, Pashyanti, Madhyama, and Vaikhari).

---

#### ॥ Mantra 6 (Beyond the Gunas and Time) ॥
त्वं गु॒णत्र॑याती॒तः । त्वमवस्थात्र॑याती॒तः । 
त्वं दे॒हत्र॑याती॒तः । त्वं का॒लत्र॑याती॒तः ।
त्वं मूलाधारस्थितो॑ऽसि नि॒त्यम् । त्वं शक्तित्र॑यात्म॒कः । त्वां योगिनो ध्याय॑न्ति नि॒त्यम् ।
त्वं ब्रह्मा त्वं विष्णुस्त्वं रुद्रस्त्वमिन्द्रस्त्वमग्निस्त्वं वायुस्त्वं सूर्यस्त्वं चन्द्रमास्त्वं ब्रह्म॒ भूर्भुव॒स्स्वरोम् ॥ ६॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning & Translation:**
* **Tvaṃ guṇatrayātītaḥ** = You are entirely beyond the three Gunas (Sattva, Rajas, and Tamas).
* **Tvamavasthātrayātītaḥ** = You are beyond the three states of consciousness (waking, dreaming, and deep sleep).
* **Tvaṃ dehatrayātītaḥ** = You are beyond the three bodies (gross, subtle, and causal).
* **Tvaṃ kālatrayātītaḥ** = You are beyond the three periods of time (past, present, and future).
* **Tvaṃ mūlādhārasthito'si nityam** = You eternally reside in the Muladhara Chakra (at the base of the spine).
* **Tvaṃ śaktitrayātmakaḥ** = You are the embodiment of the three cosmic powers (Will, Knowledge, and Action).
* **Tvāṃ yogino dhyāyanti nityam** = Great yogis meditate upon You continuously.
* **Tvaṃ brahmā... bhūrbhuvasvarom** = O Lord Ganapati! You are Brahma, You are Vishnu, You are Rudra (Shiva). You are Indra, Agni (Fire), Vayu (Air), Surya (Sun), and Chandrama (Moon). You are the Supreme Brahman, the three worlds (Bhur, Bhuva, Svaha), and the sacred syllable OM.

#### ॥ Mantra 7 (Ganesha Vidya / The Root Mantra) ॥
ग॒णादिं᳚ पूर्व॑मुच्चा॒र्य॒ व॒र्णादीं᳚स्तदन॒न्तरम् । अनुस्वारः प॑रत॒रः । अर्धे᳚न्दुल॒सितम् । तारे॑ण ऋ॒द्धम् ।
एतत्तवमनु॑स्वरू॒पम् । गकारः पू᳚र्वरू॒पम् । अकारो मध्य॑मरू॒पम् । अनु॒स्वारश्चा᳚न्त्यरू॒पम् ।
बिन्दुरुत्त॑ररू॒पम् । नादः॑ सन्धा॒नम् । सग्ंहि॑ता स॒न्धिः ।
सैषा गणे॑शवि॒द्या । गण॑क ऋ॒षिः । निचृद्गाय॑त्रीच्छ॒न्दः । गणपति॑र्देव॒ता । 
ॐ गं ग॒णप॑तये नमः ॥ ७॥

**Translation:**
(This mantra reveals the secret formation of Lord Ganesha's seed mantra **'Gam'**):
First, utter the first letter of the word 'Gana' (which is the consonant **'G'**). Then, utter the first letter of the alphabet (which is the vowel **'A'**). Follow this with the **Anusvara** (the nasal dot), beautifully adorned with the half-moon (crescent). Finally, prefix this with the Tara (the syllable **OM**). (This creates the ultimate seed mantra **'Om Gam'**).
In this mantra, 'G' is the initial form, 'A' is the middle form, the Anusvara is the final form, the Bindu (dot) is the upper form, and the Nada (sound) is the cosmic connection joining them. This complete joining is the *Samhita* (perfection of sound).
This sacred science is known as **'Ganesha Vidya'**. The sage who realized this mantra is Sage **Ganaka**. Its meter is the **Nichrid Gayatri**. Its presiding deity is Lord **Ganapati**. The supreme mantra is: **"Om Gam Ganapataye Namah"** (Salutations to Lord Ganapati).

#### ॥ Mantra 8 (Ganesha Gayatri Mantra) ॥
ए॒क॒द॒न्ताय॑ वि॒द्महे॑ वक्रतु॒ण्डाय॑ धीमहि । तन्नो॑ दन्तिः प्रचो॒दया᳚त् ॥ ८॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning & Translation:**
We realize the Lord with one tusk (Ekadanta); we meditate upon the Lord with the curved trunk (Vakratunda). May that Lord Danti (the elephant-faced Lord Ganesha) illuminate our intellects and inspire us toward the righteous path. (This is the famous Ganesha Gayatri Mantra).

---

#### ॥ Mantra 9 (Meditation / Saguna Form) ॥
ए॒क॒द॒न्तं च॑तुर्ह॒स्तं॒ पा॒शम॑ङ्कुश॒धारि॑णम् । रदं॑ च॒ वर॑दं ह॒स्तै॒र्बि॒भ्राणं॑ मूष॒कध्व॑जम् । 
रक्तं॑ ल॒म्बोद॑रं शू॒र्प॒क॒र्णकं॑ रक्त॒वास॑सम् । रक्त॑ग॒न्धानु॑लिप्ता॒ङ्गं॒ र॒क्तपु॑ष्पैः सु॒पूजि॑तम् ।
भक्ता॑नु॒कम्पि॑नं दे॒वं॒ ज॒गत्का॑रण॒मच्यु॑तम् । आवि॑र्भू॒तं च॑ सृष्ट्या॒दौ॒ प्र॒कृतेः᳚ पुरु॒षात्प॑रम् । 
एवं॑ ध्यायति॑ यो नि॒त्यं॒ स॒ योगी॑ योगि॒नां व॑रः ॥ ९॥

**Translation:**
*(Dhyana Shloka - Meditation upon His Form):*
Lord Ganesha has a single tusk (Ekadanta) and four arms. In His hands, He holds a noose (Pasha), a goad (Ankusha), His own broken tusk (Rada), and exhibits the boon-giving gesture (Vara Mudra). His flag bears the emblem of a mouse (Mushaka). 
His complexion is red, He has a large belly (Lambodara), and His ears are broad like winnowing baskets. He is adorned in red garments. His divine body is smeared with fragrant red sandalwood paste, and He is exquisitely worshipped with red flowers. 
He is deeply compassionate towards His devotees. He is the imperishable (Achyuta) root cause of the entire universe. He manifested at the very beginning of creation and is supreme—transcending both Prakriti (nature) and Purusha (the cosmic soul). 
The one who continuously meditates on this divine form of Lord Ganesha becomes the most exalted among all yogis.

#### ॥ Mantra 10 (Salutations) ॥
नमो व्रातपतये नमो गणपतये नमः प्रमथपतये नमस्तेऽस्तु लम्बोदरायैकदन्ताय
विघ्ननाशिने शिवसुताय वरदमूर्तये॒ नमः ॥ १०॥

**Translation:**
Salutations to the Lord of all multitudes (Vratapati). Salutations to the Lord of Shiva's hosts (Ganapati). Salutations to the Lord of the Pramathas (Shiva's attendants). My repeated salutations to the large-bellied, one-tusked Lord, the destroyer of all obstacles, the beloved son of Lord Shiva, and the very embodiment of boon-giving grace. Salutations to You!

### 3. Benefits of Recitation (Phalashruti Summary)

Chanting the 'Shri Ganapati Atharvashirsham' instantly destroys all forms of physical, mental, and worldly obstacles. Students and seekers who recite it daily are blessed with extraordinary intellect, memory, and mastery over speech. Performing a *Homa* (fire sacrifice) using this hymn with offerings of Durva grass, Modakas, or Ghee effortlessly grants the seeker the four fundamental goals of human life: Dharma (righteousness), Artha (wealth), Kama (desires), and Moksha (liberation), ensuring immense success and supreme inner peace.
