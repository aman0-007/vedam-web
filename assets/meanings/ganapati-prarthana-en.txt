## Shri Ganapati & Saraswati Prarthana (Vedic Mantras)
**Complete Text, Word-by-Word Meaning, Translation & Significance**

---

### 1. Introduction & Spiritual Significance

The 'Ganapati & Saraswati Prarthana' is a collection of profoundly sacred Vedic mantras sourced from the Rigveda and Yajurveda. In the Sanatana Dharma tradition, before commencing any auspicious endeavor, ritual, or Vedic study, these mantras are chanted to invoke and offer obeisance to Lord Ganapati (the remover of obstacles), Goddess Saraswati (the deity of knowledge), and the revered Gurus.

* **Rishi:** Gritsamada (Ganapati Mantra), Madhuchhanda (Saraswati Mantra)
* **Deity:** Brahmanaspati/Ganapati, Goddess Saraswati, and Shri Guru
* **Purpose (Viniyoga):** For the destruction of all obstacles, attainment of success, acquisition of knowledge, and smooth completion of rituals.

---

### 2. Sanskrit Verses, Word-by-Word Meaning & English Translation

#### ॥ Ganapati Prarthana ॥
ॐ ग॒णानां᳚ त्वा ग॒णप॑तिग्ं हवामहे क॒विं क॑वी॒नामु॑प॒मश्र॑वस्तमम् ।
ज्ये॒ष्ठ॒राजं॒ ब्रह्म॑णां ब्रह्मणस्पत॒ आ नः॑ शृ॒ण्वन्नू॒तिभि॑स्सीद॒ साद॑नम् ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Om gaṇānāṃ tvā gaṇapatigṁ havāmahe** = O Lord of the celestial hosts (Ganapati)! We invoke You
* **Kaviṃ kavīnām** = Who is the wisest among the wise / the supreme visionary
* **Upamaśravastamam** = Whose fame and glory are highest and beyond comparison
* **Jyeṣṭharājam** = Who is the eldest and supreme King of all shining entities
* **Brahmaṇāṃ brahmaṇaspate** = O Lord of Vedic mantras, prayers, and supreme knowledge (Brahmanaspati)!
* **Ā naḥ śṛṇvan** = Listening to our prayers
* **Ūtibhiḥ** = With Your protective powers and graces
* **Sīda sādanam** = Please take Your seat upon this altar of worship.

**Translation:**
O Lord of the celestial hosts, Lord Ganapati! We invoke You. You are the wisest among the wise, and Your glory is highest and incomparable. You are the supreme King of all luminous entities and the Lord of Vedic wisdom. O Brahmanaspati! Listening to our heartfelt prayers, please come with Your protective graces and take Your seat upon this sacred altar.

---

#### ॥ Saraswati Prarthana ॥
प्रणो॑ दे॒वी सर॑स्वती॒ वाजे॑भिर्वा॒जिनी॑वती । 
धी॒नाम॑वि॒त्र्य॑वतु ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Pra ṇo devī sarasvatī** = May the Divine Goddess Saraswati perfectly guide and protect us
* **Vājebhir vājinīvatī** = Who is richly endowed with knowledge, sustenance, and cosmic prosperity
* **Dhīnām avitrī** = Who is the protector, inspirer, and sanctuary of noble intellects
* **Avatu** = May She protect us.

**Translation:**
May the Divine Goddess Saraswati, who is richly endowed with supreme knowledge, sustenance, and cosmic prosperity, and who is the ultimate protector of noble intellects, inspire us toward the righteous path and perfectly protect us.

---

#### ॥ Namaskara Mantra ॥
ग॒णे॒शाय॑ नमः । स॒र॒स्व॒त्यै नमः । श्री गु॒रु॒भ्यो॒ नमः । 
हरिः ओम् ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Gaṇeśāya namaḥ** = Salutations to Lord Ganesha
* **Sarasvatyai namaḥ** = Salutations to Goddess Saraswati
* **Śrī gurubhyo namaḥ** = Salutations to the revered Gurus
* **Hariḥ Om** = Salutations to the Supreme Lord Hari (Om).

**Translation:**
My repeated salutations to Lord Ganesha, the remover of obstacles; to Goddess Saraswati, the embodiment of knowledge; and to the divine Gurus who dispel ignorance. Salutations to the Supreme Lord Hari.

---

### 3. Benefits of Recitation (Phalashruti)

By chanting these Vedic mantras before beginning any auspicious work, journey, study, or ritual, Lord Ganapati destroys all hurdles and impediments. By the grace of Goddess Saraswati, the seeker is blessed with purity of speech, a sharp memory, and superior intellect. Bowing to the Gurus grants the seeker divine protection and guarantees absolute success (*Karya-siddhi*) in their endeavors.
