## ॥ Shri Ganesha Suktam ॥
**Complete Text, Word-by-Word Meaning, Translation & Significance**

---

### 1. Introduction & Spiritual Significance

The 'Shri Ganesha Suktam' is an incredibly powerful and sacred collection of mantras from the Rig Veda. Although these initial verses were originally dedicated to 'Indra' (the Supreme Lord / Master of Opulence), in the Sanatana tradition and the Ganapatya sect, these specific mantras are used for the invocation, praise, and Abhishekam (ritual bathing) of Lord Ganapati, the remover of obstacles. The concluding verses explicitly invoke 'Brahmanaspati' and 'Ganapati'.

In this Suktam, prayers are offered to Lord Ganesha to destroy ignorance and obstacles, and to bestow supreme wisdom, immense wealth, and uninterrupted success in all endeavors. Chanting this Suktam with proper Vedic intonations (Svaras) at the beginning of any auspicious work, new business, or educational pursuit is considered highly auspicious.

* **Source:** Rig Veda (Collection of mantras from Mandalas 8, 2, and 10)
* **Presiding Deity:** Lord Ganapati (The Supreme Lord / Indra / Brahmanaspati)
* **Purpose (Viniyoga):** For the pacification of all obstacles, attainment of boundless wealth and food, mastery in knowledge, and divine protection.

---

### 2. Sanskrit Verses with Svaras, Word-by-Word Meaning & Translation

#### ॥ Mantra 1 ॥
आ तू न॑ इन्द्र क्षु॒मन्तं᳚ चि॒त्रं ग्रा॒भं सं गृ॑भाय । म॒हा॒ह॒स्ती दक्षि॑णेन ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Ā tū na indra** = O Indra (Supreme Lord Ganapati)! Please come to us
* **Kṣumantaṃ citraṃ grābhaṃ** = A grasp/collection (grābham) of abundant food/wealth (kṣumantaṃ) that is wonderful and varied (citraṃ)
* **Saṃ gṛbhāya** = Hold it firmly in Your hands (to bestow upon us)
* **Mahāhastī dakṣiṇena** = O Great-handed Lord! With Your right hand (dakṣiṇena).

**Translation:**
O Supreme Lord (Ganapati)! Please come towards us. O Great-handed Lord, firmly grasp abundant nourishment, strength, and wondrous treasures in Your right hand, so that You may bestow that immense wealth upon us.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 2 ॥
वि॒द्मा हि त्वा᳚ तुविकू॒र्मिं तु॒विदे᳚ष्णं तु॒वीम॑घम् । तु॒वि॒मा॒त्रमवो᳚भिः ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Vidmā hi tvā** = We indeed know You well
* **Tuvikūrmiṃ** = As the doer of mighty/great deeds
* **Tuvideṣṇaṃ** = As the great bestower/donor of bounties
* **Tuvīmagham** = As the Lord of immense wealth (Magha)
* **Tuvimātramavobhiḥ** = And vast/boundless (tuvimātram) in Your protective powers (avobhiḥ).

**Translation:**
O Lord! We truly know You as the performer of mighty deeds, the magnificent bestower of bounties, the absolute master of infinite wealth, and the one whose protective powers are boundless to safeguard all devotees.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 3 ॥
न॒हि त्वा᳚ शूर दे॒वा न मर्ता᳚सो॒ दित्स᳚न्तम् । भी॒मं न गां वा॒रय᳚न्ते ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Nahi tvā śūra** = None can stop You, O Valiant Lord
* **Devā na martāso** = Neither gods nor mortals
* **Ditsantam** = When You resolve/wish to give (gifts to Your devotees)
* **Bhīmaṃ na gāṃ vārayante** = Just as no one can restrain (vārayante) a formidable and powerful bull (bhīmaṃ gāṃ).

**Translation:**
O Valiant Lord! When You resolve to bestow gifts and wealth upon Your devotees, neither gods nor mortals can stop You—just as no one can restrain a formidable and powerful bull from moving forward.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 4 ॥
एतो॒न्विन्द्रं॒ स्तवा॒मेशा᳚नं॒ वस्वः॑ स्व॒राजम्᳚ । न राध॑सा मर्धिषन्नः ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Etonvindraṃ stavāma** = Come, let us all praise Indra (Lord Ganapati)
* **Īśānaṃ vasvaḥ svarājam** = The absolute Lord (Īśāna) of all wealth (vasvaḥ) and the independent sovereign (svarājam)
* **Na rādhasā mardhiṣannaḥ** = May He never deprive (mardhiṣan) us of His grace/wealth (rādhasā).

**Translation:**
Come, let us all unite to praise the Supreme Lord Ganapati, who is the absolute master of all universal wealth and the independent sovereign of the cosmos. May the compassionate Lord never deprive us of His grace, success, and prosperity.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 5 ॥
प्र स्तो᳚ष॒दुप॑ गासिष॒च्छ्रव॒त्साम॑ गी॒यमा᳚नम् । अ॒भि राध॑सा जुगुरत् ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Pra stoṣad** = May He be highly pleased with our praises
* **Upa gāsiṣad** = May He approach/come near to accept our songs
* **Śravatsāma gīyamānam** = May He hear (śravat) the Sama hymns (sāma) being sung (gīyamānam)
* **Abhi rādhasā jugurat** = And greet/bless us with abundant wealth (rādhasā).

**Translation:**
May the Supreme Lord be deeply pleased with our heartfelt praises. May He come near to accept our devotions, listen to the sacred Sama hymns being sung by us, and bless us abundantly with immense spiritual and material wealth.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 6 ॥
आ नो᳚ भर॒ दक्षि॑णेना॒भि स॒व्येन॒ प्र मृ॑श । इन्द्र॒ मा नो॒ वसो॒र्निर्भा᳚क् ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Ā no bhara dakṣiṇena** = Bring/bestow upon us with Your right hand
* **Abhi savyena pra mṛśa** = And shower/extend wealth to us with Your left hand (savyena) as well
* **Indra mā no vasornirbhāk** = O Lord! Do not exclude (nirbhāk) us from our share of wealth (vasoḥ).

**Translation:**
O Lord Ganapati! Please bestow abundant wealth upon us with Your right hand, and shower us with blessings and opulence from Your left hand as well. O Supreme Lord, never exclude us from our rightful share of divine and material treasures.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 7 ॥
उप॑ क्रम॒स्वा भ॑र धृष॒ता धृ॑ष्णो॒ जना᳚नाम् । अदा᳚शूष्टरस्य॒ वेदः॑ ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Upa kramasvā bhara** = Approach us and bring (wealth)
* **Dhṛṣatā dhṛṣṇo janānām** = By forcefully subduing (dhṛṣatā) the hostile/ignorant enemies, O Courageous One (dhṛṣṇo)
* **Adāśūṣṭarasya vedaḥ** = The wealth (vedaḥ) of the extremely miserly/ungenerous ones (adāśūṣṭarasya).

**Translation:**
O Courageous Lord, subduer of enemies and obstacles! Please approach us and, by forcefully subduing those who are hostile, ungenerous, and miserly, bring their accumulated wealth to us (Your devoted seekers).

---

#### ॥ Mantra 8 ॥
इन्द्र॒ य उ॒ नु ते॒ अस्ति॒ वाजो॒ विप्रे᳚भिः॒ सनि॑त्वः । अ॒स्माभिः॒ सु तं स॑नुहि ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Indra ya u nu te asti vājo** = O Lord! Whatever immense strength, food, or wealth (vājo) belongs to You
* **Viprebhiḥ sanitvaḥ** = Which is worthy to be attained by the wise sages (viprebhiḥ)
* **Asmābhiḥ su taṃ sanuhi** = Make that well-attained by us (grant it to us).

**Translation:**
O Supreme Lord! Whatever immense strength, spiritual power, and opulence You possess, which is meant to be attained only by the wisest of sages and true devotees; O Lord, please ensure that this supreme wisdom and wealth is fully attained by us as well.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 9 ॥
स॒द्यो॒जुव॑स्ते॒ वाजा᳚ अ॒स्मभ्यं᳚ वि॒श्वश्च᳚न्द्राः । वशै᳚श्च म॒क्षू ज॑रन्ते ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Sadyojuvaste vājā** = The strength/wealth swiftly (sadyaḥ) bestowed by You
* **Asmabhyaṃ viśvaścandrāḥ** = May they be entirely delightful and auspicious (candrāḥ) for us
* **Vaśaiśca makṣū jarante** = And according to our desires (vaśaiḥ), may they come quickly (makṣū) and endure/remain forever (jarante).

**Translation:**
May the strength, nourishment, and wealth swiftly bestowed by You prove to be completely delightful and auspicious for us. According to our pure desires, may these divine blessings and siddhis reach us quickly and remain with us permanently.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 10 ॥
ग॒णानां᳚ त्वा ग॒णप॑तिं हवामहे क॒विं क॑वी॒नामु॑प॒मश्र॑वस्तमम् ।
ज्ये॒ष्ठ॒राजं॒ ब्रह्म॑णां ब्रह्मणस्पत॒ आ नः॑ शृ॒ण्वन्नू॒तिभिः॑ सीद॒ साद॑नम् ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Gaṇānāṃ tvā gaṇapatiṃ havāmahe** = We invoke You, the Lord of all multitudes/Ganas
* **Kaviṃ kavīnāmupamaśravastamam** = The wisest of the wise (kaviṃ), whose fame (śrava) is unparalleled (upama)
* **Jyeṣṭharājaṃ brahmaṇāṃ** = The supreme king (jyeṣṭharājaṃ) of all mantras/prayers (brahmaṇāṃ)
* **Brahmaṇaspata ā naḥ śṛṇvan** = O Brahmanaspati (Lord of Vedic wisdom)! Hearing our prayers, come
* **Ūtibhiḥ sīda sādanam** = With Your protective powers (ūtibhiḥ), be seated on this altar (sādanam).

**Translation:**
We invoke You, O Lord of all celestial multitudes (Ganapati)! You are the wisest among all sages, and Your glorious fame is unparalleled. You are the supreme sovereign of all sacred Vedic mantras. O Brahmanaspati! Hearing our prayers, please come with Your boundless protective powers and be gracefully seated upon the altar of our hearts/yajna.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 11 ॥
नि षु सी᳚द गणपते ग॒णेषु॒ त्वामा᳚हु॒र्विप्र॑तमं कवी॒नाम् ।
न ऋ॒ते त्वत्क्रि॑यते॒ किं च॒नारे म॒हाम॒र्कं म॑घवञ्चि॒त्रम॑र्च ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Ni ṣu sīda gaṇapate gaṇeṣu** = Be well seated, O Ganapati, among the multitudes/devotees
* **Tvāmāhurvipratamaṃ kavīnām** = The wise proclaim You to be the most exalted among sages
* **Na ṛte tvatkriyate kiṃ canāre** = Without You (ṛte), no task whatsoever can be accomplished, near or far
* **Mahāmarkaṃ maghavañcitramarca** = O Lord of wealth (Maghavan), accept this great and wondrous offering/praise (arkam).

**Translation:**
O Lord Ganapati! Please be perfectly seated among Your devotees and celestial hosts. All the wise proclaim You to be the most exalted among sages. Without Your grace, no task—big or small—can ever be accomplished in this world. Therefore, O glorious Lord of wealth, kindly accept our great, wondrous offerings and praises.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 12 ॥
अ॒भि॒ख्या नो᳚ मघव॒न्नाध॑माना॒न्त्सखे᳚ बो॒धि व॑सुपते॒ सखी᳚नाम् ।
रणं᳚ कृधि रणकृत्सत्यशु॒ष्माभ॑क्ते चि॒दा भ॑जा रा॒ये अ॒स्मान् ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Abhikhyā no maghavannādhamānāntsakhe** = Look upon us with grace, O Lord, we are supplicants, O Friend
* **Bodhi vasupate sakhīnām** = Pay heed, O Lord of wealth (vasupate), to Your friends (devotees)
* **Raṇaṃ kṛdhi raṇakṛtsatyaśuṣmābhakte** = Help us in the battle of life, O true and valiant warrior, to us who are unendowed/destitute
* **Cidā bhajā rāye asmān** = Grant us a share of Your immense wealth (rāye).

**Translation:**
O Glorious Lord! O Supreme Friend! We come to You as humble supplicants; please cast Your graceful and compassionate glance upon us. O Lord of all wealth, listen to the earnest prayers of Your friends (devotees). O Warrior of True Might, assist us in the battles and struggles of life, and bless us, who are devoid of means, with a share of Your infinite wealth and spiritual treasures.

---

#### ॐ शान्तिः॒ शान्तिः॒ शान्तिः॑ ॥
May there be absolute peace from physical, divine, and internal suffering.

---

### 3. Benefits of Recitation (Phalashruti Summary)

Chanting the 'Shri Ganesha Suktam' with proper Svaras acts as an unfailing weapon to completely uproot all obstacles, hurdles, and ignorance from one's life. Singing this Suktam before commencing any auspicious task invokes the special grace of Lord Ganapati (Brahmanaspati), ensuring that the endeavor is completed without any interruptions. Its daily recitation blesses the seeker with a razor-sharp intellect, immense societal prestige, boundless wealth, and absolute victory over enemies and life's struggles.
