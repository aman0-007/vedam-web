## Shri Hanuman Chalisa
**Complete Text, Word-by-Word Meaning, Translation & Significance**

---

### 1. Introduction & Spiritual Significance

The Shri Hanuman Chalisa is a highly revered and powerful devotional hymn composed by Goswami Tulsidas. Written in the Awadhi language, this 40-verse hymn (Chalisa) glorifies the virtues, immense strength, valor, and supreme devotion of Lord Hanuman, the greatest devotee of Lord Shri Rama.

In the Sanatana Dharma tradition, Lord Hanuman is considered a "Jagrat Devta" (living/awakened deity) in the Kali Yuga who is easily pleased by pure devotion. Daily recitation of the Hanuman Chalisa is believed to destroy all fears, diseases, crises, and negative energies. It awakens boundless courage, self-confidence, and a sense of complete surrender to the Divine within the seeker.

* **Presiding Deity:** Lord Hanuman (Incarnation of Lord Shiva and Messenger of Lord Rama)
* **Composer:** Goswami Tulsidas
* **Purpose (Viniyoga):** For the destruction of fear, resolution of crises, attainment of inner strength, relief from diseases, and to receive the grace of Lord Shri Rama.

---

### 2. Original Text (Roman/English), Word-by-Word Meaning & Translation

#### ॥ Initial Doha 1 ॥
Shri Guru Charan Saroj Raj, Nij Manu Mukuru Sudhari।
Barnau Raghubar Bimal Jasu, Jo Dayaku Phal Chari॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning (Line-by-Line):**
* **Shri Guru Charan Saroj Raj** = With the dust (raj) of the lotus feet (charan saroj) of the divine Guru.
* **Nij Manu Mukuru Sudhari** = Having cleansed/purified (sudhari) the mirror (mukuru) of my own mind (nij manu).
* **Barnau Raghubar Bimal Jasu** = I describe/narrate (barnau) the pure, unblemished glory (bimal jasu) of Lord Rama (Raghubar).
* **Jo Dayaku Phal Chari** = Which is the bestower (dayaku) of the four fruits of life (phal chari: Dharma, Artha, Kama, Moksha).

**Translation:**
Cleansing the mirror of my mind with the holy dust from the lotus feet of my Divine Guru, I narrate the pure and unblemished glory of Lord Shri Rama, which bestows the four ultimate fruits of life (Righteousness, Wealth, Desire, and Liberation).

---

#### ॥ Initial Doha 2 ॥
Buddhiheen Tanu Janike, Sumirau Pavan-Kumar।
Bal Buddhi Vidya Dehu Mohi, Harahu Kalesh Bikaar॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning (Line-by-Line):**
* **Buddhiheen Tanu Janike** = Knowing (janike) myself to be devoid of intelligence (buddhiheen) and physical strength (tanu).
* **Sumirau Pavan-Kumar** = I remember/meditate upon (sumirau) the Son of the Wind God (Pavan-Kumar).
* **Bal Buddhi Vidya Dehu Mohi** = Please bestow upon me (dehu mohi) strength (bal), wisdom (buddhi), and true knowledge (vidya).
* **Harahu Kalesh Bikaar** = And remove (harahu) all my sufferings (kalesh) and mental impurities/flaws (bikaar).

**Translation:**
Knowing myself to be ignorant and weak in body and mind, I meditate upon you, O Son of the Wind God! Please bless me with physical strength, wisdom, and knowledge, and remove all my sorrows and mental impurities.

---

#### ॥ Chaupai 1 - 2 ॥
Jai Hanuman gyan gun sagar। Jai Kapis tihun lok ujagar॥
Ram doot atulit bal dhama। Anjani-putra Pavan sut nama॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning (Line-by-Line):**
* **Jai Hanuman gyan gun sagar** = Victory to Lord Hanuman, who is an ocean (sagar) of wisdom (gyan) and virtues (gun).
* **Jai Kapis tihun lok ujagar** = Victory to the Lord of Monkeys (Kapis), whose glory is illuminated (ujagar) across the three worlds (tihun lok).
* **Ram doot atulit bal dhama** = You are the messenger of Lord Rama (Ram doot) and the abode (dhama) of incomparable strength (atulit bal).
* **Anjani-putra Pavan sut nama** = You are the son of Mother Anjani (Anjani-putra) and are known by the name 'Son of the Wind' (Pavan sut nama).

**Translation:**
Victory to you, O Hanuman, the boundless ocean of wisdom and virtues! Victory to the Lord of the Monkeys, whose divine glory illuminates the three worlds (heaven, earth, and the netherworld). You are the divine messenger of Lord Rama and the ultimate abode of immeasurable strength. You are known to the world as the son of Mother Anjani and the son of the Wind God.

---

#### ॥ Chaupai 3 - 4 ॥
Mahabir Bikram Bajrangi। Kumati nivar sumati Ke sangi॥
Kanchan baran biraj subesa। Kanan Kundal Kunchit Kesa॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning (Line-by-Line):**
* **Mahabir Bikram Bajrangi** = You are a great hero (Mahabir), supremely valiant (Bikram), with a body as hard as a thunderbolt (Bajrangi).
* **Kumati nivar sumati Ke sangi** = You dispel (nivar) evil intellect/bad thoughts (kumati) and are the companion (sangi) of good wisdom (sumati).
* **Kanchan baran biraj subesa** = Your complexion is like molten gold (kanchan baran) and you are beautifully attired (biraj subesa).
* **Kanan Kundal Kunchit Kesa** = You wear rings in your ears (kanan kundal) and have beautiful curly hair (kunchit kesa).

**Translation:**
You are a supreme hero, highly valorous, with a body as strong as a thunderbolt. You eradicate negative thoughts and evil inclinations, and you are the true companion of good wisdom and purity. Your complexion shines like pure gold, you are beautifully adorned with lovely garments, wearing divine earrings, and your hair is delightfully curly.

---

#### ॥ Chaupai 5 - 6 ॥
Hath bajra aur dhvaja biraje। Kandhe moonj janehu saje॥
Sankar suvan kesri Nandan। Tej pratap maha jag vandan॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning (Line-by-Line):**
* **Hath bajra aur dhvaja biraje** = In your hands, a thunderbolt/mace (bajra) and a banner (dhvaja) are beautifully placed (biraje).
* **Kandhe moonj janehu saje** = On your shoulder (kandhe), the sacred thread made of Munja grass (moonj janehu) gracefully adorns you (saje).
* **Sankar suvan kesri Nandan** = You are the incarnation of Lord Shiva (Sankar suvan) and the beloved son of King Kesari (Kesri Nandan).
* **Tej pratap maha jag vandan** = Because of your immense aura (tej) and great valor (pratap), the whole world worships you (jag vandan).

**Translation:**
You hold a mace (vajra) and a flag in your hands, and a sacred thread made of holy Munja grass adorns your shoulder. You are the divine incarnation of Lord Shiva and the joy of King Kesari. Your radiant aura and majestic valor are so immense that the entire universe bows down in reverence to you.

---

#### ॥ Chaupai 7 - 8 ॥
Vidyavan guni ati chatur। Ram kaj karibe ko aatur॥
Prabhu charitra sunibe ko rasiya। Ram Lakhan Sita man basiya॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning (Line-by-Line):**
* **Vidyavan guni ati chatur** = You are master of all knowledge (vidyavan), highly virtuous (guni), and extremely clever/skillful (ati chatur).
* **Ram kaj karibe ko aatur** = You are always eager and ready (aatur) to accomplish the tasks of Lord Rama (Ram kaj karibe ko).
* **Prabhu charitra sunibe ko rasiya** = You are deeply passionate (rasiya) about listening to the glorious tales (charitra) of the Lord (Prabhu).
* **Ram Lakhan Sita man basiya** = Lord Rama, Lakshmana, and Mother Sita forever reside (basiya) in your mind/heart (man).

**Translation:**
You are the repository of supreme knowledge, richly endowed with virtues, and exceptionally wise. You are always eager and intensely devoted to fulfilling the divine missions of Lord Rama. You find immense delight in listening to the divine stories of the Lord, and Shri Rama, Lakshmana, and Mother Sita eternally reside in your heart.

---

#### ॥ Chaupai 9 - 10 ॥
Sukshma roop dhari Siyahi dikhava। Vikat roop dhari lank jarava॥
Bhima roop dhari asur sanhare। Ramachandra ke kaj sanvare॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning (Line-by-Line):**
* **Sukshma roop dhari Siyahi dikhava** = Assuming a very tiny/subtle form (sukshma roop dhari), you revealed yourself to Mother Sita (Siyahi dikhava).
* **Vikat roop dhari lank jarava** = Assuming a terrifying/massive form (vikat roop dhari), you burned down the city of Lanka (lank jarava).
* **Bhima roop dhari asur sanhare** = Assuming a colossal and dreadful form (bhima roop dhari), you destroyed the demons (asur sanhare).
* **Ramachandra ke kaj sanvare** = And thus, you successfully accomplished all the missions (kaj sanvare) of Lord Shri Ramachandra.

**Translation:**
You assumed a minuscule, subtle form when you appeared before Mother Sita in the Ashok Vatika. Then, you took on a fearsome and gigantic form to burn the golden city of Lanka to ashes. Assuming an incredibly massive and dreadful form, you slaughtered the demonic forces, thereby fulfilling all the divine objectives of Lord Ramachandra.

---

#### ॥ Chaupai 11 - 12 ॥
Laye Sanjivan Lakhan Jiyaye। Shri Raghuvir Harashi ur laye॥
Raghupati Kinhi bahut badai। Tum mam priye Bharat-hi sam bhai॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning (Line-by-Line):**
* **Laye Sanjivan Lakhan Jiyaye** = You brought the life-saving herb (Laye Sanjivan) and revived Lakshmana (Lakhan jiyaye).
* **Shri Raghuvir Harashi ur laye** = Overjoyed by this (harashi), Lord Rama (Shri Raghuvir) embraced you tightly to his chest (ur laye).
* **Raghupati Kinhi bahut badai** = The Lord of the Raghus (Raghupati) praised you immensely (kinhi bahut badai).
* **Tum mam priye Bharat-hi sam bhai** = And said, "You are as dear to me (mam priye) as my own brother Bharata (Bharat-hi sam bhai)."

**Translation:**
You brought the magical Sanjivani herb from the Himalayas and saved Lakshmana's life. Overjoyed by your devotion, Lord Shri Rama embraced you warmly. The Lord of the Raghu dynasty praised you abundantly and declared, "O Hanuman, you are as dear to me as my own beloved brother, Bharata."

---

#### ॥ Chaupai 13 - 14 ॥
Sahas badan tumharo yash gave। Us kahi Shripati kanth lagave॥
Sanakadik Brahmadi Muneesa। Narad Sarad sahit Aheesa॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning (Line-by-Line):**
* **Sahas badan tumharo yash gave** = "The thousand-headed serpent, Sheshanaga (sahas badan), sings your glory (tumharo yash gave)."
* **Us kahi Shripati kanth lagave** = Saying this (us kahi), the Lord of Lakshmi / Rama (Shripati) embraced you again (kanth lagave).
* **Sanakadik Brahmadi Muneesa** = Sages like Sanaka, Gods like Brahma, and great saints (Muneesa).
* **Narad Sarad sahit Aheesa** = Along with Sage Narada, Goddess Saraswati (Sarad), and Lord Sheshanaga (Aheesa). (Implies: they all sing your glory).

**Translation:**
Saying, "Even the thousand-headed serpent Sheshanaga eternally sings your glorious deeds," Lord Shri Rama affectionately embraced you again. The great sages like Sanaka, creator Lord Brahma, supreme saints, divine sage Narada, Goddess Saraswati, and the king of serpents—all continuously chant your praises.

---

#### ॥ Chaupai 15 - 16 ॥
Yam Kuber Digpal Jahan te। Kavi kovid kahi sake kahan te॥
Tum upkar Sugreevahin keenha। Ram milaya rajpad deenha॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning (Line-by-Line):**
* **Yam Kuber Digpal Jahan te** = Where even the God of Death (Yam), God of Wealth (Kuber), and the guardians of directions (Digpal) fail to describe you fully.
* **Kavi kovid kahi sake kahan te** = How then can mere poets (kavi) and scholars (kovid) ever find the words to describe your true glory?
* **Tum upkar Sugreevahin keenha** = You did a massive favor (upkar keenha) to the monkey king Sugriva (Sugreevahin).
* **Ram milaya rajpad deenha** = By uniting him with Lord Rama (Ram milaya), which led to him regaining his kingdom (rajpad deenha).

**Translation:**
When even the God of Death (Yama), the God of Wealth (Kubera), and the celestial guardians of the directions cannot fully articulate your boundless glory, how can ordinary poets and scholars ever hope to describe it? You did a supreme favor to Sugriva; you brought him to Lord Rama, through whose grace Sugriva regained his lost throne and kingdom.

---

#### ॥ Chaupai 17 - 18 ॥
Tumharo mantra Vibhishan mana। Lankeshwar Bhaye Sub jag jana॥
Yug sahastra yojan par Bhanu। Leelyo tahi madhur phal janu॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning (Line-by-Line):**
* **Tumharo mantra Vibhishan mana** = Vibhishana accepted and followed your advice/counsel (tumharo mantra mana).
* **Lankeshwar Bhaye Sub jag jana** = As a result, he became the King of Lanka (Lankeshwar bhaye), a fact known to the whole world (sub jag jana).
* **Yug sahastra yojan par Bhanu** = The Sun (Bhanu) is located thousands of Yojanas away (yug sahastra yojan par).
* **Leelyo tahi madhur phal janu** = Yet you swallowed it (leelyo tahi), thinking it was just a sweet fruit (madhur phal janu).

**Translation:**
Vibhishana heeded your wise counsel, and as a result, he was crowned the King of Lanka; this truth is known to the entire world. The Sun, which is situated millions of miles away in the cosmos, was easily swallowed by you as a child, mistaking it for a sweet, delicious fruit.

---

#### ॥ Chaupai 19 - 20 ॥
Prabhu mudrika meli mukh mahee। Jaladhi langhi gaye achraj nahee॥
Durgam kaj jagat ke jete। Sugam anugraha tumhare tete॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning (Line-by-Line):**
* **Prabhu mudrika meli mukh mahee** = Keeping the Lord's ring (Prabhu mudrika) securely in your mouth (meli mukh mahee).
* **Jaladhi langhi gaye achraj nahee** = You leaped across the vast ocean (jaladhi langhi gaye), there is no surprise in this (achraj nahee).
* **Durgam kaj jagat ke jete** = Whatever extremely difficult or impossible tasks (durgam kaj) exist in this world (jagat ke jete).
* **Sugam anugraha tumhare tete** = They all become incredibly easy (sugam) by your divine grace (anugraha tumhare tete).

**Translation:**
Placing Lord Rama's signet ring in your mouth, you leaped across the colossal, boundless ocean; for you, this was no astonishing feat at all. Whatever difficult, treacherous, or seemingly impossible tasks exist in this universe, they are all rendered effortlessly simple by your divine grace and blessings.

---

#### ॥ Chaupai 21 - 22 ॥
Ram duare tum rakhvare। Hoat na agya binu paisare॥
Sab sukh lahai tumhari sarna। Tum rakshak kahu ko darna॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning (Line-by-Line):**
* **Ram duare tum rakhvare** = You are the gatekeeper and guardian (rakhvare) at the door of Lord Rama's court (Ram duare).
* **Hoat na agya binu paisare** = No one can enter (paisare) without your permission (agya binu).
* **Sab sukh lahai tumhari sarna** = Whoever takes refuge in you (tumhari sarna) attains all the joys and comforts of life (sab sukh lahai).
* **Tum rakshak kahu ko darna** = When you are the protector (tum rakshak), why should anyone fear anything (kahu ko darna)?

**Translation:**
You are the vigilant guardian and sentinel at the divine doorway of Lord Rama; no one can reach the Lord without your explicit permission. Whoever seeks refuge at your feet receives all the happiness and bliss of the world. When you yourself stand as the protector, your devotee has absolutely no reason to fear anything in the universe.

---

#### ॥ Chaupai 23 - 24 ॥
Aapan tej samharo aapai। Teenon lok hank te kanpai॥
Bhoot pisach Nikat nahin aavai। Mahavir jab naam sunavai॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning (Line-by-Line):**
* **Aapan tej samharo aapai** = Only you alone (aapai) can control and manage your immense energy and power (aapan tej samharo).
* **Teenon lok hank te kanpai** = All the three worlds tremble (kanpai) at your mighty roar (hank te).
* **Bhoot pisach Nikat nahin aavai** = Ghosts (bhoot), demons, and evil spirits (pisach) do not even come close (nikat nahin aavai).
* **Mahavir jab naam sunavai** = Whenever your holy name, O Great Hero (Mahavir), is chanted or heard (jab naam sunavai).

**Translation:**
Your boundless energy, velocity, and majestic power can only be controlled by you alone; your single mighty roar makes all the three worlds tremble in awe. O Great Hero! Ghosts, evil spirits, and negative energies can never come anywhere near a person who constantly chants or listens to your holy name.

---

#### ॥ Chaupai 25 - 26 ॥
Nase rog harai sab peera। Japat nirantar Hanumant beera॥
Sankat se Hanuman chhudavai। Man Kram Vachan dhyan jo lavai॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning (Line-by-Line):**
* **Nase rog harai sab peera** = All diseases are destroyed (nase rog) and all physical/mental pains vanish (harai sab peera).
* **Japat nirantar Hanumant beera** = For the one who constantly chants the name (japat nirantar) of the brave Hanuman (Hanumant beera).
* **Sankat se Hanuman chhudavai** = Hanuman rescues from all crises and troubles (sankat se chhudavai).
* **Man Kram Vachan dhyan jo lavai** = Those who meditate upon him (dhyan jo lavai) with true focus in thought (man), action (kram), and word (vachan).

**Translation:**
O Brave Hanuman! Constant recitation and chanting of your divine name destroys all bodily diseases and eradicates all forms of pain and suffering. Whoever meditates upon Lord Hanuman with pure sincerity in their mind, actions, and speech, is effortlessly liberated from all the crises and formidable challenges of life.

---

#### ॥ Chaupai 27 - 28 ॥
Sab par Ram tapasvee raja। Tin ke kaj sakal Tum saja॥
Aur manorath jo koi lavai। Soi amit jeevan phal pavai॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning (Line-by-Line):**
* **Sab par Ram tapasvee raja** = Lord Rama is the ascetic king (tapasvee raja) who reigns supreme over everyone (sab par).
* **Tin ke kaj sakal Tum saja** = Yet, even His incredibly difficult tasks (tin ke kaj sakal) were perfectly accomplished by you (Tum saja).
* **Aur manorath jo koi lavai** = Furthermore, whoever brings any pure desire or wish to you (aur manorath jo koi lavai).
* **Soi amit jeevan phal pavai** = They receive infinite, boundless fruits and rewards in this life (soi amit jeevan phal pavai).

**Translation:**
Lord Rama is the supreme ascetic King, the ruler of the entire universe; yet, it was you who beautifully accomplished all His divine missions. Aside from that, any devotee who comes to you with a sincere wish or desire in their heart, receives infinite and everlasting rewards in their life by your grace.

---

#### ॥ Chaupai 29 - 30 ॥
Charon Yug partap tumhara। Hai persiddh jagat ujiyara॥
Sadhu Sant ke tum Rakhware। Asur nikandan Ram dulare॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning (Line-by-Line):**
* **Charon Yug partap tumhara** = Your glory and majesty (partap tumhara) span across all the four ages/Yugas (charon yug).
* **Hai persiddh jagat ujiyara** = It is famously known (hai persiddh) and illuminates the entire universe (jagat ujiyara).
* **Sadhu Sant ke tum Rakhware** = You are the eternal protector (rakhware) of the ascetics, good people, and saints (sadhu sant).
* **Asur nikandan Ram dulare** = You are the destroyer of demons/evil (asur nikandan) and the beloved of Lord Rama (Ram dulare).

**Translation:**
Your divine glory and immense majesty are present throughout all four Yugas (Satya, Treta, Dvapara, and Kali Yuga), and your fame radiates brightly across the entire universe. You are the ultimate guardian and protector of saints and righteous people. You are the annihilator of demons and evil forces, and you are exceedingly dear to Lord Rama.

---

#### ॥ Chaupai 31 - 32 ॥
Ashta siddhi nav nidhi ke data। As var deen Janki mata॥
Ram rasayan tumhare pasa। Sada raho Raghupati ke dasa॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning (Line-by-Line):**
* **Ashta siddhi nav nidhi ke data** = You are the bestower (data) of the eight supernatural powers (ashta siddhi) and the nine divine treasures (nav nidhi).
* **As var deen Janki mata** = Mother Sita (Janki mata) granted you this special boon (as var deen).
* **Ram rasayan tumhare pasa** = You possess the supreme elixir/medicine of Lord Rama's devotion (Ram rasayan tumhare pasa).
* **Sada raho Raghupati ke dasa** = And you eternally remain (sada raho) the humble servant of Lord Shri Rama (Raghupati ke dasa).

**Translation:**
Mother Sita has blessed you with a divine boon, empowering you to grant anyone the eight supernatural powers (Siddhis) and the nine types of divine wealth (Nidhis). You hold the ultimate elixir of devotion to Lord Rama, and you forever remain the most devoted, humble servant of the Lord of the Raghus.

---

#### ॥ Chaupai 33 - 34 ॥
Tumhare bhajan Ram ko pavai। Janam janam ke dukh bisravai॥
Antakaal Raghubar pur jayee। Jahan janam Hari-Bhakt Kahayee॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning (Line-by-Line):**
* **Tumhare bhajan Ram ko pavai** = By singing your praises and chanting your name (tumhare bhajan), one attains Lord Rama Himself (Ram ko pavai).
* **Janam janam ke dukh bisravai** = And the accumulated sorrows of countless past lifetimes are forgotten/destroyed (janam janam ke dukh bisravai).
* **Antakaal Raghubar pur jayee** = At the end of life (antakaal), such a devotee goes directly to the supreme abode of Lord Rama (Raghubar pur jayee).
* **Jahan janam Hari-Bhakt Kahayee** = And even if reborn (jahan janam), they are known only as a devoted servant of God (Hari-Bhakt kahayee).

**Translation:**
By singing your praises and dedicating oneself to you, a devotee directly attains the grace of Lord Rama, erasing the pains and sorrows accumulated over countless lifetimes. At the time of death, such a devoted soul departs to the supreme heavenly abode of Lord Rama (Vaikuntha/Saket Loka), and if they ever take birth again on earth, they are born strictly as a pure devotee of the Lord.

---

#### ॥ Chaupai 35 - 36 ॥
Aur Devta Chitt na dharayi। Hanumant se hi sarve sukh karayi॥
Sankat kate mite sab peera। Jo sumirai Hanumant Balbeera॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning (Line-by-Line):**
* **Aur Devta Chitt na dharayi** = Even if one does not keep any other deity in their mind/heart (aur devta chitt na dharayi).
* **Hanumant se hi sarve sukh karayi** = Devotion and service to Lord Hanuman alone yields all happiness (sarve sukh karayi).
* **Sankat kate mite sab peera** = All crises are averted (sankat kate) and all pain is eradicated (mite sab peera).
* **Jo sumirai Hanumant Balbeera** = For the one who remembers and meditates upon the mighty and brave Hanuman (jo sumirai Hanumant Balbeera).

**Translation:**
Even if a devotee does not meditate upon or worship any other deity, serving Lord Hanuman alone is entirely sufficient to grant every possible joy and comfort in life. All worldly troubles are resolved, and all pains are entirely eradicated for the one who sincerely remembers the mighty hero, Lord Hanuman.

---

#### ॥ Chaupai 37 - 38 ॥
Jai Jai Jai Hanuman Gosain। Kripa Karahu Gurudev ki nyain॥
Jo sat bar path kare koi। Chhutahi bandi maha sukh hoi॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning (Line-by-Line):**
* **Jai Jai Jai Hanuman Gosain** = Victory, victory, victory to you, O Lord Hanuman, Master of the Senses (Gosain)!
* **Kripa Karahu Gurudev ki nyain** = Please bestow your grace upon me (kripa karahu) just like a supreme Guru (Gurudev ki nyain).
* **Jo sat bar path kare koi** = Whoever recites this Chalisa one hundred times (jo sat bar path kare koi).
* **Chhutahi bandi maha sukh hoi** = Will be completely freed from all worldly bondages (chhutahi bandi) and attain great bliss (maha sukh hoi).

**Translation:**
Victory, victory, victory to you, O Lord Hanuman, the master of the senses! Please shower your divine blessings and grace upon me just as a supreme Guru blesses a disciple. Whoever recites this Hanuman Chalisa a hundred times with deep faith will be liberated from all worldly chains and physical bondages, ultimately attaining supreme cosmic bliss.

---

#### ॥ Chaupai 39 - 40 ॥
Jo yah padhe Hanuman Chalisa। Hoye siddhi sakhi Gaureesa॥
Tulsidas sada hari chera। Keejai Nath Hriday mahn dera॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning (Line-by-Line):**
* **Jo yah padhe Hanuman Chalisa** = Whoever recites this Hanuman Chalisa with devotion.
* **Hoye siddhi sakhi Gaureesa** = Will definitely attain perfection and success (hoye siddhi); Lord Shiva (Gaureesa - Lord of Gauri) Himself is the witness (sakhi) to this.
* **Tulsidas sada hari chera** = The poet Tulsidas says, "I am eternally the humble servant of Lord Hari/Rama (sada hari chera)."
* **Keejai Nath Hriday mahn dera** = "Therefore, O Lord (Nath), please make your permanent residence (dera) inside my heart (hriday mahn)."

**Translation:**
Whoever recites this Hanuman Chalisa with true devotion will certainly attain spiritual perfection and success; Lord Shiva Himself stands as a witness to this truth. The poet Goswami Tulsidas declares, "I am forever the humble servant of Lord Shri Rama. Therefore, O Supreme Lord Hanuman, please make my heart your permanent abode."

---

#### ॥ Final Doha ॥
Pavan Tanay Sankat Haran, Mangal Murati Roop।
Ram Lakhan Sita Sahit, Hriday Basahu Sur Bhoop॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning (Line-by-Line):**
* **Pavan Tanay Sankat Haran** = O Son of the Wind God (Pavan Tanay), the ultimate destroyer of all crises and sorrows (sankat haran).
* **Mangal Murati Roop** = You are the living embodiment of all that is auspicious and blessed (mangal murati roop).
* **Ram Lakhan Sita Sahit** = Along with Lord Rama, Lakshmana, and Mother Sita.
* **Hriday Basahu Sur Bhoop** = O King of the Gods (Sur Bhoop), please reside forever in my heart (hriday basahu).

**Translation:**
O Son of the Wind God, the vanquisher of all sorrows and the living embodiment of pure auspiciousness! O King of Deities, please reside eternally within my heart, alongside Lord Shri Rama, Lord Lakshmana, and Mother Sita.

### 3. Benefits of Recitation (Phalashruti)

The Hanuman Chalisa is an awakened (Jagrat) mantra-kavya. As Goswami Tulsidas wrote, *"Jo sat bar path kare koi, Chhutahi bandi maha sukh hoi"* — emphasizing its power to grant absolute liberation and joy.

* **Eradication of Fear & Crises:** Daily recitation dispels unknown fears, negative energies, planetary afflictions, and psychological stress.
* **Mental & Physical Fortitude:** The hymn actively cultivates immense patience, emotional resilience, positive energy, and sharp decision-making skills.
* **Healing & Health:** As stated in the verse *"Nase rog harai sab peera"*, chanting it with unwavering faith provides immense relief from chronic illnesses and physical/mental anguish.
* **Grace of Lord Rama:** Because Hanuman is the dearest servant of Lord Rama, pleasing Lord Hanuman automatically unlocks the highest grace, protection, and blessings of the Supreme Lord Shri Rama.
