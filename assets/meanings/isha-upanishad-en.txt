## ॥ Ishavasya Upanishad ॥ (Isha Upanishad)
**Complete Text, Word-by-Word Meaning, Translation & Significance**

---

### 1. Introduction & Spiritual Significance

The 'Ishavasya Upanishad' (or Isha Upanishad) constitutes the 40th and final chapter of the Shukla Yajurveda (Kanva Shakha). It is considered the foremost among all the principal Upanishads because it is the only one that forms a direct part of a Veda Samhita. It consists of exactly 18 mantras.

The Upanishad derives its name from its opening word 'Ishavasyam', meaning "Enveloped by the Lord." It delivers a profound message on the harmonization of knowledge (Vidya) and action (Avidya), the practice of desireless action (Nishkama Karma), the omnipresence of the Supreme Being, and finally, a deeply moving prayer at the time of death seeking the ultimate Truth. Mahatma Gandhi famously remarked that if all the Upanishads and scriptures were lost to ashes, and only the first verse of the Isha Upanishad remained in the memory of Hindus, Hinduism would live on forever.

* **Veda:** Shukla Yajurveda (40th Chapter)
* **Core Subject:** The omnipresence of God, detached action (Karma Yoga), synthesizing worldly duties with spiritual pursuit, and liberation (Moksha).

---

### 2. Sanskrit Verses, Word-by-Word Meaning & English Translation

#### ॥ Mantra 1 ॥
ईशावास्यमिदँ सर्वं यत्किञ्च जगत्यां जगत् ।
तेन त्यक्तेन भुञ्जीथा मा गृधः कस्यस्विद्धनम् ॥ १॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Īśāvāsyamidaṃ sarvaṃ** = All this is to be pervaded/enveloped by the Lord (*Isha*)
* **Yatkiñca jagatyāṃ jagat** = Whatever is moving/animate or inanimate in this changing world
* **Tena tyaktena bhuñjīthā** = Therefore, enjoy (this world) with a spirit of renunciation/detachment
* **Mā gṛdhaḥ kasyasviddhanam** = Do not covet or be greedy, for whose is the wealth? (It belongs to no one).

**Translation:**
Everything animate or inanimate that exists within this changing universe is completely enveloped by the Lord. Therefore, enjoy what the world offers with a profound sense of detachment and renunciation. Do not covet anyone's wealth, for whose wealth is it really? (Everything ultimately belongs to the Supreme).

---

#### ॥ Mantra 2 ॥
कुर्वन्नेवेह कर्माणि जिजीविषेच्छतँ समाः ।
एवं त्वयि नान्यथेतोऽस्ति न कर्म लिप्यते नरे ॥ २॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Kurvanneveha karmāṇi** = Only by performing one's duties (Karma) in this world
* **Jijīviṣecchataṃ samāḥ** = One should desire to live for a hundred years
* **Evaṃ tvayi nānyatheto'sti** = Thus, for you, there is no other way than this
* **Na karma lipyate nare** = (By doing so) action does not cling to (or bind) the human being.

**Translation:**
One should desire to live a full life of a hundred years in this world only while continuously performing one's prescribed duties. There is no other path for you but this. When actions are performed with such detachment (without desiring the fruits), the bondage of Karma does not cling to a person.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 3 ॥
असुर्या नाम ते लोका अन्धेन तमसावृताः ।
ताँस्ते प्रेत्याभिगच्छन्ति ये के चात्महनो जनाः ॥ ३॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Asuryā nāma te lokā** = Those worlds are known as demoniac (*Asurya* - sunless/godless)
* **Andhena tamasāvṛtāḥ** = They are enveloped in blinding darkness/ignorance
* **Tāṃste pretyābhigacchanti** = After departing (death), they go to those very worlds
* **Ye ke cātmahano janāḥ** = Those people who are slayers of the Self (ignorant of the Atman).

**Translation:**
Those worlds, completely enveloped in blinding darkness and ignorance, are known as the demoniac (sunless) realms. Those people who slay their own Self (i.e., those who remain ignorant of the Atman and lose themselves in material illusion) are destined to go to those dark realms after death.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 4 ॥
अनेजदेकं मनसो जवीयो नैनद्देवा आप्नुवन्पूर्वमर्षत् ।
तद्धावतोऽन्यानत्येति तिष्ठत्तस्मिन्नपो मातरिश्वा दधाति ॥ ४॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Anejadekaṃ manaso javīyo** = The One (Atman) is unmoving (*anejat*), yet faster than the mind (*javiyas*)
* **Nainaddevā āpnuvan-pūrvamarṣat** = The gods (senses) cannot reach it, for it has run ahead of them
* **Taddhāvato'nyānatyeti tiṣṭhat-** = Remaining stationary, it outruns all others who are running
* **Tasminnapo mātariśvā dadhāti** = By its existence, Matarishva (the cosmic Life-Force/Air) sustains all activities (waters/actions).

**Translation:**
The Supreme Self is One and unmoving, yet it is faster than the mind. The senses (gods) can never reach it, because it is always already there ahead of them. Though remaining perfectly still, it outruns everything else that runs. By its sheer presence, the cosmic life-force (Prana/Air) is able to sustain all activities of the universe.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 5 ॥
तदेजति तन्नैजति तद्दूरे तद्वन्तिके ।
तदन्तरस्य सर्वस्य तदु सर्वस्यास्य बाह्यतः ॥ ५॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Tadejati tannaijati** = It moves, and It moves not
* **Taddūre tadvantike** = It is very far, and It is also very near
* **Tadantarasya sarvasya** = It is inside all this (the universe)
* **Tadu sarvasyāsya bāhyataḥ** = And It is also outside all this.

**Translation:**
That Supreme Truth moves, and yet It does not move. It is infinitely far away, and yet It is exceedingly near. It is seated deep inside all that exists, and It also entirely surrounds everything from the outside.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 6 ॥
यस्तु सर्वाणि भूतान्यात्मन्येवानुपश्यति ।
सर्वभूतेषु चात्मानं ततो न विजुगुप्सते ॥ ६॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Yastu sarvāṇi bhūtāni-** = He who sees all beings
* **Ātmanyevānupaśyati** = Within his own Self (Atman)
* **Sarvabhūteṣu cātmānaṃ** = And his own Self within all beings
* **Tato na vijugupsate** = Thereafter, he feels no hatred or revulsion towards anyone.

**Translation:**
He who perceives all beings as existing within his own Self, and his own Self residing within all beings, thereafter feels no hatred, revulsion, or enmity towards anyone.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 7 ॥
यस्मिन्सर्वाणि भूतान्यात्मैवाभूद्विजानतः ।
तत्र को मोहः कः शोक एकत्वमनुपश्यतः ॥ ७॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Yasminsarvāṇi bhūtāni-** = In the state where all beings
* **Ātmaivābhūd-vijānataḥ** = Have become the very Self of the knower
* **Tatra ko mohaḥ kaḥ śoka** = Then what delusion and what sorrow can there be?
* **Ekatvamanupaśyataḥ** = For the one who perceives this absolute oneness everywhere.

**Translation:**
When a wise person realizes that all beings have become his very own Self, then what delusion or sorrow can possibly remain for that seer who perceives absolute oneness everywhere?

---

#### ॥ Mantra 8 ॥
स पर्यगाच्छुक्रमकायमव्रणमस्नाविरँ शुद्धमपापविद्धम् ।
कविर्मनीषी परिभूः स्वयम्भूर्याथातथ्यतोऽर्थान्व्यदधाच्छाश्वतीभ्यः समाभ्यः ॥ ८॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Sa paryagāt-śukram-akāyam-avraṇam-asnāviraṃ** = He (the Atman) is all-pervading, radiant, bodiless, scatheless, without muscles/veins
* **Śuddhamapāpaviddham** = Pure, and completely untouched by sin or evil
* **Kavirmanīṣī paribhūḥ svayambhūr-** = He is the all-seeing seer (Kavi), the ruler of the mind, transcendent, and self-existent
* **Yāthātathyato'rthānvyadadhācchāśvatībhyaḥ samābhyaḥ** = He has duly and perfectly assigned the respective duties/objects to all eternal years (creators/beings).

**Translation:**
That Supreme Self is all-pervading, radiant, bodiless, without wounds or veins, supremely pure, and absolutely untouched by sin. He is the omniscient seer, the sovereign of the mind, the transcendent, and the self-existent One. From beginningless time, He has perfectly assigned the roles, duties, and objects to all beings according to their nature.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 9 ॥
अन्धं तमः प्रविशन्ति येऽविद्यामुपासते ।
ततो भूय इव ते तमो य उ विद्यायाँ रताः ॥ ९॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Andhaṃ tamaḥ praviśanti** = They enter into blinding darkness
* **Ye'vidyāmupāsate** = Those who worship Avidya (ignorance / mere rituals / selfish actions)
* **Tato bhūya iva te tamo** = And into even greater darkness than that, they fall
* **Ya u vidyāyāṃ ratāḥ** = Those who are engrossed solely in Vidya (theoretical knowledge / false ego).

**Translation:**
Those who are devoted entirely to 'Avidya' (ignorant, selfish worldly actions and mere rituals) enter into blinding darkness. But those who are engrossed solely in 'Vidya' (theoretical knowledge without practical action, leading to spiritual arrogance) fall into a darkness that is seemingly even greater.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 10 ॥
अन्यदेवाहुर्विद्ययाऽन्यदाहुरविद्यया ।
इति शुश्रुम धीराणां ये नस्तद्विचचक्षिरे ॥ १०॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Anyadevāhurvidyayā-** = One result is obtained from Vidya (knowledge), they say
* **Anyadāhuravidyayā** = And a completely different result is obtained from Avidya (action)
* **Iti śuśruma dhīrāṇāṃ** = Thus we have heard from the wise ones
* **Ye nastadvicacakṣire** = Who explained that truth to us clearly.

**Translation:**
One specific result is achieved through Vidya (true knowledge), and a completely different result is achieved through Avidya (selfless action)—thus we have heard from the wise sages who have clearly explained these truths to us.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 11 ॥
विद्यां चाविद्यां च यस्तद्वेदोभयँ सह ।
अविद्यया मृत्युं तीर्त्वा विद्ययामृतमश्नुते ॥ ११॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Vidyāṃ cāvidyāṃ ca** = Both Vidya (knowledge) and Avidya (action)
* **Yastadvedobhayaṃ saha** = He who knows and harmonizes both of them together
* **Avidyayā mṛtyuṃ tīrtvā** = By means of Avidya (selfless action) he crosses over death
* **Vidyayāmṛtamaśnute** = And by means of Vidya (knowledge) he attains immortality.

**Translation:**
He who understands the true balance of both 'Vidya' (knowledge of the Self) and 'Avidya' (the performance of selfless worldly duties) together—crosses over death (the sorrows of the world) through Avidya, and attains eternal immortality (Moksha) through Vidya.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 12 ॥
अन्धं तमः प्रविशन्ति येऽसम्भूतिमुपासते ।
ततो भूय इव ते तमो य उ सम्भूत्याँ रताः ॥ १२॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Andhaṃ tamaḥ praviśanti ye'sambhūtimupāsate** = They enter into blinding darkness who worship Asambhuti (the unmanifest / the destructible matter)
* **Tato bhūya iva te tamo** = And into an even greater darkness than that, they fall
* **Ya u sambhūtyāṃ ratāḥ** = Those who are absorbed only in Sambhuti (the manifest / absolute unmanifest).
*(Note: Sambhuti and Asambhuti interpretations vary across commentators; generally it implies the balance of unmanifest Brahman and manifest creation).*

**Translation:**
Those who worship only the destructible, manifest world (*Asambhuti*) enter into blinding darkness. However, those who remain absorbed solely in the unmanifest, formless reality (*Sambhuti*) while neglecting their worldly duties, fall into a darkness that is seemingly even deeper.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 13 ॥
अन्यदेवाहुः सम्भवादन्यदाहुरसम्भवात् ।
इति शुश्रुम धीराणां ये नस्तद्विचचक्षिरे ॥ १३॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Anyadevāhuḥ sambhavād-** = One result is obtained from the worship of the manifest (Sambhava)
* **Anyadāhurasambhavāt** = And a different result is obtained from the unmanifest (Asambhava)
* **Iti śuśruma dhīrāṇāṃ ye nastadvicacakṣire** = Thus we have heard from the wise seers who explained these truths to us.

**Translation:**
A distinct result is achieved through the worship of the manifest world (Sambhava), and a completely different result is obtained through the worship of the unmanifest reality (Asambhava)—thus we have heard from the wise sages who have expounded these matters to us.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 14 ॥
सम्भूतिं च विनाशं च यस्तद्वेदोभयँ सह ।
विनाशेन मृत्युं तीर्त्वा सम्भूत्यामृतमश्नुते ॥ १४॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Sambhūtiṃ ca vināśaṃ ca** = The Unmanifest reality (Sambhuti) and the destructible, manifest world (Vinasha)
* **Yastadvedobhayaṃ saha** = He who knows and integrates both of them together
* **Vināśena mṛtyuṃ tīrtvā** = By means of the manifest world (performing duties) he crosses over death
* **Sambhūtyāmṛtamaśnute** = And through the Unmanifest reality (knowledge of the Absolute) he attains immortality.

**Translation:**
He who understands and perfectly balances both 'Sambhuti' (the eternal unmanifest Brahman) and 'Vinasha' (the transient manifest world) together—crosses over death by performing his duties in the manifest world, and attains immortality through the knowledge of the eternal Unmanifest.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 15 ॥
हिरण्मयेन पात्रेण सत्यस्यापिहितं मुखम् ।
तत्त्वं पूषन्नपावृणु सत्यधर्माय दृष्टये ॥ १५॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Hiraṇmayena pātreṇa** = By a golden (dazzling/material) lid or vessel
* **Satyasyāpihitaṃ mukham** = The face of the Ultimate Truth is covered
* **Tattvaṃ Pūṣannapāvṛṇu** = O Pushan (Sun/Sustainer)! Please remove that covering
* **Satyadharmāya dṛṣṭaye** = So that I, who am dedicated to the truth (Satya-dharma), may see it.

**Translation:**
(A prayer at the time of death): The face of the Ultimate Truth is hidden by a dazzling golden lid (the glamour of the material world). O Pushan (the Sustainer/Sun God), please remove that veil, so that I, who have faithfully practiced the religion of Truth, may behold the Supreme Reality.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 16 ॥
पूषन्नेकर्षे यम सूर्य प्राजापत्य व्यूह रश्मीन् समूह तेजः ।
यत्ते रूपं कल्याणतमं तत्ते पश्यामि योऽसावसौ पुरुषः सोऽहमस्मि ॥ १६॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Pūṣannekarṣe Yama Sūrya Prājāpatya** = O Pushan (Sustainer), O solitary wanderer, O Controller (Yama), O Sun, O son of Prajapati!
* **Vyūha raśmīn samūha tejaḥ** = Gather back Your blinding rays and withdraw Your burning brilliance
* **Yatte rūpaṃ kalyāṇatamaṃ tatte paśyāmi** = So that I may behold Your most auspicious and beautiful form
* **Yo'sāvasau puruṣaḥ so'hamasmi** = That Supreme Person (Purusha) who dwells out there (in the sun), I am He (So'ham asmi).

**Translation:**
O Sustainer, O solitary traveler, O Controller of all, O Sun God, O son of Prajapati! Please withdraw your blinding rays and gather up your scorching brilliance, so that I may behold your most auspicious and glorious form. (Upon seeing it, the seeker realizes:) That Supreme Being dwelling there in the solar orb—I am He! ("So'ham asmi").

---

#### ॥ Mantra 17 ॥
वायुरनिलममृतमथेदं भस्मान्तँ शरीरम् ।
ॐ क्रतो स्मर कृतँ स्मर क्रतो स्मर कृतँ स्मर ॥ १७॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Vāyuranilamamṛtamathedaṃ-** = May this vital breath (Vayu) now merge into the immortal, all-pervading Air (Anila)
* **Bhasmāntaṃ śarīram** = And let this physical body be reduced to ashes
* **Om Krato smara kṛtaṃ smara** = O OM! O Mind/Will (Krato)! Remember the Lord, remember your deeds (kritam)
* **Krato smara kṛtaṃ smara** = O Mind! Remember, remember what has been done.

**Translation:**
(The final prayer): May my vital breath now merge into the immortal, universal cosmic life-force, and may this physical body be reduced to ashes. O OM! O my mind (will), remember the Supreme Lord! Remember all the good deeds you have done! O mind, remember! Remember your actions.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 18 ॥
अग्ने नय सुपथा राये अस्मान्विश्वानि देव वयुनानि विद्वान् ।
युयोध्यस्मज्जुहुराणमेनो भूयिष्ठां ते नमउक्तिं विधेम ॥ १८॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Agne naya supathā rāye asmān-** = O Agni (Lord of Fire)! Lead us by the good/auspicious path (supatha) to spiritual wealth/liberation (raye)
* **Viśvāni deva vayunāni vidvān** = O God, You are the knower of all our deeds and thoughts
* **Yuyodhyasmajjuhurāṇameno** = Destroy and remove from us our crooked/deceitful sins
* **Bhūyiṣṭhāṃ te namauktiṃ vidhema** = We offer You our words of salutation and reverence again and again.

**Translation:**
O Agni! You know all our actions and thoughts. Lead us by the most auspicious path (the northern path / Uttarayana) toward the ultimate spiritual wealth (liberation). Eradicate from us all our crooked and deceitful sins. We bow down to You and offer our words of salutation again and again.

---

### 3. Benefits of Recitation (Phalashruti)

The 'Ishavasya Upanishad' teaches the profound art of living. Its regular study instills a deep balance between worldly duties (Karma) and spiritual knowledge (Jnana). By realizing that the entire universe is enveloped by the Lord, the seeker drops all greed and learns to enjoy the world through renunciation. Mantras 15 to 18 are powerful verses that remove the fear of death, affirming the soul's ultimate identity with the Supreme (*So'ham asmi*). Chanting and meditating upon this Upanishad destroys the darkness of ignorance and effortlessly guides the soul to eternal liberation (*Moksha*).
