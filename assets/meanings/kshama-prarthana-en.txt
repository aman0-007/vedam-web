## Kshama Prarthana (Prayer for Forgiveness - Sri Saishwara)
**Complete Text, Word-by-Word Meaning, Translation & Significance**

---

### 1. Introduction & Spiritual Significance

The 'Kshama Prarthana' (Prayer for Forgiveness) is a deeply significant hymn chanted at the conclusion of any puja, stotra recitation, mantra japa, or spiritual ritual. This particular version is dedicated to Lord Narayana and 'Sri Saishwara' (the Supreme Lord embodied as Sai Baba).

In the Vedic and Puranic traditions, it is acknowledged that human beings, out of ignorance or lack of focus, may commit errors in the pronunciation, meter, or procedural aspects of a ritual. Through this prayer, the seeker approaches the Lord with complete surrender and humility, seeking forgiveness for all known and unknown mistakes, and acknowledging that the Lord's compassion is their ultimate refuge.

* **Presiding Deity:** Lord Narayana and Sri Saishwara (The Supreme Lord)
* **Purpose (Viniyoga):** Chanted at the end of prayers or rituals to seek forgiveness for any flaws, omissions, or mispronunciations, and to offer the merits of the worship entirely to the Divine.

---

### 2. Sanskrit Verses, Word-by-Word Meaning & English Translation

#### ॥ Verse 1 ॥
यदक्षरपदभ्रष्टं मात्राहीनं तु यद्भवेत् ।
तत्सर्वं क्षम्यतां देव नारायण नमोऽस्तु ते ॥ १॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Yad-akṣara-pada-bhraṣṭaṃ** = Whatever letter (*akshara*) or word (*pada*) has been slipped/omitted (*bhrashtam*)
* **Mātrāhīnaṃ tu yadbhavet** = Or whatever has been devoid (*hīnam*) of the proper phonetic measure/meter (*mātrā*)
* **Tat-sarvaṃ kṣamyatāṃ deva** = O Lord (*Deva*)! Please forgive (*kṣamyatām*) all of that (*tat-sarvam*)
* **Nārāyaṇa namo'stu te** = O Lord Narayana! My salutations be unto You.

**Translation:**
O Lord! Whatever letter or word I may have omitted or mispronounced, or whatever phonetic measure or meter I may have missed during this chanting—please forgive all of my shortcomings. O Lord Narayana! My repeated salutations unto You.

---

#### ॥ Verse 2 ॥
विसर्गबिन्दुमात्राणि पदपादाक्षराणि च ।
न्यूनानि चातिरिक्तानि क्षमस्व साईश्वर ॥ २॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Visarga-bindu-mātrāṇi** = Regarding the aspirate sounds (*visarga*), nasal sounds (*bindu/anusvara*), and vowel measurements (*mātrā*)
* **Pada-pādākṣarāṇi ca** = And regarding the words (*pada*), the quarters of the verses (*pāda*), and the letters (*akṣara*)
* **Nyūnāni cātiriktāni** = Whatever has been deficient/lacking (*nyūna*) or excessive/added (*atirikta*)
* **Kṣamasva Sāīśvara** = Please forgive me, O Saishwara!

**Translation:**
Whatever mistakes I may have made regarding the pronunciation of visargas, bindus (nasal sounds), vowel lengths, words, phrasing of the verses, or individual letters; whatever I may have left out or mistakenly added—please forgive me, O Lord Saishwara!

---

#### ॥ Verse 3 ॥
अपराधसहस्राणि क्रियन्तेऽहर्निशं मया ।
दासोऽयमिति मां मत्वा क्षमस्व साईश्वर ॥ ३॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Aparādha-sahasrāṇi** = Thousands of offenses/faults
* **Kriyante'harniśaṃ mayā** = Are committed by me day and night (*aharniśam*)
* **Dāso'yam-iti māṃ matvā** = Considering me to be Your humble servant (*dāsa*)
* **Kṣamasva Sāīśvara** = Please forgive me, O Saishwara!

**Translation:**
Thousands of faults and offenses are committed by me day and night, knowingly and unknowingly. O Lord Saishwara, considering me to be Your humble and ignorant servant, please forgive me.

---

#### ॥ Verse 4 ॥
अन्यथा शरणं नास्ति त्वमेव शरणं मम ।
तस्मात्कारुण्यभावेन रक्ष रक्ष साईश्वर ॥ ४॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Anyathā śaraṇaṃ nāsti** = There is no other refuge or shelter for me
* **Tvameva śaraṇaṃ mama** = You alone (*tvameva*) are my ultimate refuge (*śaraṇam*)
* **Tasmāt-kāruṇyabhāvena** = Therefore (*tasmāt*), out of Your boundless compassion (*kāruṇya-bhāvena*)
* **Rakṣa rakṣa Sāīśvara** = Protect me, protect me, O Saishwara!

**Translation:**
O Lord, I have no other refuge in this world; You alone are my ultimate sanctuary. Therefore, out of Your boundless compassion, please protect me, protect me, O Saishwara!

---

#### ॥ Dedication & Shanti Patha ॥
हरिः ॐ तत्सत् श्री साईश्वरार्पणमस्तु ॥
ॐ शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning & Translation:**
* **Hariḥ Om Tat Sat** = The Supreme Lord Hari (Om) alone is the Ultimate and Eternal Truth.
* **Śrī Sāīśvarārpaṇamastu** = May all this (my actions, prayers, and worship) be entirely dedicated and offered (*arpaṇam-astu*) at the lotus feet of Sri Saishwara.
* **Om Śāntiḥ Śāntiḥ Śāntiḥ** = Om. May there be absolute peace from physical, divine, and internal suffering.

---

### 3. Benefits of Recitation (Phalashruti)

In the Sanatana Dharma tradition, performing a Kshama Prarthana at the end of any worship is considered mandatory to complete the ritual. By chanting these verses, the seeker is absolved of the doshas (blemishes) incurred from unintentional mistakes in mantra pronunciation, procedural flaws, or lack of focus during the puja. Since the Divine Lord looks only at the devotion in the seeker's heart, this prayer helps dissolve the ego, cultivates total surrender, and ensures the seeker receives the Lord's complete grace and protection (*Abhaya*).
