## ॥ Lingashtakam ॥
**Complete Text, Word-by-Word Meaning, Translation & Significance**

---

### 1. Introduction & Spiritual Significance

The 'Lingashtakam' is an intensely powerful, awakening, and universally chanted hymn dedicated to the worship of the *Shiva Lingam*—the formless, infinite, and unmanifest representation of Lord Sadashiva. In the Sanatana tradition, the Lingam symbolizes the entire cosmic creation, containing within it the divine presence of Brahma, Vishnu, and Maheshwara.

This sweet octet (eight verses) beautifully describes the glory of the Lingam, how it is revered by gods, demons, and sages alike, and the immense spiritual benefits of beholding it. Reciting this stotram during daily Shiva worship, Rudrabhishekam, or on Mondays effortlessly destroys the accumulated sins of countless lifetimes and ultimately grants the seeker a place in Shivaloka (the eternal abode of Shiva).

* **Presiding Deity:** Lord Sadashiva (in the form of the Shiva Lingam)
* **Purpose (Viniyoga):** For the destruction of the cycle of birth and death, eradication of accumulated sins, removal of all forms of poverty, and the attainment of absolute liberation (Moksha).

---

### 2. Sanskrit Verses, Word-by-Word Meaning & English Translation

#### ॥ Verse 1 ॥
ब्रह्ममुरारिसुरार्चितलिङ्गं निर्मलभासितशोभितलिङ्गम् ।
जन्मजदुःखविनाशकलिङ्गं तत्प्रणमामि सदाशिवलिङ्गम् ॥ १॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Brahma-murāri-surārcita-liṅgaṃ** = The Lingam that is worshipped (*archita*) by Brahma, Murari (Vishnu), and the gods (*sura*)
* **Nirmala-bhāsita-śobhita-liṅgam** = The Lingam that is extremely pure (*nirmala*), beautifully radiant (*bhasita*), and splendid (*shobhita*)
* **Janmaja-duḥkha-vināśaka-liṅgaṃ** = The Lingam that is the destroyer (*vinashaka*) of the sorrows (*duhkha*) born from the cycle of birth (*janmaja*)
* **Tat-praṇamāmi sadāśivaliṅgam** = I bow down (*pranamami*) to that (*tat*) Lingam of Lord Sadashiva.

**Translation:**
I bow to that holy Lingam of Lord Sadashiva, which is adored and worshipped by Brahma, Vishnu, and the gods. I bow to the Lingam that shines with pure, immaculate radiance, and which completely destroys the agonies and sorrows arising from the cycle of birth and death.

---

#### ॥ Verse 2 ॥
देवमुनिप्रवरार्चितलिङ्गं कामदहं करुणाकरलिङ्गम् ।
रावणदर्पविनाशनलिङ्गं तत्प्रणमामि सदाशिवलिङ्गम् ॥ २॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Deva-muni-pravarārcita-liṅgaṃ** = The Lingam that is worshipped by the gods and the most exalted (*pravara*) sages (*muni*)
* **Kāmadahaṃ karuṇākara-liṅgam** = The Lingam that burnt down/destroyed the God of Lust (*kama-daham*) and is the ocean of compassion (*karunakara*)
* **Rāvaṇa-darpa-vināśana-liṅgaṃ** = The Lingam that utterly destroyed (*vinashana*) the immense pride/ego (*darpa*) of Ravana
* **Tat-praṇamāmi sadāśivaliṅgam** = I bow down to that Lingam of Lord Sadashiva.

**Translation:**
I bow to that holy Lingam of Lord Sadashiva, which is highly revered by the gods and the most exalted sages. I bow to the Lingam that burnt the God of Lust to ashes, which is an infinite source of compassion, and which shattered the colossal pride and ego of the demon king Ravana.

---

#### ॥ Verse 3 ॥
सर्वसुगन्धिसुलेपितलिङ्गं बुद्धिविवर्धनकारणलिङ्गम् ।
सिद्धसुरासुरवन्दितलिङ्गं तत्प्रणमामि सदाशिवलिङ्गम् ॥ ३॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Sarva-sugandhi-sulepita-liṅgaṃ** = The Lingam that is beautifully smeared (*sulepita*) with all sorts of fragrant pastes (*sarva-sugandhi*)
* **Buddhi-vivardhana-kāraṇa-liṅgam** = The Lingam that is the cause (*karana*) for the great enhancement/expansion (*vivardhana*) of pure intellect (*buddhi*)
* **Siddha-surāsura-vandita-liṅgaṃ** = The Lingam that is prostrated to/praised (*vandita*) by perfected beings (*siddhas*), gods (*sura*), and demons (*asura*)
* **Tat-praṇamāmi sadāśivaliṅgam** = I bow down to that Lingam of Lord Sadashiva.

**Translation:**
I bow to that holy Lingam of Lord Sadashiva, which is beautifully smeared with every kind of divine and fragrant essence. I bow to the Lingam that elevates and expands the seeker's intellect and wisdom, and which is equally revered and worshipped by perfected beings, gods, and demons alike.

---

#### ॥ Verse 4 ॥
कनकमहामणिभूषितलिङ्गं फणिपतिवेष्टितशोभितलिङ्गम् ।
दक्षसुयज्ञविनाशनलिङ्गं तत्प्रणमामि सदाशिवलिङ्गम् ॥ ४॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Kanaka-mahāmaṇi-bhūṣita-liṅgaṃ** = The Lingam that is adorned (*bhushita*) with gold (*kanaka*) and great precious gems (*mahamani*)
* **Phaṇipati-veṣṭita-śobhita-liṅgam** = The Lingam that looks splendid (*shobhita*) entwined (*veshtita*) by the Lord of Serpents (*phanipati*)
* **Dakṣa-suyajña-vināśana-liṅgaṃ** = The Lingam that destroyed (*vinashana*) the grand sacrifice (*suyajna*) of Prajapati Daksha
* **Tat-praṇamāmi sadāśivaliṅgam** = I bow down to that Lingam of Lord Sadashiva.

**Translation:**
I bow to that holy Lingam of Lord Sadashiva, which is exquisitely adorned with gold and magnificent precious jewels. I bow to the Lingam that looks breathtakingly splendid enveloped by the King of Serpents, and which utterly destroyed the arrogant and grand sacrifice of Prajapati Daksha.

---

#### ॥ Verse 5 ॥
कुङ्कुमचन्दनलेपितलिङ्गं पङ्कजहारसुशोभितलिङ्गम् ।
सञ्चितपापविनाशनलिङ्गं तत्प्रणमामि सदाशिवलिङ्गम् ॥ ५॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Kuṅkuma-candana-lepita-liṅgaṃ** = The Lingam that is smeared (*lepita*) with saffron (*kunkuma*) and sandalwood paste (*chandana*)
* **Paṅkaja-hāra-suśobhita-liṅgam** = The Lingam that is beautifully adorned (*sushobhita*) with garlands (*hara*) of lotus flowers (*pankaja*)
* **Sañcita-pāpa-vināśana-liṅgaṃ** = The Lingam that completely eradicates (*vinashana*) the accumulated (*sanchita*) sins (*papa*) of many lifetimes
* **Tat-praṇamāmi sadāśivaliṅgam** = I bow down to that Lingam of Lord Sadashiva.

**Translation:**
I bow to that holy Lingam of Lord Sadashiva, which is smeared with fragrant saffron and sandalwood paste, and which looks stunningly beautiful adorned with garlands of lotus flowers. I bow to the Lingam whose mere sight completely obliterates the accumulated sins of countless past lifetimes.

---

#### ॥ Verse 6 ॥
देवगणार्चितसेवितलिङ्गं भावैर्भक्तिभिरेव च लिङ्गम् ।
दिनकरकोटिप्रभाकरलिङ्गं तत्प्रणमामि सदाशिवलिङ्गम् ॥ ६॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Devagaṇārcita-sevita-liṅgaṃ** = The Lingam that is worshipped (*archita*) and served (*sevita*) by the multitudes/hosts of gods (*devagana*)
* **Bhāvair-bhaktibhireva ca liṅgam** = The Lingam that is truly attained/pleased only (*eva*) through pure sentiment (*bhava*) and true devotion (*bhakti*)
* **Dinakara-koṭi-prabhākara-liṅgaṃ** = The Lingam whose effulgence/radiance (*prabhakara*) is equal to millions (*koti*) of suns (*dinakara*)
* **Tat-praṇamāmi sadāśivaliṅgam** = I bow down to that Lingam of Lord Sadashiva.

**Translation:**
I bow to that holy Lingam of Lord Sadashiva, which is constantly worshipped and served by hosts of divine beings. I bow to the Lingam that can only be reached through the absolute purity of heart and unconditional devotion, and whose blinding spiritual radiance equals the brilliance of millions of suns.

---

#### ॥ Verse 7 ॥
अष्टदलोपरिवेष्टितलिङ्गं सर्वसमुद्भवकारणलिङ्गम् ।
अष्टदरिद्रविनाशकलिङ्गं तत्प्रणमामि सदाशिवलिङ्गम् ॥ ७॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Aṣṭadalopari-veṣṭita-liṅgaṃ** = The Lingam that is seated/enveloped (*veshtita*) upon (*upari*) an eight-petalled lotus (*ashtadala*)
* **Sarva-samudbhava-kāraṇa-liṅgam** = The Lingam that is the primordial cause (*karana*) for the creation/origin (*samudbhava*) of everything (*sarva*)
* **Aṣṭa-daridra-vināśaka-liṅgaṃ** = The Lingam that is the destroyer (*vinashaka*) of the eight types of poverty/miseries (*ashta-daridra*)
* **Tat-praṇamāmi sadāśivaliṅgam** = I bow down to that Lingam of Lord Sadashiva.

**Translation:**
I bow to that holy Lingam of Lord Sadashiva, which is majestically seated upon an eight-petalled lotus (symbolizing the heart chakra or the ritual pedestal). I bow to the Lingam that is the ultimate, primordial cause of the entire cosmic creation, and which completely eradicates the eight kinds of poverty and worldly miseries from a devotee's life.

---

#### ॥ Verse 8 ॥
सुरगुरुसुरवरपूजितलिङ्गं सुरवनपुष्पसदार्चितलिङ्गम् ।
परात्परं परमात्मकलिङ्गं तत्प्रणमामि सदाशिवलिङ्गम् ॥ ८॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Suraguru-suravara-pūjita-liṅgaṃ** = The Lingam that is worshipped (*pujita*) by the preceptor of the gods/Brihaspati (*suraguru*) and the best among gods (*suravara*)
* **Suravana-puṣpa-sadārcita-liṅgam** = The Lingam that is always worshipped (*sadarchita*) with divine flowers (*pushpa*) from the celestial gardens (*suravana*)
* **Parātparaṃ paramātmaka-liṅgaṃ** = The Lingam that is the Supreme beyond the supreme (*paratparam*) and the very embodiment of the Supreme Soul (*paramatmaka*)
* **Tat-praṇamāmi sadāśivaliṅgam** = I bow down to that Lingam of Lord Sadashiva.

**Translation:**
I bow to that holy Lingam of Lord Sadashiva, which is worshipped by the preceptor of the gods (Brihaspati) and the most exalted celestial beings. I bow to the Lingam upon which divine flowers from the heavenly gardens are continuously offered, and which is the absolute embodiment of the Supreme Soul, the highest reality beyond all that is supreme.

---

#### ॥ Phalashruti (Fruits of Recitation) ॥
लिङ्गाष्टकमिदं पुण्यं यः पठेच्छिवसन्निधौ ।
शिवलोकमवाप्नोति शिवेन सह मोदते ॥
ॐ शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning & Translation:**
* **Liṅgāṣṭakamidaṃ puṇyaṃ** = This highly meritorious and holy Lingashtakam
* **Yaḥ paṭhecchivasannidhau** = Whoever recites it in the holy presence of Lord Shiva (near a Shivalinga or temple)
* **Śivalokamavāpnoti** = Attains the supreme, eternal abode of Shiva (*Shivaloka*)
* **Śivena saha modate** = And rejoices there in eternal bliss with Lord Shiva.
* **Om Śāntiḥ Śāntiḥ Śāntiḥ** = Om. May there be absolute peace from physical, divine, and internal suffering.

---

### 3. Benefits of Recitation (Phalashruti)

Chanting the 'Lingashtakam' is exceptionally meritorious. When sung daily during Shiva worship or in the holy presence of a Shiva Lingam, this octet severs the painful cycle of birth and death. It effortlessly washes away the heavy burden of sins accumulated over countless past lifetimes and annihilates all forms of poverty and worldly misery. This beautiful stotram blesses the seeker with a pure intellect, immense inner joy, and ultimately grants residence in Shivaloka, where the soul rejoices eternally with Lord Shiva.
