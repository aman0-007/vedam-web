## ॥ Mandukya Upanishad ॥
**Complete Text, Word-by-Word Meaning, Translation & Significance**

---

### 1. Introduction & Spiritual Significance

The 'Mandukya Upanishad' belongs to the Atharva Veda and is the shortest of all the 108 major Upanishads, containing only 12 mantras. Despite its brevity, it holds the highest position in Advaita Vedanta philosophy. The Muktika Upanishad famously declares: *"If one desires to attain liberation (Moksha), the study of the Mandukya Upanishad alone is sufficient."*

This Upanishad provides a profound, scientific, and psychological analysis of the sacred syllable **'OM' (Omkara)** and the four states of human consciousness: Waking (*Jagrat*), Dreaming (*Svapna*), Deep Sleep (*Sushupti*), and the transcendental state of pure consciousness (*Turiya*). It perfectly maps the three syllables of OM (A, U, M) to the first three states, and the silence that follows OM (*Amatra*) to the ultimate truth—the Atman (Self).

* **Veda:** Atharva Veda
* **Core Subject:** The mystery of OM (Omkara), the four states of consciousness, and the realization of the Supreme Self (Atman).

---

### 2. Sanskrit Verses, Word-by-Word Meaning & English Translation

#### ॥ Mantra 1 ॥
ॐ इत्येतदक्षरमिदँ सर्वं तस्योपव्याख्यानं भूतं भवद्भविष्यदिति सर्वमोङ्कार एव । 
यच्चान्यत् त्रिकालातीतं तदप्योङ्कार एव ॥ १॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Om ityetadakṣaramidaṃ sarvaṃ** = This syllable 'OM' is indeed all this (the entire universe)
* **Tasyopavyākhyānaṃ** = This is its explanation/exposition
* **Bhūtaṃ bhavadbhaviṣyaditi** = All that is past, present, and future
* **Sarvamoṅkāra eva** = All that is verily Omkara (OM) alone
* **Yaccānyat trikālātītaṃ** = And whatever else there is, beyond the three periods of time
* **Tadapyoṅkāra eva** = That too is verily Omkara alone.

**Translation:**
This entire universe is nothing but the sacred syllable 'OM'. All that was in the past, all that is in the present, and all that will be in the future—everything is verily OM. Furthermore, whatever exists beyond the boundaries of these three periods of time (the eternal, unmanifest reality), that too is verily OM alone.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 2 ॥
सर्वं ह्येतद् ब्रह्मायमात्मा ब्रह्म सोऽयमात्मा चतुष्पात् ॥ २॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Sarvaṃ hyetad Brahma** = All this (the visible universe) is indeed Brahman (the Supreme Reality)
* **Ayamātmā Brahma** = This Atman (the inner Self) is Brahman
* **So'yamātmā catuṣpāt** = That same Atman has four quarters (states/aspects).

**Translation:**
All this manifest universe is verily Brahman (the Supreme Reality). And this Atman (the inner Self residing within us) is precisely that Brahman. This Atman is known to have four quarters or four states of experience.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 3 (The First Quarter - Waking State) ॥
जागरितस्थानो बहिष्प्रज्ञः सप्ताङ्ग एकोनविंशतिमुखः स्थूलभुग्वैश्वानरः प्रथमः पादः ॥ ३॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Jāgaritasthāno bahiṣprajñaḥ** = Whose sphere of activity is the waking state, and whose consciousness is directed outwards
* **Saptāṅga ekonaviṃśatimukhaḥ** = Who possesses seven limbs and nineteen mouths (doors of perception)
* **Sthūlabhug-vaiśvānaraḥ** = The experiencer of gross/physical objects, known as 'Vaishvanara'
* **Prathamaḥ pādaḥ** = This is the first quarter (of the Atman).

**Translation:**
The first quarter is 'Vaishvanara', whose sphere of activity is the waking state (*Jagrat*). In this state, consciousness is turned outward. He has seven limbs and nineteen mouths (representing the 5 organs of perception, 5 organs of action, 5 vital breaths, and the 4 aspects of the mind). In this state, the Atman experiences the gross, physical world.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 4 (The Second Quarter - Dream State) ॥
स्वप्नस्थानोऽन्तःप्रज्ञः सप्ताङ्ग एकोनविंशतिमुखः प्रविविक्तभुक् तैजसो द्वितीयः पादः ॥ ४॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Svapnasthāno'ntaḥprajñaḥ** = Whose sphere of activity is the dream state, and whose consciousness is directed inwards
* **Saptāṅga ekonaviṃśatimukhaḥ** = Who possesses seven limbs and nineteen mouths
* **Praviviktabhuk taijaso** = The experiencer of subtle/mental impressions, known as 'Taijasa'
* **Dvitīyaḥ pādaḥ** = This is the second quarter.

**Translation:**
The second quarter is 'Taijasa', whose sphere of activity is the dream state (*Svapna*). In this state, consciousness is turned inward (into the mind). He also possesses the same seven limbs and nineteen mouths, but here, he experiences only subtle, mental impressions created by the mind. This is the second aspect of the Atman.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 5 (The Third Quarter - Deep Sleep State) ॥
यत्र सुप्तो न कञ्चन कामं कामयते न कञ्चन स्वप्नं पश्यति तत् सुषुप्तम् । 
सुषुप्तस्थान एकीभूतः प्रज्ञानघन एवानन्दमयो ह्यानन्दभुक् चेतोमुखः प्राज्ञस्तृतीयः पादः ॥ ५॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Yatra supto na kañcana kāmaṃ kāmayate** = Where the sleeping person does not desire any object
* **Na kañcana svapnaṃ paśyati tat suṣuptam** = Nor sees any dream, that state is called 'Sushupti' (deep sleep)
* **Suṣuptasthāna ekībhūtaḥ prajñānaghana eva-** = Whose sphere is deep sleep, where all experiences become unified into a dense mass of consciousness
* **Ānandamayo hyānandabhuk** = Who is full of bliss and is the experiencer of pure bliss
* **Cetomukhaḥ prājñastṛtīyaḥ pādaḥ** = Who is the doorway to consciousness, known as 'Prajna'; this is the third quarter.

**Translation:**
That state is 'Sushupti' (deep sleep), where the sleeping person desires nothing and sees no dreams. The third quarter is 'Prajna', whose sphere is this deep sleep. In this state, all fragmented experiences become unified into an undifferentiated, dense mass of consciousness. Here, the Atman is full of bliss and experiences pure bliss, serving as the doorway to the knowledge of the other two states.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 6 (The Nature of Ishwara) ॥
एष सर्वेश्वर एष सर्वज्ञ एषोऽन्तर्याम्येष योनिः सर्वस्य प्रभवाप्ययौ हि भूतानाम् ॥ ६॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Eṣa sarveśvara eṣa sarvajña** = This (Prajna) is the Lord of all, this is the Omniscient One
* **Eṣo'ntaryāmyeṣa yoniḥ sarvasya** = This is the Inner Controller, this is the source/womb of all
* **Prabhavāpyayau hi bhūtānām** = This is indeed the origin and the dissolution of all beings.

**Translation:**
This Prajna (the Atman in its causal state) is the Lord of all (*Sarveshwara*), the all-knowing (*Sarvajna*), and the inner controller (*Antaryami*). It is the cosmic womb, the source from which the entire universe originates, and into which all beings ultimately dissolve.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 7 (The Fourth Quarter - Turiya / The Ultimate Truth) ॥
नान्तःप्रज्ञं न बहिष्प्रज्ञं नोभयतःप्रज्ञं न प्रज्ञानघनं न प्रज्ञं नाप्रज्ञम् । 
अदृष्टमव्यवहार्यमग्राह्यमलक्षणमचिन्त्यमव्यपदेश्यमेकात्मप्रत्ययसारं प्रपञ्चोपशमं शान्तं शिवमद्वैतं चतुर्थं मन्यन्ते स आत्मा स विज्ञेयः ॥ ७॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning & Translation:**
* **Nāntaḥprajñaṃ na bahiṣprajñaṃ nobhayataḥprajñaṃ** = It is not conscious of the internal (dream state), nor conscious of the external (waking state), nor conscious of both simultaneously.
* **Na prajñānaghanaṃ na prajñaṃ nāprajñam** = It is not a dense mass of consciousness (deep sleep), nor is it simple knowing, nor is it non-knowing (insentient).
* **Adṛṣṭam-avyavahāryam-agrāhyam-alakṣaṇam-** = It is unseen (by the senses), beyond empirical dealings, ungraspable (by action), devoid of any characteristics.
* **Acintyam-avyapadeśyam-ekātmapratyayasāraṃ** = It is unthinkable, indescribable, and its sole essence is the firm conviction of the existence of the one Self.
* **Prapañcopaśamaṃ śāntaṃ śivam-advaitaṃ** = It is the state where all worldly phenomena cease; it is supremely peaceful, perfectly auspicious, and non-dual (without a second).
* **Caturthaṃ manyante sa ātmā sa vijñeyaḥ** = The wise consider this to be the fourth quarter (*Turiya*). This is the true Atman, and this is what must be realized.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 8 (Correlation with the syllables of OM) ॥
सोऽयमात्माध्यक्षरमोङ्कारोऽधिमात्रं पादा मात्रा मात्राश्च पादा अकार उकारो मकार इति ॥ ८॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **So'yamātmādhyakṣaramoṅkāro'dhimātraṃ** = This very Atman, from the standpoint of syllables, is Omkara (OM)
* **Pādā mātrā mātrāśca pādā** = The quarters of the Atman are the syllables of OM, and the syllables of OM are the quarters of the Atman
* **Akāra ukāro makāra iti** = These syllables/letters are: A, U, and M.

**Translation:**
This very Atman (described in the four quarters above) is exactly identical to the sacred syllable 'OM' from the perspective of sound. The four quarters of the Atman are identical to the elements (matras) of OM, and the elements of OM are identical to the quarters of the Atman. These elements are the letters 'A', 'U', and 'M'.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 9 (The Letter 'A' - Waking State) ॥
जागरितस्थानो वैश्वानरोऽकारः प्रथमा मात्राऽऽप्तेरादिमत्त्वाद्वाऽऽप्नोति ह वै सर्वान्कामानादिश्च भवति य एवं वेद ॥ ९॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Jāgaritasthāno vaiśvānaro'kāraḥ prathamā mātrā** = Vaishvanara (whose sphere is the waking state) is the letter 'A', the first element of OM
* **Āpterādimattvādvā** = Because of its all-pervasiveness and its being the beginning
* **Āpnoti ha vai sarvānkāmān-ādiśca bhavati ya evaṃ veda** = He who knows this truth attains all desirable objects and becomes the foremost/chief among all.

**Translation:**
Vaishvanara, the consciousness of the waking state, is the letter 'A'—the first element of OM—because it is all-pervading and is the beginning of all sounds. He who meditates and realizes this profound truth attains the fulfillment of all his desires and becomes foremost among all.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 10 (The Letter 'U' - Dream State) ॥
स्वप्नस्थानस्तैजस उकारो द्वितीया मात्रोत्कर्षादुभयत्वाद्वोत्कर्षति ह वै ज्ञानसन्ततिं समानश्च भवति नास्याब्रह्मवित्कुले भवति य एवं वेद ॥ १०॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Svapnasthānastaijasa ukāro dvitīyā mātrā** = Taijasa (whose sphere is the dream state) is the letter 'U', the second element of OM
* **Utkarṣād-ubhayatvādvā** = Because of its superiority (over 'A') and being intermediate (between A and M)
* **Utkarṣati ha vai jñānasantatiṃ samānaśca bhavati** = He who knows this exalts his stream of knowledge and becomes equal-minded towards all
* **Nāsyābrahmavit kule bhavati ya evaṃ veda** = And in his lineage, no one is ever born who does not know Brahman.

**Translation:**
Taijasa, the consciousness of the dream state, is the letter 'U'—the second element of OM—because it is superior (more subtle) and stands between the waking and deep sleep states. He who realizes this truth elevates his tradition of knowledge, attains equanimity with everyone, and no one ignorant of Brahman is ever born in his family.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 11 (The Letter 'M' - Deep Sleep State) ॥
सुषुप्तस्थानः प्राज्ञो मकारस्तृतीया मात्रा मितेरपीतेर्वा मिनोति ह वा इदँ सर्वमपीतिश्च भवति य एवं वेद ॥ ११॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Suṣuptasthānaḥ prājño makārastṛtīyā mātrā** = Prajna (whose sphere is deep sleep) is the letter 'M', the third element of OM
* **Miterapītervā** = Because it is the measure (of everything) and the place of merging/dissolution
* **Minoti ha vā idaṃ sarvamapītiśca bhavati ya evaṃ veda** = He who knows this, truly measures (comprehends) this entire universe and becomes the place of its merging.

**Translation:**
Prajna, the consciousness of the deep sleep state, is the letter 'M'—the third element of OM—because it measures the other two states and is the point where they merge (dissolve). He who realizes this profound truth comprehends the true nature of the entire universe and himself becomes the ultimate reality into which everything merges.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 12 (Amatra / Turiya - The Supreme Truth) ॥
अमात्रश्चतुर्थोऽव्यवहार्यः प्रपञ्चोपशमः शिवोऽद्वैत एवमोङ्कार आत्मैव संविशत्यात्मनाऽऽत्मानं य एवं वेद ॥ १२॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Amātraścaturtho** = That which is without syllables/soundless (Amatra), is the fourth aspect (Turiya)
* **Avyavahāryaḥ prapañcopaśamaḥ śivo'dvaita** = It is beyond empirical dealings, the cessation of all phenomena, supremely auspicious, and non-dual
* **Evamoṅkāra ātmaiva** = Thus, the syllable OM is identically the Atman (the Supreme Self)
* **Saṃviśatyātmanā''tmānaṃ ya evaṃ veda** = He who knows this truth, merges his self into the Supreme Self.

**Translation:**
The partless, soundless silence that follows the chanting of OM (*Amatra*) is the fourth state (Turiya). It is completely beyond worldly dealings, it is the cessation of the phenomenal universe; it is supremely peaceful, auspicious, and non-dual. Thus, the complete Omkara is identically the Atman. The wise seeker who realizes this profound truth merges his individual self into the Supreme Self, attaining absolute liberation.

---

### 3. Benefits of Recitation (Phalashruti)

The Mandukya Upanishad is the most ancient and authoritative text on human consciousness and transcendental psychology. By understanding the profound meaning of its 12 mantras and meditating upon the sacred syllable 'OM', a seeker discovers their true, eternal identity (the Atman). This Upanishad shatters the darkness of ignorance, pulls the mind away from the external world (waking) and internal fantasies (dreaming), and anchors it in the non-dual state of *Turiya*. A seeker who continuously studies and contemplates these truths is liberated from the cycle of birth and death, merging entirely into the Supreme Brahman.
