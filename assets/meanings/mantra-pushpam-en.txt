## ॥ Mantra Pushpam ॥
**Complete Text, Word-by-Word Meaning, Translation & Significance**

---

### 1. Introduction & Spiritual Significance

The 'Mantra Pushpam' is an incredibly sacred and profound Vedic hymn extracted from the Taittiriya Aranyaka of the Yajur Veda. In the Sanatana tradition, it is chanted ceremoniously at the conclusion of a puja, yajna, or aarti, while devotees hold flowers in their hands to offer to the deity (Mantra Pushpanjali).

This hymn presents a beautiful cosmic science, illustrating the deep, interdependent relationship (Ayatanam - abode, source, or resting place) between 'Apah' (Water) and various forces of nature such as Fire, Wind, the Sun, the Moon, the Stars, the Rain-clouds, and Time (the Year). It serves as a tribute to the cosmic water cycle and the foundation of life. The hymn concludes with a majestic prayer to the King of Kings, Lord Kubera (Vaishravana), seeking the fulfillment of all worldly desires and the blessing of prosperity.

* **Source:** Taittiriya Aranyaka (1.22 and 1.31.6)
* **Presiding Deities:** Apah (Water), Moon, Fire, Wind, Sun, Stars, Clouds, Time (Samvatsara), and Lord Kubera
* **Purpose (Viniyoga):** For the successful completion of worship, to attain a stable and strong foundation (Ayatan) in life, and to fulfill all righteous material desires and wealth.

### 2. Sanskrit Verses with Svaras, Word-by-Word Meaning & Translation

#### ॥ Mantra 1 (Water and the Moon) ॥
ॐ यो॑ऽपां पुष्पं॒ वेद॑ । पुष्प॑वान् प्र॒जावा᳚न् पशु॒मान् भ॑वति ।
च॒न्द्रमा॒ वा अ॒पां पुष्पम्᳚ । पुष्प॑वान् प्र॒जावा᳚न् पशु॒मान् भ॑वति ।
य ए॒वं वेद॑ । यो॑ऽपामा॒यत॑नं॒ वेद॑ । आ॒यत॑नवान् भवति ॥ १॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Om yo'pāṃ puṣpaṃ veda** = He who knows the flower (essence/beauty) of water
* **Puṣpavān prajāvān paśumān bhavati** = He becomes endowed with flowers, excellent progeny, and cattle
* **Candramā vā apāṃ puṣpam** = The Moon is indeed the flower of water
* **Ya evaṃ veda** = He who knows this (attains the aforementioned blessings)
* **Yo'pāmāyatanaṃ veda** = He who knows the abode/source (Ayatanam) of water
* **Āyatanavān bhavati** = He becomes endowed with a firm abode (a stable foundation/home).

**Translation:**
He who understands the true flower (essence) of water becomes endowed with flowers, good progeny, and wealth in the form of cattle. The Moon is indeed the flower of water. He who realizes this profound truth is blessed with all these. Furthermore, he who knows the source and resting place of water becomes firmly established with a strong foundation and a stable home in life.

#### ॥ Mantra 2 (Water and Fire) ॥
अ॒ग्निर्वा अ॒पामा॒यत॑नम् । आ॒यत॑नवान् भवति ।
यो᳚ऽग्नेरा॒यत॑नं॒ वेद॑ । आ॒यत॑नवान् भवति ।
आपो॒ वा अ॒ग्नेरा॒यत॑नम् । आ॒यत॑नवान् भवति ।
य ए॒वं वेद॑ । यो॑ऽपामा॒यत॑नं॒ वेद॑ । आ॒यत॑नवान् भवति ॥ २॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Agnirvā apāmāyatanam** = Fire is indeed the abode/resting place of water
* **Yo'gnerāyatanaṃ veda** = He who knows the abode of fire
* **Āpo vā agnerāyatanam** = Water is indeed the abode of fire
* **Ya evaṃ veda... āyatanavān bhavati** = He who knows this... becomes endowed with a firm abode.

**Translation:**
Fire is indeed the source and resting place of water; he who knows this attains a stable foundation. He who knows the true abode of fire also attains a stable foundation. Water is, in truth, the abode of fire (as latent heat/electricity exists within water). He who understands this mutual relationship, and knows the source of water, becomes firmly established in life.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 3 (Water and Air/Wind) ॥
वा॒युर्वा अ॒पामा॒यत॑नम् । आ॒यत॑नवान् भवति ।
यो वा॒योरा॒यत॑नं॒ वेद॑ । आ॒यत॑नवान् भवति ।
आपो॒ वै वा॒योरा॒यत॑नम् । आ॒यत॑नवान् भवति ।
य ए॒वं वेद॑ । यो॑ऽपामा॒यत॑नं॒ वेद॑ । आ॒यत॑नवान् भवति ॥ ३॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Vāyurvā apāmāyatanam** = Air/Wind is indeed the abode of water
* **Yo vāyorāyatanaṃ veda** = He who knows the abode of the air
* **Āpo vai vāyorāyatanam** = Water is indeed the abode of the air.

**Translation:**
The Wind (Air) is indeed the source and resting place of water; he who knows this attains a stable foundation. He who knows the true abode of the wind also attains a stable foundation. Water is, in truth, the abode of the wind. He who understands this mutual relationship, and knows the source of water, becomes firmly established in life.

#### ॥ Mantra 4 (Water and the Sun) ॥
अ॒सौ वै तप॑न्न॒पामा॒यत॑नम् । आ॒यत॑नवान् भवति ।
यो॑ऽमुष्य॒ तप॑त आ॒यत॑नं॒ वेद॑ । आ॒यत॑नवान् भवति ।
आपो॒ वा अ॒मुष्य॒ तप॑त आ॒यत॑नम् । आ॒यत॑नवान् भवति ।
य ए॒वं वेद॑ । यो॑ऽपामा॒यत॑नं॒ वेद॑ । आ॒यत॑नवान् भवति ॥ ४॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Asau vai tapannapāmāyatanam** = That blazing Sun (yonder) is indeed the abode of water
* **Yo'muṣya tapata āyatanaṃ veda** = He who knows the abode of that blazing Sun
* **Āpo vā amuṣya tapata āyatanam** = Water is indeed the abode of that blazing Sun.

**Translation:**
That blazing Sun shining in the sky is indeed the source and resting place of water; he who knows this attains a stable foundation. He who knows the true abode of the blazing Sun also attains a stable foundation. Water is, in truth, the abode of the Sun (as the Sun draws energy and vapor from water). He who understands this mutual relationship, and knows the source of water, becomes firmly established in life.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 5 (Water and the Moon's Abode) ॥
च॒न्द्रमा॒ वा अ॒पामा॒यत॑नम् । आ॒यत॑नवान् भवति ।
यश्च॒न्द्रम॑स आ॒यत॑नं॒ वेद॑ । आ॒यत॑नवान् भवति ।
आपो॒ वै च॒न्द्रम॑स आ॒यत॑नम् । आ॒यत॑नवान् भवति ।
य ए॒वं वेद॑ । यो॑ऽपामा॒यत॑नं॒ वेद॑ । आ॒यत॑नवान् भवति ॥ ५॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning & Translation:**
The Moon is indeed the source and resting place of water; he who knows this attains a stable foundation. He who knows the true abode of the Moon also attains a stable foundation. Water is, in truth, the abode of the Moon (symbolizing the deep connection between lunar cycles and ocean tides). He who understands this mutual relationship, and knows the source of water, becomes firmly established in life.

#### ॥ Mantra 6 (Water and the Stars) ॥
नक्ष॑त्राणि॒ वा अ॒पामा॒यत॑नम् । आ॒यत॑नवान् भवति ।
यो नक्ष॑त्राणामा॒यत॑नं॒ वेद॑ । आ॒यत॑नवान् भवति ।
आपो॒ वै नक्ष॑त्राणामा॒यत॑नम् । आ॒यत॑नवान् भवति ।
य ए॒वं वेद॑ । यो॑ऽपामा॒यत॑नं॒ वेद॑ । आ॒यत॑नवान् भवति ॥ ६॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning & Translation:**
The Stars (Nakshatras) are indeed the source and resting place of water; he who knows this attains a stable foundation. He who knows the true abode of the stars also attains a stable foundation. Water is, in truth, the abode of the stars. He who understands this mutual cosmic relationship, and knows the source of water, becomes firmly established in life.

#### ॥ Mantra 7 (Water and the Rain-Clouds) ॥
प॒र्जन्यो॒ वा अ॒पामा॒यत॑नम् । आ॒यत॑नवान् भवति ।
यः प॒र्जन्य॑स्या॒यत॑नं॒ वेद॑ । आ॒यत॑नवान् भवति ।
आपो॒ वै प॒र्जन्य॑स्या॒यत॑नम् । आ॒यत॑नवान् भवति ।
य ए॒वं वेद॑ । यो॑ऽपामा॒यत॑नं॒ वेद॑ । आ॒यत॑नवान् भवति ॥ ७॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning & Translation:**
The Parjanya (rain-bearing clouds) are indeed the source and resting place of water; he who knows this attains a stable foundation. He who knows the true abode of the clouds also attains a stable foundation. Water is, in truth, the abode of the clouds. He who understands this mutual relationship, and knows the source of water, becomes firmly established in life.

#### ॥ Mantra 8 (Water, Time, and the Divine Boat) ॥
सं॒व॒त्स॒रो वा अ॒पामा॒यत॑नम् । आ॒यत॑नवान् भवति ।
यः सं॑वत्स॒रस्या॒यत॑नं॒ वेद॑ । आ॒यत॑नवान् भवति ।
आपो॒ वै सं॑वत्स॒रस्या॒यत॑नम् । आ॒यत॑नवान् भवति ।
य ए॒वं वेद॑ । यो᳚ऽप्सु नावं॒ प्रति॑ष्ठितां॒ वेद॑ । प्रत्ये॒व ति॑ष्ठति ॥ ८॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Saṃvatsaro vā apāmāyatanam** = The Samvatsara (the Year / wheel of Time) is indeed the abode of water
* **Yo'psu nāvaṃ pratiṣṭhitāṃ veda** = He who knows the boat (navam) established perfectly in the waters
* **Pratyeva tiṣṭhati** = He becomes completely established and stable (in this ocean of existence).

**Translation:**
The Year (the cycle of time/seasons) is indeed the source and resting place of water; he who knows this attains a stable foundation. He who knows the true abode of the Year also attains a stable foundation. Water is, in truth, the abode of the Year. He who understands this, and who recognizes the divine boat securely established in the vast waters (the support of the Divine in the ocean of samsara), becomes absolutely established and stable in this world.

#### ॥ Mantra 9 (Prayer to King of Kings, Lord Kubera) ॥
ॐ रा॒जा॒धि॒रा॒जाय॑ प्रसह्यसा॒हिने᳚ । नमो॑ व॒यं वै᳚श्रव॒णाय॑ कुर्महे ।
स मे॒ कामा॒न्काम॒कामा॑य॒ मह्यम्᳚ । का॒मे॒श्व॒रो वै᳚श्रव॒णो د॑दातु ।
कु॒बे॒राय॑ वैश्रव॒णाय॑ । म॒हा॒रा॒जाय॒ नमः॑ ॥ ९॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Rājādhirājāya** = To the King of Kings (Lord Kubera)
* **Prasahyasāhine** = Who is the forceful conqueror/endurer of adversaries
* **Namo vayaṃ vaiśravaṇāya kurmahe** = We offer our salutations to Vaishravana (the son of Sage Vishrava, i.e., Kubera)
* **Sa me kāmān-kāmakāmāya mahyam** = May He, to me who is desirous of fulfilling desires (kamakamaya), all my desires (kaman)
* **Kāmeśvaro vaiśravaṇo dadātu** = May Vaishravana, the Lord of Desires (Kameshwara), grant them
* **Kuberāya vaiśravaṇāya mahārājāya namaḥ** = Salutations to the great King Kubera, the descendant of Vishrava.

**Translation:**
We offer our deepest salutations to the King of Kings, the forceful conqueror of all adversaries, Lord Vaishravana (Kubera). May He, the Supreme Lord of desires, grant all the desires and objects of enjoyment to me, a seeker who is yearning for their fulfillment. Our repeated salutations to the great King, Lord Kubera Vaishravana.

---

#### ॐ शान्तिः॒ शान्तिः॒ शान्तिः॑ ॥
May there be absolute peace from physical, divine, and internal suffering.

### 3. Benefits of Recitation (Phalashruti Summary)

In the Vedic tradition, the chanting of 'Mantra Pushpam' is considered the crowning glory of any divine worship. Its profound mantras map the deep balance between human life and nature. The devotee who sings this hymn with proper Vedic intonations while offering flowers to the deity is blessed with an unshakable foundation in life—a good home, a stable career, and lasting prestige. The concluding prayer to Lord Kubera, the treasurer of the gods, ensures that all righteous material desires are met and the home is forever filled with immense wealth and prosperity.
