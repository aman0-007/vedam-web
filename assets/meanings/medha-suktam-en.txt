## ॥ Medha Suktam ॥
**Complete Text, Word-by-Word Meaning, Translation & Significance**

---

### 1. Introduction & Spiritual Significance

'Medha Suktam' is an incredibly powerful and mystical Vedic hymn found in the Taittiriya Aranyaka (Mahanarayana Upanishad). 'Medha' refers to the retentive, pure intellect—the power of memory, grasping capacity, and the inner wisdom required to absorb supreme knowledge.

For students, seekers of knowledge, and spiritual practitioners, this Suktam is an absolute boon. Chanting it daily with the proper Vedic intonations invokes the grace of the Supreme Brahman (Omkara), Lord Indra, and Goddess Saraswati (the presiding deity of knowledge). This hymn does not merely enhance worldly memory; it awakens the 'Daivi Medha' (divine intellect) that qualifies a human being to realize the Ultimate Truth (Brahma-Jnana).

* **Source:** Taittiriya Aranyaka (Prapataka 4, Anuvaka 41) / Mahanarayana Upanishad
* **Presiding Deities:** Supreme Brahman (OM), Indra, Saraswati, and the Ashvins
* **Purpose (Viniyoga):** For the immense enhancement of memory, attainment of Sattvic intellect (Medha), mastery over speech (Vak-siddhi), and spiritual enlightenment.

---

### 2. Sanskrit Verses with Svaras, Word-by-Word Meaning & Translation

#### ॥ Mantra 1 (Invocation to Omkara/Brahman) ॥
ॐ यश्छन्द॑सामृष॒भो वि॒श्वरू॑पः । छन्दो॒भ्योऽध्य॒मृता᳚त्सम्ब॒भूव॑ ।
स मेन्द्रो॑ मे॒धया᳚ स्पृणोतु । अ॒मृत॑स्य देव॒धार॑णो भूयासम् ।
शरी॑रं मे॒ विच॑र्षणम् । जि॒ह्वा मे॒ मधु॑मत्तमा ।
कर्णा᳚भ्यां॒ भूरि॒विश्रु॑वम् । ब्रह्म॑णः को॒शो॑ऽसि मे॒धया पि॑हितः ।
श्रु॒तं मे॑ गोपाय ॥ १॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Om yaśchandasāmṛṣabho** = OM, He who is the preeminent (bull/chief) among the Vedas (Chandas)
* **Viśvarūpaḥ** = Who is the universal form (manifest as the entire universe)
* **Chandobhyo'dhyamṛtātsambabhūva** = Who emerged as the immortal essence from the Vedas
* **Sa mendro medhayā spṛṇotu** = May that Supreme Lord (Indra) strengthen/endow me with wisdom (Medha)
* **Amṛtasya devadhāraṇo bhūyāsam** = O Lord, may I become the bearer of the immortal truth (Brahman)
* **Śarīraṃ me vicarṣaṇam** = May my body be healthy, active, and highly capable
* **Jihvā me madhumattamā** = May my tongue (speech) be exceedingly sweet
* **Karṇābhyāṃ bhūriviśruvam** = May I hear abundantly (the highest spiritual truths) with my ears
* **Brahmaṇaḥ kośo'si medhayā pihitaḥ** = O Omkara, You are the sheath/receptacle of Brahman, concealed by ordinary worldly intellect
* **Śrutaṃ me gopāya** = Please protect what I have heard (preserve my scriptural learning).

**Translation:**
May the Supreme Lord (Indra), who is the preeminent syllable 'OM' among all the Vedas, who is of universal form, and who emerged as the immortal essence of the Vedas, endow me with supreme intellect (Medha). O Lord, may I become a worthy bearer of the immortal Truth. May my body be highly capable and healthy, may my speech be exceedingly sweet, and may my ears listen abundantly to profound spiritual wisdom. O Omkara, You are the very receptacle of the Supreme Brahman, hidden only by ordinary worldly intellect; I pray that You constantly protect all the sacred knowledge I have heard and learned.

#### ॥ Mantra 2 (Invocation of Goddess Medha) ॥
मे॒धा दे॒वी जु॒षमा॑णा न॒ आगा᳚द्वि॒श्वाची॑ भ॒द्रा सु॑मन॒स्यमा॑ना ।
त्वया॒ जुष्टा॑ नु॒दमा॑ना दु॒रुक्ता᳚न् बृ॒हद्व॑देम वि॒दथे॑ सु॒वीराः᳚ ॥ २॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Medhā devī juṣamāṇā na āgāt** = May Goddess Medha (Intellect) come to us with delight
* **Viśvācī bhadrā sumanasyamānā** = She who is all-pervading (Vishvachi), highly auspicious (Bhadra), and favorably disposed towards us
* **Tvayā juṣṭā** = Blessed/graced by You
* **Nudamānā duruktān** = May we discard and destroy all harsh and evil words
* **Bṛhadvadema vidathe suvīrāḥ** = May we, blessed with excellent heroic progeny, speak profound truths in the sacred assembly/sacrifice.

**Translation:**
May Goddess Medha, who is all-pervading, highly auspicious, and favorably disposed towards us, come to our hearts with delight. Blessed and graced by You, O Goddess, may we completely abandon all harsh, untrue, and evil speech. Endowed with excellent, capable children, may we always speak profound and sacred truths in the holy assemblies.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 3 (The Glory of Divine Intellect) ॥
त्वया॒ जुष्ट॑ ऋ॒षिर्भ॑वति देवि॒ त्वया॒ ब्रह्मा॑ऽऽग॒तश्री॑रु॒त त्वया᳚ ।
त्वया॒ जुष्ट॑श्चि॒त्रं वि॑न्दते वसु॒ सा नो॑ जुषस्व॒ द्रवि॑णो न मेधे ॥ ३॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Tvayā juṣṭa ṛṣirbhavati devi** = O Goddess, favored by You, a person becomes a sage (Rishi/seer of mantras)
* **Tvayā brahmā** = By Your grace, one becomes a knower of Brahman
* **Āgataśrīruta tvayā** = And by Your grace, one attains immense wealth/splendor (Shri)
* **Tvayā juṣṭaścitraṃ vindate vasu** = Blessed by You, one acquires various kinds of multifaceted wealth (both material and spiritual)
* **Sā no juṣasva draviṇo na medhe** = O Goddess Medha! Being such, please be pleased to bestow upon us that supreme wealth (of wisdom).

**Translation:**
O Goddess! It is only when favored by You that a mortal becomes a Rishi (a seer of truth). By Your grace alone, one becomes a true knower of Brahman, and by Your grace, one attains supreme wealth and splendor. Blessed by You, an individual acquires wondrous and multifaceted spiritual and material treasures. O Goddess Medha, being so glorious, please be pleased with us and bestow upon us that ultimate wealth of divine wisdom.

#### ॥ Mantra 4 (Prayer for Medha from Deities) ॥
मे॒धां म॒ इन्द्रो॑ ददातु मे॒धां दे॒वी सर॑स्वती ।
मे॒धां मे॑ अ॒श्विना॑वु॒भावाध॑त्तां॒ पुष्क॑रस्रजा ॥ ४॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Medhāṃ ma indro dadātu** = May Lord Indra bestow Medha (intellect) upon me
* **Medhāṃ devī sarasvatī** = May Goddess Saraswati bestow Medha upon me
* **Medhāṃ me aśvināvubhāvādhattāṃ** = May both the Ashvins (the divine twin physicians) establish Medha in me
* **Puṣkarasrajā** = Who are beautifully adorned with garlands of lotuses.

**Translation:**
May Lord Indra bestow upon me a sharp and robust intellect. May Goddess Saraswati, the divine embodiment of knowledge, grant me pure intellect. And may the twin Ashvini Kumaras, who are adorned with beautiful lotus garlands, establish perfect health along with divine intellect within me.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 5 (Prayer for the Divine Intellect) ॥
अ॒प्स॒रासु॑ च॒ या मे॒धा ग॑न्ध॒र्वेषु॑ च॒ यन्मनः॑ ।
दैवी᳚ मे॒धा सर॑स्वती॒ सा मां᳚ मे॒धा सु॒रभि॑र्जुषता॒ग्॒ स्वाहा᳚ ॥ ५॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Apsarāsu ca yā medhā** = The intelligence and brilliance that exists within the Apsaras (celestial nymphs)
* **Gandharveṣu ca yanmanaḥ** = And the artistic mind/skill that exists within the Gandharvas (celestial musicians)
* **Daivī medhā sarasvatī** = The divine intellect of Goddess Saraswati
* **Sā māṃ medhā surabhirjuṣatāgṃ svāhā** = May that fragrant, blissful Medha be pleased to reside in me. Svaha (I offer this oblation).

**Translation:**
The captivating intelligence and brilliance found in the celestial Apsaras, the exquisite artistic mind residing in the Gandharvas, and the supreme divine intellect of Goddess Saraswati Herself—may that fragrant, auspicious, and blissful Medha gladly take residence within me. With this profound desire, I offer this oblation (Svaha).

---

#### ॥ Mantra 6 (The Universal Form of Medha) ॥
आमां᳚ मे॒धा सु॒रभि॑र्वि॒श्वरू॑पा हि॒रण्य॑वर्णा॒ जग॑ती जग॒म्या ।
ऊर्ज॑स्वती॒ पय॑सा॒ पिन्व॑माना॒ सा मां᳚ मे॒धा सु॒प्रती॑का जुषताम् ॥ ६॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Āmāṃ medhā surabhirviśvarūpā** = May that Medha, which is fragrant/pleasing and all-pervading (universal form), come to me
* **Hiraṇyavarṇā jagatī jagamyā** = Who is of golden complexion, the foundation of the world, and worthy to be attained by seekers
* **Ūrjasvatī payasā pinvamānā** = Who is full of vigor/energy, continuously nourishing us with milk (wisdom)
* **Sā māṃ medhā supratīkā juṣatām** = May that beautiful-faced/auspicious Medha be pleased to reside in me.

**Translation:**
May that divine Medha come to me—She who is pleasing, whose form encompasses the entire universe, whose complexion is like radiant gold, who is the very foundation of the world, and who is the ultimate goal of all seekers. She is full of vibrant energy and constantly nourishes us with the milk of wisdom. May that beautifully formed and auspicious Goddess Medha be pleased to dwell forever in my heart.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 7 (Prayer to the Five Elements/Deities) ॥
मयी॑ मे॒धां मयी॑ प्र॒जां मय्य॒ग्निस्तेजो॑ दधातु ।
मयी॑ मे॒धां मयी॑ प्र॒जां मयीन्द्र॑ इन्द्रि॒यं द॑धातु ।
मयी॑ मे॒धां मयी॑ प्र॒जां मयि॒ सूर्यो॒ भ्राजो॑ दधातु ॥ ७॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning & Translation:**
* **Mayi medhāṃ mayi prajāṃ mayyagnistejo dadhātu** = May Agni (the Fire God) establish within me pure intellect, excellent progeny, and brilliant radiance.
* **Mayi medhāṃ mayi prajāṃ mayīndra indriyaṃ dadhātu** = May Lord Indra establish within me pure intellect, excellent progeny, and immense sensory vitality/strength.
* **Mayi medhāṃ mayi prajāṃ mayi sūryo bhrājo dadhātu** = May Surya (the Sun God) establish within me pure intellect, excellent progeny, and divine illumination.

#### ॥ Mantra 8 (Hamsa Gayatri & Shanti Path) ॥
ॐ हं॒स॒हं॒साय॑ वि॒द्महे॑ परमहं॒साय॑ धीमहि । तन्नो॑ हंसः प्रचो॒दया᳚त् ॥
ॐ शान्तिः॒ शान्तिः॒ शान्तिः॑ ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning & Translation:**
* **Om haṃsahaṃsāya vidmahe paramahaṃsāya dhīmahi | Tanno haṃsaḥ pracodayāt ||** = We realize the Supreme Swan (the pure discriminator of truth and untruth); we meditate upon the Paramahamsa (the ultimate pure consciousness). May that Supreme Swan illuminate our intellects and inspire us toward the righteous path.
* **Om Śāntiḥ Śāntiḥ Śāntiḥ** = Om. May there be absolute peace from physical, divine, and internal suffering.

---

### 3. Benefits of Recitation (Phalashruti Summary)

Chanting the 'Medha Suktam' daily with complete purity, concentration, and proper Vedic intonations sharpens both worldly and spiritual intellect. For students who struggle with studies or those who suffer from weak memory, this Suktam acts as an infallible divine remedy. Its recitation bestows sweetness of speech, physical vitality, and a mind filled with positive, auspicious thoughts. Ultimately, by the grace of Lord Indra and Goddess Saraswati, the seeker attains the 'Daivi Medha' (divine intellect) required to become a true knower of Brahman.
