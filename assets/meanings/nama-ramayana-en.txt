## ॥ Nama Ramayanam (Sankshipta Ramayanam) ॥
**Complete Text, Word-by-Word Meaning, Translation & Significance**

---

### 1. Introduction & Spiritual Significance

The 'Nama Ramayanam' (also known as 'Sankshipta Ramayanam' or 'The Brief Ramayana') is an exceptionally sweet and powerful stotram composed by Sri Lakshmanacharya. In this hymn, the entire epic story of the Valmiki Ramayana is masterfully woven into a garland of 108 sacred names (Ashtottara Shatanamavali) of Lord Shri Rama.

From the Bala Kanda to the Uttara Kanda, every major event in Lord Rama's life is encapsulated as a divine name. In the Sanatana tradition, it is meant to be sung or chanted with devotion. At the end of each Kanda (chapter), a melodious chorus (Dhruvapada) — *"Rama Rama Jaya Raja Ram, Rama Rama Jaya Sita Ram"* — is sung, filling the mind with boundless peace and joy. It is believed that a person who is unable to read the complete, voluminous Ramayana can attain the exact same spiritual merit and the causeless grace of Lord Rama simply by chanting this 'Nama Ramayanam'.

* **Composer:** Sri Lakshmanacharya
* **Presiding Deity:** Lord Shri Rama
* **Purpose (Viniyoga):** To attain the immense spiritual merit of reciting the entire Ramayana, to destroy sins, and to cultivate pure, unalloyed devotion to Lord Rama.

### 2. Sanskrit Verses, Word-by-Word Meaning & English Translation

#### ॥ Mangalam (Chorus) ॥
Rāma Rāma Jaya Rājā Rām ।
Rāma Rāma Jaya Sītā Rām ॥

*(This auspicious chorus is traditionally sung at the end of each Kanda.)*

---

#### ॥ Bala Kandam ॥

**1. Śuddhabrahmaparātpara Rām**
* **Word Meaning:** The pure (*Shuddha*) Brahman, Supreme beyond the highest (*Paratpara*) Rama
* **Translation:** Victory to Lord Rama, who is the pure Absolute Truth and the Supreme Lord beyond all.

**2. Kālātmakaparameśvara Rām**
* **Word Meaning:** The embodiment of Time (*Kalatmaka*), the Supreme Lord (*Parameshwara*) Rama
* **Translation:** Victory to Lord Rama, who is the Supreme Lord and the very controller of Time.

**3. Śeṣatalpasukhanidrita Rām**
* **Word Meaning:** Sleeping happily (*sukhanidrita*) on the bed (*talpa*) of Adishesha (*Shesha*) Rama
* **Translation:** Victory to Lord Rama (as Lord Vishnu), who rests blissfully in Yogic slumber upon the serpent bed of Adishesha in the milk ocean.

**4. Brahmādyāmaraprārthita Rām**
* **Word Meaning:** Prayed to (*prarthita*) by immortal gods (*amara*) like Brahma (*brahmadya*) Rama
* **Translation:** Victory to Lord Rama, whose incarnation was ardently prayed for by Lord Brahma and other immortal gods (to end Ravana's tyranny).

**5. Caṇḍakiraṇakulamaṇḍana Rām**
* **Word Meaning:** The adornment/jewel (*mandana*) of the dynasty (*kula*) of the fierce-rayed Sun (*Chandakirana*) Rama
* **Translation:** Victory to Lord Rama, who is the glorious jewel adorning the Solar Dynasty (Suryavansha).

**6. Śrīmaddaśarathanandana Rām**
* **Word Meaning:** The joy-giving son (*nandana*) of the glorious King Dasharatha (*Srimaddasharatha*) Rama
* **Translation:** Victory to Lord Rama, the divine son who brings immense joy to His father, King Dasharatha.

**7. Kausalyāsukhavardhana Rām**
* **Word Meaning:** The enhancer (*vardhana*) of the happiness (*sukha*) of Mother Kausalya (*Kausalya*) Rama
* **Translation:** Victory to Lord Rama, who constantly multiplies the happiness of His mother, Queen Kausalya.

**8. Viśvāmitrapriyadhana Rām**
* **Word Meaning:** The beloved/dear (*priya*) wealth (*dhana*) of Sage Vishwamitra (*Vishwamitra*) Rama
* **Translation:** Victory to Lord Rama, who is like the most precious and beloved treasure to Sage Vishwamitra.

**9. Ghoratāṭakāghātaka Rām**
* **Word Meaning:** The slayer (*ghataka*) of the terrifying (*ghora*) demoness Tataka (*Tataka*) Rama
* **Translation:** Victory to Lord Rama, who slew the terrifying demoness Tataka.

**10. Mārīcādinipātaka Rām**
* **Word Meaning:** The one who cast down/struck down (*nipataka*) demons like Maricha (*Marichadi*) Rama
* **Translation:** Victory to Lord Rama, who struck down and defeated demons like Maricha and Subahu.

**11. Kauśikamakhasaṃrakṣaka Rām**
* **Word Meaning:** The protector (*samrakshaka*) of the sacrifice (*makha*) of Sage Kaushika/Vishwamitra (*Kaushika*) Rama
* **Translation:** Victory to Lord Rama, who successfully protected the holy fire sacrifice of Sage Vishwamitra from the demons.

**12. Śrīmadahalyoddhāraka Rām**
* **Word Meaning:** The deliverer/savior (*uddharaka*) of the venerable Ahalya (*Srimadahalya*) Rama
* **Translation:** Victory to Lord Rama, who liberated the pious Mother Ahalya from her curse (which had turned her into stone).

**13. Gautamamunisampūjita Rām**
* **Word Meaning:** Perfectly worshipped (*sampujita*) by Sage Gautama (*Gautamamuni*) Rama
* **Translation:** Victory to Lord Rama, who was respectfully worshipped by Sage Gautama (after Ahalya's liberation).

**14. Suramunivaragaṇasaṃstuta Rām**
* **Word Meaning:** Well-praised (*samstuta*) by the multitudes (*gana*) of gods (*sura*) and excellent sages (*munivara*) Rama
* **Translation:** Victory to Lord Rama, whose glories are sung by the hosts of gods and exalted sages.

**15. Nāvikadhāvitamṛdupada Rām**
* **Word Meaning:** Whose soft/tender feet (*mridupada*) were washed (*dhavita*) by the boatman (*navika*) Rama
* **Translation:** Victory to Lord Rama, whose soft lotus feet were lovingly washed by the boatman (Kevata).

**16. Mithilāpurajanamohaka Rām**
* **Word Meaning:** The captivator/enchanter (*mohaka*) of the people (*jana*) of the city of Mithila (*Mithilapura*) Rama
* **Translation:** Victory to Lord Rama, whose divine beauty captivated the hearts of the citizens of Mithila.

**17. Videhamānasarañjaka Rām**
* **Word Meaning:** The delighter (*ranjaka*) of the mind (*manasa*) of King Videha/Janaka (*Videha*) Rama
* **Translation:** Victory to Lord Rama, who brought immense delight and happiness to the mind of King Janaka.

**18. Tryambakakārmukabhañjaka Rām**
* **Word Meaning:** The breaker (*bhanjaka*) of the bow (*karmuka*) of Lord Shiva (*Tryambaka*) Rama
* **Translation:** Victory to Lord Rama, who effortlessly broke the mighty bow of Lord Shiva.

**19. Sītārpita-varamālika Rām**
* **Word Meaning:** To whom the garland of choosing a husband (*varamalika*) was offered (*arpita*) by Sita (*Sita*) Rama
* **Translation:** Victory to Lord Rama, around whose neck Mother Sita lovingly placed the wedding garland.

**20. Kṛtavaivāhikakautuka Rām**
* **Word Meaning:** Who performed (*krita*) the joyous/auspicious rites (*kautuka*) of marriage (*vaivahika*) Rama
* **Translation:** Victory to Lord Rama, who partook in the joyous and auspicious wedding festivities with Sita.

**21. Bhārgavadarpavināśaka Rām**
* **Word Meaning:** The destroyer (*vinashaka*) of the pride/ego (*darpa*) of Parashurama (*Bhargava*) Rama
* **Translation:** Victory to Lord Rama, who subdued the fiery pride of Lord Parashurama.

**22. Śrīmadayodhyāpālaka Rām**
* **Word Meaning:** The protector/ruler (*palaka*) of the glorious Ayodhya (*Srimadayodhya*) Rama
* **Translation:** Victory to Lord Rama, the benevolent protector and ruler of the glorious city of Ayodhya.

Rāma Rāma Jaya Rājā Rām ।

**34. Bharatārpitanijapāduka Rām**

* **Word Meaning:** The savior/uplifter (*uddharaka*) of all (*samasta*) the worlds (*loka*) Rama
* **Translation:** Victory to Lord Rama, who by His divine rule (Rama-Rajya) uplifted and brought prosperity to all the worlds.

Rāma Rāma Jaya Rājā Rām ।
Rāma Rāma Jaya Sītā Rām ॥

---

#### ॥ Uttara Kandam ॥

**87. Āgatamunigaṇasaṃstuta Rām**
* **Word Meaning:** Well-praised (*samstuta*) by the arriving (*agata*) multitudes of sages (*muni-gana*) Rama
* **Translation:** Victory to Lord Rama, whose glories were sung by the assembly of great sages who came to congratulate Him.

**88. Viśrutadaśakaṇṭhodbhava Rām**
* **Word Meaning:** Who heard the famous history (*vishruta*) of the origin/birth (*udbhava*) of the ten-necked Ravana (*dashakantha*) Rama
* **Translation:** Victory to Lord Rama, who listened to Sage Agastya narrate the history of Ravana's birth and ancestry.

**89. Sītāliṅgananirvṛta Rām**
* **Word Meaning:** Immensely satisfied/blissful (*nirvrita*) in the embrace (*alingana*) of Sita (*Sita*) Rama
* **Translation:** Victory to Lord Rama, who found supreme peace and bliss in the loving company of Mother Sita.

**90. Nītisurakṣitajanapada Rām**
* **Word Meaning:** Who safely protected (*surakshita*) His empire/people (*janapada*) through righteous policies (*niti*) Rama
* **Translation:** Victory to Lord Rama, who perfectly governed and protected His kingdom through absolute righteousness (Dharma).

**91. Vipinatyājitajanakaja Rām**
* **Word Meaning:** Who caused the daughter of Janaka/Sita (*janakaja*) to be abandoned/left (*tyajita*) in the forest (*vipina*) Rama
* **Translation:** Victory to Lord Rama, who, to uphold the harsh duties of a king, had to forsake pregnant Sita in the forest.

**92. Kāritalavaṇāsuravadha Rām**
* **Word Meaning:** Who caused the slaying (*vadha-karita*) of the demon Lavanasura (*Lavanasura*) Rama
* **Translation:** Victory to Lord Rama, who commanded Shatrughna to slay the evil demon Lavanasura.

**93. Svargataśambukasaṃstuta Rām**
* **Word Meaning:** Praised (*samstuta*) by Shambuka who had gone to heaven (*svargata-Shambuka*) Rama
* **Translation:** Victory to Lord Rama, who was praised by Shambuka after granting him heaven through punishment.

**94. Svatanayakuśalavanandita Rām**
* **Word Meaning:** Delighted (*nandita*) by His own sons (*sva-tanaya*) Kusha and Lava (*Kusha-Lava*) Rama
* **Translation:** Victory to Lord Rama, whose heart was filled with joy upon seeing and hearing His brilliant sons, Lava and Kusha.

**95. Aśvamedhakratudīkṣita Rām**
* **Word Meaning:** Initiated (*deekshita*) into the great Ashvamedha sacrifice (*Ashvamedha-kratu*) Rama
* **Translation:** Victory to Lord Rama, who took the vow and performed the grand Ashvamedha (Horse) sacrifice.

**96. Kālāveditasurapada Rām**
* **Word Meaning:** Informed (*avedita*) by Kala/Time (*Kala*) about His divine abode (*sura-pada*) Rama
* **Translation:** Victory to Lord Rama, to whom Kala (the God of Time) announced that it was time to return to Vaikuntha.

**97. Āyodhyakajanamuktita Rām**
* **Word Meaning:** Who granted liberation (*muktita*) to the people (*jana*) of Ayodhya (*Ayodhyaka*) Rama
* **Translation:** Victory to Lord Rama, who graciously granted eternal liberation (Moksha) to all the citizens of Ayodhya who followed Him.

**98. Vidhimukhavibudhānandaka Rām**
* **Word Meaning:** The giver of joy (*anandaka*) to the gods (*vibudha*) led by Brahma (*vidhi-mukha*) Rama
* **Translation:** Victory to Lord Rama, whose return to the heavens brought immense joy to Brahma and all the celestial gods.

**99. Tejomayanijarūpaka Rām**
* **Word Meaning:** Having a brilliantly radiant (*tejomaya*) original form (*nija-rupaka*) Rama
* **Translation:** Victory to Lord Rama, who reassumed His infinitely radiant, supreme form as Lord Mahavishnu.

**100. Saṃsṛtibandhavimocaka Rām**
* **Word Meaning:** The liberator (*vimochaka*) from the bonds (*bandha*) of worldly existence (*samsriti*) Rama
* **Translation:** Victory to Lord Rama, the ultimate liberator who frees His devotees from the endless cycle of birth and death.

**101. Dharmasthāpanatatpara Rām**
* **Word Meaning:** Ever-dedicated (*tatpara*) to the establishment (*sthapana*) of righteousness (*dharma*) Rama
* **Translation:** Victory to Lord Rama, who incarnates in every age, ever-dedicated to establishing Truth and Dharma.

**102. Bhaktiparāyaṇamuktida Rām**
* **Word Meaning:** The bestower of liberation (*muktida*) to those devoted to bhakti (*bhakti-parayana*) Rama
* **Translation:** Victory to Lord Rama, who effortlessly grants liberation to those who are completely surrendered to Him in devotion.

**103. Sarvacarācarapālaka Rām**
* **Word Meaning:** The protector/sustainer (*palaka*) of all (*sarva*) moving and non-moving beings (*charachara*) Rama
* **Translation:** Victory to Lord Rama, the supreme sustainer and protector of the entire animate and inanimate universe.

**104. Sarvabhavāmayavāraka Rām**
* **Word Meaning:** The curer/remover (*varaka*) of all (*sarva*) the diseases/sorrows (*amaya*) of worldly existence (*bhava*) Rama
* **Translation:** Victory to Lord Rama, the divine physician who destroys all the miseries and diseases of worldly attachment.

**105. Vaikuṇṭhālayasaṃsthita Rām**
* **Word Meaning:** Well-established (*samsthita*) in His eternal abode (*alaya*) of Vaikuntha (*Vaikuntha*) Rama
* **Translation:** Victory to Lord Rama, who is eternally seated in His supreme, transcendental abode of Vaikuntha.

**106. Nityānandapadasthita Rām**
* **Word Meaning:** Situated (*sthita*) in the state/abode (*pada*) of eternal bliss (*nityananda*) Rama
* **Translation:** Victory to Lord Rama, who is established in the absolute, supreme state of eternal bliss.

---

#### ॥ Mangalam (Concluding Chorus) ॥
**107. Rāma Rāma Jaya Rājā Rām**
* **Word Meaning & Translation:** Victory to King Rama! (This serves as the 107th name/chant in the rosary of 108 names).

**108. Rāma Rāma Jaya Sītā Rām**
* **Word Meaning & Translation:** Victory to Sita Rama! (This completes the 108th and final name of the Ashtottara Shatanamavali).

॥ Thus ends the Nama Ramayanam composed by Sri Lakshmanacharya ॥
॥ Om Shantih Shantih Shantih ॥

---

### 3. Benefits of Recitation (Phalashruti)

Regular recitation of the 'Nama Ramayanam' (Sankshipta Ramayanam) bestows upon the seeker the exact same immense spiritual merit as reading or listening to the entire, voluminous Valmiki Ramayana. Chanting this divine garland of 108 names eradicates all sorrows, sins, and diseases from the mind. By the causeless grace of Lord Shri Rama and Mother Sita, the devotee's home is filled with peace, prosperity, and joy, ultimately leading the soul to the supreme abode of Vaikuntha (Moksha).
