## ॥ Shri Narayana Suktam ॥
**Complete Text, Word-by-Word Meaning, Translation & Significance**

---

### 1. Introduction & Spiritual Significance

The 'Shri Narayana Suktam' is a profoundly mystical and divine hymn from the Yajur Veda (Taittiriya Aranyaka / Mahanarayana Upanishad). It represents the absolute pinnacle of Yoga, meditation, and Vedanta. In this Suktam, Lord Narayana is not merely described as a mythological deity but is established as the 'Antaryami'—the Supreme Brahman residing within the heart of every being.

This hymn provides a highly subtle and scientific description of the 'Heart Lotus' (Hridaya Kamala) located within the human body (above the navel) and the divine flame of the Supreme Soul burning within it. Chanting this Suktam with proper Vedic intonations, especially during Sandhyavandanam or meditation, grants the seeker self-realization, profound mental peace, and ultimate liberation.

* **Source:** Taittiriya Aranyaka (Prapataka 10, Anuvaka 13) / Mahanarayana Upanishad
* **Presiding Deity:** Shri Srimannarayana (The Supreme Soul / Paramatma)
* **Purpose (Viniyoga):** For self-realization, deep meditation on the Supreme residing in the heart-lotus, mental tranquility, and Moksha (liberation from the cycle of birth and death).

---

### 2. Sanskrit Verses with Svaras, Word-by-Word Meaning & Translation

#### ॥ Mantra 1 ॥
ॐ धा॒ता पु॒रस्ता॒द्यमु॑दाज॒हार॑ । श॒क्रः प्रवि॒द्वान्प्र॒दिश॒श्चत॑स्रः ।
तमे॒वं वि॒द्वान॒मृत॑ इ॒ह भ॑वति । नान्यः पन्था॒ अय॑नाय विद्यते ।
स॒ह॒स्र॒शीर्॑षं दे॒वं॒ वि॒श्वाक्षं॑ वि॒श्वश॑म्भुवम् । विश्वं॑ ना॒राय॑णं दे॒व॒म॒क्षरं॑ पर॒मं प॒दम् ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Om dhātā purastādyamudājahāra** = Om, He whom the Creator (Brahma) brought forth/revealed in the beginning
* **Śakraḥ pravidvānpradiśaścatasraḥ** = Whom Indra (Shakra) clearly perceived across the four directions
* **Tamevaṃ vidvānamṛta iha bhavati** = Knowing Him thus, one becomes immortal here (in this very life)
* **Nānyaḥ panthā ayanāya vidyate** = There is no other path to liberation
* **Sahasraśīrṣaṃ devaṃ** = The Lord with a thousand (infinite) heads
* **Viśvākṣaṃ viśvaśambhuvam** = Who has eyes everywhere (all-seeing) and is the benefactor of the universe
* **Viśvaṃ nārāyaṇaṃ devamakṣaraṃ paramaṃ padam** = The universe itself is Lord Narayana, the imperishable, the supreme goal.

**Translation:**
He whom the Creator (Brahma) realized and revealed at the very beginning of creation, and whom Lord Indra clearly perceived across the four directions—knowing that Supreme Lord thus, a mortal becomes immortal in this very life. There is no other path to ultimate liberation. We meditate upon that Lord who has infinite heads and infinite eyes, who is the ultimate benefactor of the cosmos. He is the universal form, Lord Narayana, the imperishable One, and the supreme destination of all souls.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 2 ॥
वि॒श्वतः॒ पर॑मान्नि॒त्यं॒ वि॒श्वं ना॑राय॒णग्ं ह॑रिम् । 
विश्व॑मे॒वेदं पुरु॑ष॒-स्तद्विश्व॒-मुप॑जीवति ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Viśvataḥ paramānnityaṃ** = Who is supreme (greater) than the universe and eternal
* **Viśvaṃ nārāyaṇagṃ harim** = The universal form, Lord Narayana, the destroyer of sins (Hari)
* **Viśvamevedaṃ puruṣaḥ** = This entire universe is indeed the Supreme Person (Purusha)
* **Tadviśvamupajīvati** = And that universe subsists/lives supported by Him.

**Translation:**
Lord Hari (Narayana) is greater than the entire universe; He is eternal and encompasses the cosmos within Himself. This entire visible universe is nothing but the manifestation of that Supreme Person (Purusha), and the entire creation is sustained only because it rests upon Him.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 3 ॥
पतिं॒ विश्व॑स्या॒त्मेश्व॑र॒ग्ं॒ शाश्व॑तग्ं शि॒व-म॑च्युतम् । 
ना॒राय॒णं म॑हाज्ञे॒यं॒ वि॒श्वात्मा॑नं प॒राय॑णम् ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Patiṃ viśvasyātmeśvaragṃ** = The Master of the universe, the Lord of all souls
* **Śāśvatagṃ śivamacyutam** = The eternal, auspicious, and infallible/imperishable One
* **Nārāyaṇaṃ mahājñeyaṃ** = Narayana is the supreme object to be known
* **Viśvātmānaṃ parāyaṇam** = The soul of the universe and the ultimate refuge.

**Translation:**
He is the master of the universe, the Supreme Lord of all individual souls, eternal, profoundly auspicious (Shiva), and infallible (Achyuta). Lord Narayana is the ultimate truth to be known. He is the Soul of the universe and the ultimate refuge for all beings.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 4 ॥
ना॒राय॒णप॑रो ज्यो॒ति॒रा॒त्मा ना॑राय॒णः प॑रः ।
ना॒राय॒णप॑रं ब्र॒ह्मं॒ त॒त्त्वं ना॑राय॒णः प॑रः ।
ना॒राय॒णप॑रो ध्या॒ता॒ ध्या॒नं ना॑राय॒णः प॑रः ।
यच्च॑ कि॒ञ्चिज्ज॑गत्स॒र्वं॒ दृ॒श्यते᳚ श्रूय॒तेऽपि॑ वा ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Nārāyaṇaparo jyotirātmā nārāyaṇaḥ paraḥ** = Narayana is the supreme light; Narayana is the supreme soul
* **Nārāyaṇaparaṃ brahmaṃ tattvaṃ nārāyaṇaḥ paraḥ** = Narayana is the supreme Brahman; Narayana is the supreme truth
* **Nārāyaṇaparo dhyātā dhyānaṃ nārāyaṇaḥ paraḥ** = Narayana is the supreme meditator; Narayana is the supreme object of meditation
* **Yacca kiñcijjagatsarvaṃ dṛśyate śrūyate'pi vā** = Whatever there is in this entire universe that is seen or heard...

**Translation:**
Lord Narayana is the supreme divine Light. Narayana is the Supreme Soul. Narayana is the Supreme Brahman. Narayana is the Supreme Reality (Tattva). Narayana is the highest meditator, and Narayana is the ultimate object of meditation. In this entire universe, whatever is seen by the eyes or heard by the ears... (this connects to the next verse).

---

#### ॥ Mantra 5 ॥
अन्त॑र्ब॒हिश्च॑ तत्स॒र्वं॒ व्या॒प्य ना॑राय॒णः स्थि॑तः ।
अन॑न्त॒मव्य॑यं क॒विग्ं स॑मु॒द्रेऽन्तं॑ वि॒श्वश॑म्भुवम् ।

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Antarbahiśca tatsarvaṃ vyāpya nārāyaṇaḥ sthitaḥ** = Pervading all that, inside and out, Lord Narayana stands established
* **Anantamavyayaṃ kavigṃ** = He is infinite, imperishable, and omniscient (kavi)
* **Samudre'ntaṃ viśvaśambhuvam** = Residing in the ocean (of the heart), He is the benefactor of the universe.

**Translation:**
... pervading all of that, both inside and out, Lord Narayana alone is established. He is infinite, imperishable, and omniscient. He is the ultimate benefactor of the universe, residing deep within the ocean of the human heart.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 6 (Location of the Heart Lotus) ॥
प॒द्म॒को॒श-प्र॑तीका॒श॒ग्ं॒ हृ॒दयं॑ चाप्य॒धोमु॑खम् ।
अधो॑ नि॒ष्ट्या वि॑तस्त्या॒न्ते॒ ना॒भ्यामु॑परि॒ तिष्ठ॑ति ।

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Padmakośa-pratīkāśagṃ** = Resembling the bud of a lotus
* **Hṛdayaṃ cāpyadhomukham** = And the heart, facing downwards
* **Adho niṣṭyā vitastyānte** = Below the Adam's apple (throat bone) at a distance of a hand-span
* **Nābhyāmupari tiṣṭhati** = And situated above the navel.

**Translation:**
(Now describing the location for meditation): Resembling a closed lotus bud, the human heart hangs with its face pointing downwards. It is situated at a distance of one hand-span below the throat bone (Adam's apple) and above the navel.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 7 ॥
ज्वा॒ल॒मा॒लाकु॑लं भा॒ती॒ वि॒श्वस्या॑यत॒नं म॑हत् । 
सन्त॑तग्ं शि॒लाभि॑स्तु॒ लम्ब॑त्याकोश॒सन्नि॑भम् ।

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Jvālamālākulaṃ bhātī** = It shines, surrounded by a garland of flames
* **Viśvasyāyatanaṃ mahat** = It is the great abode of the universe
* **Santatagṃ śilābhistu** = It is surrounded on all sides by veins/arteries (śilābhiḥ)
* **Lambatyākośasannibham** = And it hangs down like an unbloomed lotus bud.

**Translation:**
That heart lotus shines brilliantly, enveloped by a garland of radiant flames (of consciousness), and it is the great dwelling place of the entire universe (and the Supreme Lord). It is surrounded on all sides by a network of veins, hanging downwards like an unbloomed lotus bud.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 8 ॥
तस्यान्ते॑ सुषि॒रग्ं सू॒क्ष्मं तस्मिन्᳚ स॒र्वं प्रति॑ष्ठितम् । 
तस्य॒ मध्ये॑ म॒हान॑ग्नि-र्वि॒श्वार्चि॑-र्वि॒श्वतो॑मुखः ।

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Tasyānte suṣiragṃ sūkṣmaṃ** = In the center of it is an extremely subtle space/cavity
* **Tasmin sarvaṃ pratiṣṭhitam** = In that (subtle space), the entire universe is established
* **Tasya madhye mahānagniḥ** = In the middle of that space is a Great Fire
* **Viśvārci-rviśvatomukhaḥ** = With flames spreading everywhere, facing all directions.

**Translation:**
In the exact center of that heart lotus is an incredibly subtle space (void/akasha). The entire cosmos is established within that subtle void. In the middle of that void burns a Great Fire (the fire of consciousness/digestive fire), whose brilliant flames radiate in all directions and which has faces on all sides.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 9 ॥
सोऽग्र॑भु॒ग्विभ॑जन्ति॒ष्ठ॒-न्नाहा॑रमज॒रः क॒विः । 
ति॒र्य॒गू॒र्ध्व म॑धश्शा॒यी॒ र॒श्मय॑स्तस्य॒ सन्त॑ता ।

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **So'grabhugvibhajantiṣṭhan** = That (fire) is the first consumer, constantly distributing
* **Nāhāramajaraḥ kaviḥ** = The food, being ageless and omniscient
* **Tiryagūrdhva madhaśśāyī** = Its rays spread horizontally, upwards, and downwards
* **Raśmayastasya santatā** = Its rays are continuous and pervasive.

**Translation:**
That supreme fire of consciousness is the first to consume the food eaten by the individual, and it perfectly distributes the nourishment throughout the body. That fire is ageless and all-knowing. Its radiant rays continuously spread everywhere—horizontally, upwards, and downwards.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 10 ॥
स॒न्ता॒पय॑ति स्वं दे॒हमापा॑दतल॒मस्त॒कः । 
तस्य॒ मध्ये॒ वह्नि॑शिखा अ॒णीयो᳚र्ध्वा व्य॒वस्थि॒॑तः ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Santāpayati svaṃ dehamāpādatalamastakaḥ** = It warms its own body from the soles of the feet to the crown of the head
* **Tasya madhye vahniśikhā** = In the center of that (fire) is a tongue of flame
* **Aṇīyōrdhvā vyavasthitaḥ** = Which is extremely subtle (like an atom) and rises upwards.

**Translation:**
That conscious fire continuously warms and invigorates this physical body from the soles of the feet to the crown of the head. In the exact center of that great fire is a single tongue of flame, which is extremely subtle (as small as an atom) and points upwards.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 11 ॥
नी॒लतो॑-यद॑मध्य॒स्था॒-द्वि॒द्युल्ले॑खेव॒ भास्व॑रा ।
नी॒वार॒शूक॑वत्त॒न्वी॒ पी॒ता भा᳚स्वत्य॒णूप॑मा ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Nīlatoyadamadhyasthād-vidyullekheva bhāsvarā** = Shining brilliantly like a streak of lightning in the midst of a dark blue rain-cloud
* **Nīvāraśūkavattanvī** = Slender like the awn (fine tip) of a wild paddy grain
* **Pītā bhāsvatyaṇūpamā** = Yellowish/golden, luminous, and subtle like an atom.

**Translation:**
What does that subtle flame look like? It shines brilliantly like a dazzling streak of lightning amidst a dark blue rain-cloud. That flame is as incredibly slender as the fine tip of a wild paddy grain; it is yellowish-golden in color, highly luminous, and as subtle as a single atom. (This is the visual representation of the soul/Supreme Soul).

---

#### ॥ Mantra 12 ॥
तस्याः᳚ शिखा॒या म॑ध्ये प॒रमा᳚त्मा व्य॒वस्थि॑तः ।
स ब्रह्म॒ स शिवः॒ स हरिः॒ सेन्द्रः॒ सोऽक्ष॑रः पर॒मः स्व॒राट् ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Tasyāḥ śikhāyā madhye** = In the exact middle of that flame
* **Paramātmā vyavasthitaḥ** = The Supreme Soul (Paramatma) is established
* **Sa brahma sa śivaḥ sa hariḥ sendraḥ** = He is Brahma, He is Shiva, He is Hari, He is Indra
* **So'kṣaraḥ paramaḥ svarāṭ** = He is the imperishable, the supreme, the self-luminous sovereign.

**Translation:**
In the exact center of that subtle flame, the Supreme Soul (Paramatma) is securely established. That Supreme Soul is Brahma, He is Shiva, He is Hari (Vishnu), and He is Indra. He is the imperishable truth, the ultimate Supreme, and the self-luminous sovereign of the universe.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 13 ॥
ऋ॒तग्ं स॒त्यं प॑रं ब्र॒ह्म॒ पु॒रुषं॑ कृष्ण॒पिङ्ग॑लम् ।
ऊ॒र्ध्वरे॑तं वि॑रूपा॒क्षं॒ वि॒श्वरू॑पाय॒ वै नमो॒ नमः॑ ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Ṛtagṃ satyaṃ paraṃ brahma** = The cosmic truth, the reality, the Supreme Brahman
* **Puruṣaṃ kṛṣṇapiṅgalam** = The Supreme Person, who is dark blue and reddish-yellow (Hari-Hara)
* **Ūrdhvaretaṃ virūpākṣaṃ** = The supreme yogi of absolute purity, having extraordinary eyes (Shiva)
* **Viśvarūpāya vai namo namaḥ** = To that Universal Form, salutations again and again.

**Translation:**
To the One who is the eternal cosmic law, the absolute truth, and the Supreme Brahman; to the Supreme Person whose complexion is a blend of dark blue and reddish-yellow; to the ultimate celibate Yogi endowed with extraordinary vision; to that Supreme Lord who manifests as the entire universe—our salutations again and again.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 14 (Narayana Gayatri) ॥
ना॒रा॒य॒णाय॑ वि॒द्महे॑ वासुदे॒वाय॑ धीमहि ।
तन्नो॑ विष्णुः प्रचो॒दया᳚त् ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning & Translation:**
We realize Lord Narayana; we meditate upon the all-pervading Lord Vasudeva. May that Lord Vishnu illuminate our intellects and inspire us toward the righteous path of liberation.

---

#### ॐ शान्तिः॒ शान्तिः॒ शान्तिः॑ ॥
May there be absolute peace from physical, divine, and internal suffering.

---

### 3. Benefits of Recitation (Phalashruti Summary)

Chanting the 'Shri Narayana Suktam' is the most powerful tool for deep meditation and self-realization. A seeker who meditates on the 'subtle golden flame' within the heart-lotus, as described in these verses, is instantly freed from ignorance and the fear of death. Daily recitation ensures perfect mental focus, inner purity, and eventually qualifies the devotee for Moksha (complete liberation and union with Lord Narayana).
