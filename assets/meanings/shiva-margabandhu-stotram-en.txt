## Shri Margabandhu Stotram (Composed by Sri Appayya Dikshita)
**Complete Text, Word-by-Word Meaning, Translation & Significance**

---

### 1. Introduction & Spiritual Significance

The **Shri Margabandhu Stotram** is a revered and highly effective Vedic hymn composed by the 16th-century Shaiva philosopher, scholar, and polymath **Sri Appayya Dikshita** (Appayya Yajvendra).

In Sanskrit, **'Margabandhu'** translates to *"the Protector, Companion, and Friend along the path of a journey and life."* Lord Shiva is worshipped as 'Margabandhu' (the presiding deity of the Sri Margabandheeswarar Temple in Virinchipuram, Tamil Nadu). In Sanatana Dharma, reciting this hymn before undertaking any physical journey—or while navigating difficult circumstances and spiritual pathways—is considered immensely auspicious for ensuring safety, fearlessness, and success.

* **Composer:** Sri Appayya Dikshita (Appayya Yajvendra)
* **Presiding Deity:** Lord Shiva (Margabandhu / Ashutosha Mahesha)
* **Purpose (Viniyoga):** For divine protection (*Abhaya*) during travel, accident prevention, removal of obstacles along the path, and successful accomplishment of goals.

---

### 2. Sanskrit Verses, Word-by-Word Meaning & English Translation

#### ॥ Pallavi ॥
शंभो महादेव देव शिव शंभो महादेव देवेश शंभो ।
शंभो महादेव देव ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Śambho** = O Source of Auspiciousness (Shambhu)!
* **Mahādeva Deva** = O Great Lord, God of Gods!
* **Śiva Śambho** = O Supreme Auspicious Lord Shambhu!
* **Deveśa** = O Lord of all Devas (Celestial Beings)!

**Translation:**
O Shambhu, the source of auspiciousness! O Mahadeva, God of Gods! O Lord of all Devas, Margabandhu Shiva Shambhu! Please bless my journey with safety and auspiciousness; salutations to You again and again.

---

#### ॥ Verse 1 ॥
फालावनम्रत्किरीटं फालनेत्रार्चिषा दग्धपञ्चेषुकीटम् ।
शूलाहतारातिकूटं शुद्धमर्धेन्दुचूडं भजे मार्गबन्धुम् ॥ १ ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Phālāvanamrat-kirīṭam** = At whose feet bow the crowns of prostrating Devas (like Indra)
* **Phāla-netrārciṣā** = By the fiery flame of whose forehead (third) eye
* **Dagdha-pañceṣu-kīṭam** = Who burnt to ashes the insect-like Kamadeva (wielder of five arrows)
* **Śūlāhatārāti-kūṭam** = Who destroys multitudes of enemies with His Trident (*Trishula*)
* **Śuddham** = Who is supremely pure and untainted
* **Ardhendu-cūḍam** = Who wears the crescent moon as an ornament in His matted hair
* **Bhaje Mārgabandhum** = I worship that Lord Shiva, the Friend and Protector of the path (*Margabandhu*).

**Translation:**
I worship Lord Shiva, the true Protector of the path (*Margabandhu*), before whose feet bow the crowned heads of all Devas, who burnt the insect-like Kamadeva to ashes with the fierce flame of His forehead eye, who destroys hordes of enemies with His Trident, who is supremely pure, and who wears the crescent moon upon His crest.

---

#### ॥ Verse 2 ॥
अङ्गे विराजद्भुजङ्गं अभ्रगङ्गातरङ्गाभिरामोत्तमाङ्गम् ।
ओंकारवाटीकुरङ्गं सिद्धसंसेविताङ्घ्रिं भजे मार्गबन्धुम् ॥ २ ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Aṅge virājad-bhujaṅgam** = On whose limbs shine serpent kings as adornments
* **Abhra-gaṅgā-taraṅgābhirāmottamāṅgam** = Whose head (*uttamāṅga*) is beautifully adorned by the sacred waves of the celestial Ganga (river of the sky)
* **Oṅkāra-vāṭī-kuraṅgam** = Who is like a divine deer (*kuraṅga*) sporting in the garden (*vāṭī*) of Omkara (*Pranava*)
* **Siddha-saṃsevitāṅghrim** = Whose lotus feet are constantly served and worshipped by Siddhas and enlightened sages
* **Bhaje Mārgabandhum** = I worship that Lord Shiva, the Friend and Protector of the path.

**Translation:**
I worship Lord Margabandhu Shiva, whose limbs are adorned with radiant serpents, whose head is made resplendent by the sacred waves of the celestial Ganga, who sports like a divine deer in the garden of Omkara, and whose lotus feet are eternally worshipped by Siddhas and perfected masters.

---

#### ॥ Verse 3 ॥
नित्यं चिदानन्दरूपं निह्नुताशेषलोकेशवैरिप्रतापम् ।
कार्तस्वरागेन्द्रचापं कृत्तिवासं भजे दिव्यसन्मार्गबन्धुम् ॥ ३ ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Nityam** = Who is eternal and imperishable
* **Cidānanda-rūpam** = Who is the embodiment of absolute consciousness and supreme bliss (*Sat-Chit-Ananda*)
* **Nihnutāśeṣa-lokeśa-vairi-pratāpam** = Who has entirely crushed the arrogance and might of all enemies (*asuras*) of the rulers of the worlds (*Devas*)
* **Kārtasvarāgendra-cāpam** = Who made the golden mountain (Mount Meru) His bow during the destruction of Tripura
* **Kṛttivāsam** = Who wears a tiger skin (*kṛtti*) as His garment
* **Bhaje Divya-sanmārga-bandhum** = I worship that divine Lord Shiva, the Companion of the righteous spiritual path.

**Translation:**
I worship Lord Shiva, the divine Companion of the righteous path, who is eternal and the embodiment of consciousness and bliss, who completely crushed the pride and power of the enemies of the Devas, who wielded the golden Mount Meru as His bow, and who is clad in a tiger skin.

---

#### ॥ Verse 4 ॥
कन्दर्पदर्पघ्नमीशं कालकण्ठं महेशं महाव्योमकेशम् ।
कुन्दाभदन्तं सुरेशं कोटिसूर्यप्रकाशं भजे मार्गबन्धुम् ॥ ४ ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Kandarpa-darpaghnam** = Who destroyed the pride and conceit of Kandarpa (Kamadeva)
* **Īśam** = Who is the Supreme Lord
* **Kālakaṇṭham** = Whose throat is blue (*Neelakantha*) due to holding the deadly Halahala poison
* **Maheśam** = Who is the Great God (*Maheshwara*)
* **Mahā-vyoma-keśam** = Whose hair is the vast infinite sky (*Digambara* form)
* **Kundābha-dantam** = Whose teeth are as white and radiant as Kunda flowers
* **Sureśam** = Who is the Lord of all Devas
* **Koṭi-sūrya-prakāśam** = Who shines with the brilliance of ten million suns
* **Bhaje Mārgabandhum** = I worship that Lord Shiva, the Friend and Protector of the path.

**Translation:**
I worship Lord Margabandhu Shiva, the Supreme Lord who crushed the pride of Kamadeva, the blue-throated Maheshwara whose matted locks are the vast open sky, whose teeth gleam like white Kunda blossoms, who is the ruler of the Devas, and who blazes with the radiance of ten million suns.

---

#### ॥ Verse 5 ॥
मन्दारभूतेरुदारं मन्दरागेन्द्रसारं महाघौघदूरम् ।
सिन्धूरदूरप्रचारं सिन्धुराजातिधीरं भजे मार्गबन्धुम् ॥ ५ ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Mandāra-bhūter-udāram** = Who is more generous and wish-fulfilling than the Kalpavriksha (Mandara tree) in granting abundance
* **Mandarāgendra-sāram** = Who is as immovable, steadfast, and substantial as Mount Mandara
* **Mahāghaugha-dūram** = Who removes and drives far away the great flood of sins (*mahā-agha-ogha*)
* **Sindhūra-dūra-pracāram** = Whose divine grace and might stride far and wide like a royal elephant (*Airavata / Sindhura*)
* **Sindhurājāti-dhīram** = Who is deeper, calmer, and more steadfast than the king of oceans (*Sindhuraja*)
* **Bhaje Mārgabandhum** = I worship that Lord Shiva, the Friend and Protector of the path.

**Translation:**
I worship Lord Margabandhu Shiva, who is more bountiful than the celestial wish-fulfilling Mandara tree, as steadfast as Mount Mandara, who drives far away the great floods of sins, whose majestic power moves far and wide like a royal elephant, and who is profoundly deeper and calmer than the king of oceans.

---

#### ॥ Verse 6 (Phalashruti) ॥
अप्पय्ययज्वेन्द्रगीतं स्तोत्रराजं पठेद्यस्तु भक्त्या प्रयाणे ।
तस्यार्थसिद्धिं विधत्ते मार्गमध्येऽभयं चाशुतोषी महेशः ॥ ६ ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Appayya-yajvendra-gītam** = Composed by the great Vedic ritualist and scholar Sri Appayya Dikshita (*Yajvendra*)
* **Stotra-rājam** = This king among hymns (*Stotraraja — Margabandhu Stotram*)
* **Paṭhed-yas-tu bhaktyā** = Whoever recites with devotion
* **Prayāṇe** = At the commencement of a journey (or along the journey of life)
* **Tasyārtha-siddhiṃ vidhatte** = For him, He grants the complete fulfillment of desired goals and purpose
* **Mārga-madhye'bhayaṃ ca** = And complete fearlessness and safety (*abhaya*) throughout the path
* **Āśutoṣī Maheśaḥ** = Lord Maheshwara, who is easily and swiftly pleased (*Ashutosha*).

**Translation:**
Whoever recites with devotion this king of hymns composed by the great scholar-ritualist Sri Appayya Dikshita before embarking on a journey, the easily pleased Lord Maheshwara (*Ashutosha*) grants him total fulfillment of his purpose and ensures complete safety, protection, and fearlessness throughout the path.

---

#### ॥ इति श्री अप्पय्यदीक्षितप्रणीतम् श्रीमार्गबन्धुस्तोत्रम् संपूर्णम् ॥

---

### 3. Benefits of Recitation (Phalashruti)

Reciting the **Sri Margabandhu Stotram** before undertaking any journey or during daily prayer eliminates all obstacles, perils, and fear of accidents along the path. Lord Shiva, as *Ashutosha Mahesha* (He who is swiftly pleased), walks with the seeker as a guardian and friend (*Margabandhu*), granting divine safety (*Abhaya*) and ensuring complete success and fulfillment of purpose (*Artha-siddhi*) in the endeavor.
