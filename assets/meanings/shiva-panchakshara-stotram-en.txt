## Shri Shiva Panchakshara Stotram (Composed by Adi Shankaracharya)
**Complete Text, Word-by-Word Meaning, Translation & Significance**

---

### 1. Introduction & Spiritual Significance

The 'Shri Shiva Panchakshara Stotram' is an incredibly sweet and powerful hymn composed by the great philosopher-saint Adi Guru Shankaracharya. This stotram is based on the five sacred syllables of Lord Shiva's supreme mantra: **"Na-Ma-Shi-Va-Ya"** (Om Namah Shivaya).

Each verse of the hymn begins sequentially with the syllables 'Na', 'Ma', 'Shi', 'Va', and 'Ya'. In Sanatana Dharma, the Panchakshari Mantra is considered the essence of all the Vedas and the ultimate bestower of liberation (*Moksha*). By reciting this stotram, the seeker contemplates both the *Saguna* (manifest, compassionate, and beautiful) and *Nirguna* (unmanifest, eternal, and absolute) aspects of Lord Shiva simultaneously.

* **Composer:** Adi Guru Shankaracharya
* **Presiding Deity:** Lord Shiva (as the embodiment of the Panchakshara Mantra)
* **Purpose (Viniyoga):** For deepening devotion to Shiva, self-purification, mental peace, and the ultimate attainment of Shivaloka (the divine abode of Shiva).

---

### 2. Sanskrit Verses, Word-by-Word Meaning & English Translation

#### ॥ Verse 1 (Syllable 'Na') ॥
नागेन्द्रहाराय त्रिलोचनाय भस्माङ्गरागाय महेश्वराय ।
नित्याय शुद्धाय दिगम्बराय तस्मै 'न' काराय नमः शिवाय ॥ १॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Nāgendra-hārāya** = To Him who wears the king of snakes (Vasuki) as a garland
* **Trilocanāya** = To the three-eyed Lord
* **Bhasmāṅgarāgāya** = To Him whose body is smeared with sacred ash (*bhasma*)
* **Maheśvarāya** = To the Great Lord (*Maheshwara*)
* **Nityāya** = To Him who is eternal and everlasting
* **Śuddhāya** = To Him who is supremely pure and untainted
* **Digambarāya** = To Him who is clad in the directions / the sky (*Digambara*)
* **Tasmai 'Na' kārāya namaḥ śivāya** = Salutations to that Lord Shiva, who is embodied as the syllable 'Na'.

**Translation:**
He who wears the king of snakes as a garland, who has three eyes, whose body is smeared with sacred ash, and who is the Great Lord; He who is eternal, supremely pure, and clad in the four directions—my salutations to that Lord Shiva, who is the embodiment of the syllable 'Na'.

---

#### ॥ Verse 2 (Syllable 'Ma') ॥
मन्दाकिनीसलिलचन्दनचर्चिताय नन्दीश्वरप्रमथनाथमहेश्वराय ।
मन्दारपुष्पबहुपुष्पसुपूजिताय तस्मै 'म' काराय नमः शिवाय ॥ २॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Mandākinī-salila-candana-carcitāya** = To Him who is adorned with the sacred water of the Mandakini (celestial Ganga) and sandalwood paste
* **Nandīśvara-pramathanātha-maheśvarāya** = To the Great Lord of Nandi and the multitudes of goblin hosts (*pramathas*)
* **Mandāra-puṣpa-bahu-puṣpa-supūjitāya** = To Him who is beautifully worshipped with Mandara flowers and many other fragrant blossoms
* **Tasmai 'Ma' kārāya namaḥ śivāya** = Salutations to that Lord Shiva, who is embodied as the syllable 'Ma'.

**Translation:**
He who is beautifully adorned with sandalwood paste and the holy waters of the Mandakini river, who is the Great Lord of Nandi and the celestial hosts, and who is worshipped deeply with Mandara and manifold other flowers—my salutations to that Lord Shiva, who is the embodiment of the syllable 'Ma'.

---

#### ॥ Verse 3 (Syllable 'Shi') ॥
शिवाय गौरीवदनाब्जवृन्दसूर्याय दक्षाध्वरनाशकाय ।
श्रीनीलकण्ठाय वृषध्वजाय तस्मै 'शि' काराय नमः शिवाय ॥ ३॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Śivāya** = To the supremely auspicious One
* **Gaurī-vadanābja-vṛnda-sūryāya** = Who is like the sun that causes the lotus-like face of Mother Gauri to bloom
* **Dakṣādhvara-nāśakāya** = To the destroyer of the grand sacrifice (*adhvara*) of Prajapati Daksha
* **Śrī-nīlakaṇṭhāya** = To the glorious blue-throated Lord (who drank the Halahala poison)
* **Vṛṣadhvajāya** = To Him who has a bull (Nandi/Dharma) as His emblem on His flag
* **Tasmai 'Śi' kārāya namaḥ śivāya** = Salutations to that Lord Shiva, who is embodied as the syllable 'Shi'.

**Translation:**
He who is absolute auspiciousness, who is like the radiant sun that blossoms the lotus-like face of Mother Gauri, who destroyed the arrogant sacrifice of Daksha, who has a glorious blue throat, and whose banner bears the emblem of the bull—my salutations to that Lord Shiva, who is the embodiment of the syllable 'Shi'.

---

#### ॥ Verse 4 (Syllable 'Va') ॥
वसिष्ठकुम्भोद्भवगौतमार्यमुनीन्द्रदेवार्चितशेखराय ।
चन्द्रार्कवैश्वानरलोचनाय तस्मै 'व' काराय नमः शिवाय ॥ ४॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Vasiṣṭha-kumbhodbhava-gautama-ārya** = The noble/great (*ārya*) sages like Vashistha, Agastya (*Kumbhodbhava*), and Gautama
* **Munīndra-deva-ārcita-śekharāya** = To Him whose crown/head (and feet) is constantly worshipped by the best of sages and the gods
* **Candra-arka-vaiśvānara-locanāya** = To Him who has the moon, the sun (*arka*), and fire (*vaishvanara*) as His three eyes
* **Tasmai 'Va' kārāya namaḥ śivāya** = Salutations to that Lord Shiva, who is embodied as the syllable 'Va'.

**Translation:**
He whose crown and lotus feet are constantly worshipped by the gods and great, noble sages like Vashistha, Agastya, and Gautama, and who possesses the moon, the sun, and the fire as His three eyes—my salutations to that Lord Shiva, who is the embodiment of the syllable 'Va'.

---

#### ॥ Verse 5 (Syllable 'Ya') ॥
यज्ञस्वरूपाय जटाधराय पिनाकहस्ताय सनातनाय ।
दिव्याय देवाय दिगम्बराय तस्मै 'य' काराय नमः शिवाय ॥ ५॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Yajñasvarūpāya** = To Him who is the very embodiment of sacrifice / the Supreme Yajna Purusha
* **Jaṭādharāya** = To Him who wears matted locks of hair
* **Pinākahastāya** = To Him who holds the mighty bow named 'Pinaka' in His hand
* **Sanātanāya** = To Him who is the eternal, beginningless, and endless Lord
* **Divyāya devāya digambarāya** = To the divine, resplendent God who is clad in the directions
* **Tasmai 'Ya' kārāya namaḥ śivāya** = Salutations to that Lord Shiva, who is embodied as the syllable 'Ya'.

**Translation:**
He who is the very embodiment of the supreme sacrifice, who wears matted locks, who wields the Pinaka bow in His hand, and who is the eternal, timeless Lord; to the divine and resplendent God who is clad in the vast sky—my salutations to that Lord Shiva, who is the embodiment of the syllable 'Ya'.

---

#### ॥ Phalashruti (Fruits of Recitation) ॥
पञ्चाक्षरमिदं पुण्यं यः पठेच्छिवसन्निधौ ।
शिवलोकमवाप्नोति शिवेन सह मोदते ॥
ॐ शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning & Translation:**
* **Pañcākṣaramidaṃ puṇyaṃ** = This highly meritorious Panchakshara Stotram
* **Yaḥ paṭhet śivasannidhau** = Whoever recites it in the holy presence of Lord Shiva (near a Shivalinga or temple)
* **Śivalokam avāpnoti** = Attains the supreme abode of Shiva (*Shivaloka* / Kailasha) after death
* **Śivena saha modate** = And rejoices there in eternal bliss with Lord Shiva.
* **Om Śāntiḥ Śāntiḥ Śāntiḥ** = Om. May there be absolute peace from physical, divine, and internal suffering.

---

### 3. Benefits of Recitation (Phalashruti)

Regular recitation of this magnificent stotram, composed by Adi Guru Shankaracharya, grants the seeker the same immense spiritual merit as chanting the Panchakshari Mantra (Om Namah Shivaya). Singing this hymn with devotion in the presence of a Shivalinga or within a Shiva temple purifies the mind of all afflictions and sins. The devotee becomes a recipient of Lord Shiva's uncaused grace, and ultimately attains Shivaloka (Kailasha), experiencing eternal bliss and liberation.
