## Shri Shivopasana Mantrah (Taittiriya Aranyaka / Mahanarayana Upanishad)
**Complete Text, Word-by-Word Meaning, Translation & Significance**

---

### 1. Introduction & Spiritual Significance

The **Shri Shivopasana Mantrah** is a deeply revered and spiritually potent collection of Vedic mantras from the *Taittiriya Aranyaka* (Mahanarayana Upanishad - Tenth Prapathaka, Krishna Yajurveda). These sacred verses adore Lord Shiva in His five-faced *Panchabrahma* forms (Sadyojata, Vamadeva, Aghora, Tatpurusha, and Ishana), His infinite *Linga* manifestations, and as the Supreme Brahman, *Sarvatma Rudra* (the All-Pervading Soul of the Universe).

In Sanatana Dharma, these mantras are chanted during daily Shiva worship, *Rudrabhisheka*, consecration of a Shivalinga (*Linga-pratishtha*), and rituals for spiritual purification. Chanting these verses frees the seeker from fear, ignorance, and karmic impurities, bestowing supreme peace, self-realization, and ultimate liberation (*Moksha*).

* **Rishi:** Vedic Seers (Seers of the Supreme Brahman Sadashiva and Panchabrahma)
* **Presiding Deity:** Lord Shiva / Sadashiva (Sadyojata, Vamadeva, Aghora, Tatpurusha, Ishana, and Rudra)
* **Chandas:** Yajurvedic Prose (*Gadya*) and Trishtubh / Anushtubh Vedic meters
* **Purpose (Viniyoga):** For worship of Lord Shiva, self-purification, Rudrabhisheka, destruction of sins, and attainment of liberation.

### 2. Sanskrit Verses, Word-by-Word Meaning & English Translation

#### ॥ Mantra 1 (Linga-Namaskara Mantra) ॥
निध॑नपतये॒ नमः । निध॑नपतान्तिकाय॒ नमः । ऊर्ध्वाय॒ नमः । ऊर्ध्वलिङ्गाय॒ नमः । हिरण्याय॒ नमः । हिरण्यलिङ्गाय॒ नमः । सुवर्णाय॒ नमः । सुवर्णलिङ्गाय॒ नमः । दिव्याय॒ नमः । दिव्यलिङ्गाय॒ नमः । भवाय॒ नमः । भवलिङ्गाय॒ नमः । शर्वाय॒ नमः । शर्वलिङ्गाय॒ नमः । शिवाय॒ नमः । शिवलिङ्गाय॒ नमः । ज्वलाय॒ नमः । ज्वललिङ्गाय॒ नमः । आत्माय॒ नमः । आत्मलिङ्गाय॒ नमः । परमाय॒ नमः । परमलिङ्गाय॒ नमः । 
एतथ्सोमस्य॑ सूर्य॒स्य॒ सर्वलिङ्गग्ग्॑ स्थाप॒य॒ति॒ पाणिमन्त्रं॑ पवि॒त्रम् ॥ १ ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Nidhanapataye namaḥ** = Salutations to Lord Shiva, the Master of dissolution (*nidhana*)
* **Nidhanapatāntikāya namaḥ** = Salutations to Him who is the Lord of dissolution and the ender of death/time itself
* **Ūrdhvāya namaḥ... Ūrdhvaliṅgāya namaḥ** = Salutations to the Supreme/Transcendent (*ūrdhva*) Shiva and His transcendent Linga
* **Hiraṇyāya namaḥ... Hiraṇyaliṅgāya namaḥ** = Salutations to the golden/effulgent (*hiraṇya*) Shiva and His effulgent Linga
* **Suvarṇāya namaḥ... Suvarṇaliṅgāya namaḥ** = Salutations to Shiva of bright, beautiful luster (*suvarṇa*) and His bright Linga
* **Divyāya namaḥ... Divyaliṅgāya namaḥ** = Salutations to the Divine (*divya*) Shiva and His divine Linga
* **Bhavāya namaḥ... Bhavaliṅgāya namaḥ** = Salutations to Shiva, the source of existence (*bhava*), and His Linga of existence
* **Śarvāya namaḥ... Śarvaliṅgāya namaḥ** = Salutations to Shiva, the destroyer of sins/darkness (*śarva*), and His Linga
* **Śivāya namaḥ... Śivaliṅgāya namaḥ** = Salutations to the supremely auspicious (*śiva*) Lord and the Shivalinga
* **Jvalāya namaḥ... Jvalaliṅgāya namaḥ** = Salutations to the blazing/radiant (*jvala*) Shiva and His blazing Linga
* **Atmāya namaḥ... Atmaliṅgāya namaḥ** = Salutations to Shiva who is the indwelling Soul (*ātman*) and His Atma-Linga
* **Paramāya namaḥ... Paramaliṅgāya namaḥ** = Salutations to the Supreme Lord (*parama*) and His Supreme Linga
* **Etat somasya sūryasya sarvaliṅgam** = All this—the entire universe illuminated by the Moon (*soma*) and the Sun (*sūrya*)—is indeed the symbol (*Linga*) of Lord Shiva
* **Stāpayati pāṇimantraṃ pavitram** = Whoever holds this sacred mantra in his hand (*pāṇi*) and establishes/worships the Linga becomes supremely purified.

**Translation:**
Salutations to Lord Shiva—the Master of dissolution, the ender of time itself, the Supreme, the golden and effulgent One, the Divine, the source of existence, the destroyer of sins, the auspicious Lord, the blazing flame, the indwelling Self, and the Supreme God—and salutations to His sacred Linga manifestations. This entire universe, illuminated by the Sun and the Moon, is the symbol (*Linga*) of Shiva; whoever holds this holy mantra in his hand and performs the Linga worship becomes supremely purified.

#### ॥ Mantra 2 (Sadyojata Mantra — West Face) ॥
स॒द्योजा॒तं प्र॑पद्या॒मि॒ स॒द्योजा॒ताय॒ वै नमो॒ नमः॑ । 
भ॒वे भ॑वे॒ नाति॑भवे भवस्व॒ माम् । भ॒वोद्भ॑वाय॒ नमः॑ ॥ २ ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Sadyojātaṃ prapadyāmi** = I take refuge in the 'Sadyojata' (First-Born / Westward-facing) aspect of Lord Shiva
* **Sadyojātāya vai namo namaḥ** = Salutations again and again to that Sadyojata Shiva
* **Bhave bhave** = In every birth / in the cycle of existence (*samsara*)
* **Nātibhave** = Do not let me be caught in the endless cycle of rebirth (or spiritual ignorance)
* **Bhavasva mām** = Be gracious, benevolent, and present for me
* **Bhavodbhavāya namaḥ** = Salutations to Lord Shiva, the primal source of all existence (*bhavodbhava*).

**Translation:**
I take refuge in the Sadyojata (West-facing) aspect of Lord Shiva; salutations again and again to Lord Sadyojata. O Lord! In every birth and state of existence, be gracious unto me and liberate me from the endless cycle of rebirth. Salutations to Maheshwara, the primal source of all existence.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 3 (Vamadeva Mantra — North Face) ॥
वा॒म॒दे॒वाय॒ नमो᳚ ज्ये॒ष्ठाय॒ नम॑श्श्रे॒ष्ठाय॒ नमो॑ रु॒द्राय॒ नमः॒ काला॑य॒ नमः॒ कल॑विकरणाय॒ नमो॒ बल॑विकरणाय॒ नमो॒ बला॑य॒ नमो॒ बल॑प्रमथनाय॒ नम॒स्सर्व॑भूतदमनाय॒ नमो॑ म॒नोन्म॑नाय॒ नमः॑ ॥ ३ ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Vāmadevāya namaḥ** = Salutations to the beautiful, bounteous Lord 'Vamadeva'
* **Jyeṣṭhāya namaḥ** = Salutations to the Eldest / Primal One (*jyeṣṭha*)
* **Śreṣṭhāya namaḥ** = Salutations to the Most Excellent and Supreme (*śreṣṭha*)
* **Rudrāya namaḥ** = Salutations to Rudra, the dispeller of sorrows
* **Kālāya namaḥ** = Salutations to Him who is Time (*kāla*) and its ruler
* **Kalavikaraṇāya namaḥ** = Salutations to Him who causes changes in time and destroys modifications
* **Balavikaraṇāya namaḥ** = Salutations to Him who dispenses strength and vanquishes evil forces
* **Balāya namaḥ** = Salutations to Him who is pure Strength and Omnipotence (*bala*)
* **Balapramathanāya namaḥ** = Salutations to Him who crushes the power of the wicked
* **Sarvabhūtadamanāya namaḥ** = Salutations to the Ruler and Controller of all living beings
* **Manonmanāya namaḥ** = Salutations to Him who is the Lord of the transcendent state beyond the mind (*Manonmani*).

**Translation:**
Salutations to the Vamadeva (North-facing) aspect of Lord Shiva. Salutations to the Eldest, the Supreme, Lord Rudra, the Ruler of Time, the modifier of cycles, the omnipotent Lord of strength, the crusher of evil forces, the Controller of all living beings, and the Lord of the transcendent spiritual state beyond the mind (*Manonmani*).

---

#### ॥ Mantra 4 (Aghora Mantra — South Face) ॥
अ॒घोरे᳚भ्योऽथ॒ घोरे᳚भ्यो॒ घोर॒घोर॑तरेभ्यः । 
सर्वे᳚भ्यः सर्व॒शर्वे᳚भ्यो॒ नम॑स्ते अस्तु रु॒द्ररू॑पेभ्यः ॥ ४ ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Aghorebhyaḥ** = To those forms that are benevolent, gentle, and peaceful (*aghora*)
* **Atha ghorebhyaḥ** = And to those forms that are fierce and terrifying (*ghora*)
* **Ghoraghoratarebhyaḥ** = To those forms that are exceedingly fierce and destructive (*ghoraghoratara*)
* **Sarvebhyaḥ sarvaśarvebhyaḥ** = To all those and to every all-pervading, destroying (*śarva*) manifestation
* **Namaste astu rudrarūpebhyaḥ** = O Rudra! May my salutations be unto all Your divine forms.

**Translation:**
O Lord Rudra! May my salutations be unto all Your divine manifestations—to Your peaceful, gentle, and benevolent (*Aghora*) forms, to Your fierce and terrifying (*Ghora*) forms, and to Your exceedingly destructive, cosmic-dissolver forms. Salutations to every one of Your all-pervading, auspicious, and destructive aspects.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 5 (Tatpurusha / Rudra Gayatri Mantra — East Face) ॥
तत्पुरु॑षाय वि॒द्महे॑ महादे॒वाय॑ धीमहि । 
तन्नो॑ रुद्रः प्रचो॒दया᳚त् ॥ ५ ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Tatpuruṣāya vidmahe** = We realize and commune with that Supreme Person (*Tatpurusha Shiva*)
* **Mahādevāya dhīmahi** = We meditate deeply upon the Great Lord (*Mahadeva*)
* **Tat no rudraḥ pracodayāt** = May that Lord Rudra awaken, illuminate, and guide our intellect.

**Translation:**
We realize and know that Supreme Person, Lord Tatpurusha (East-facing); we meditate deeply upon the Great Lord Mahadeva. May Lord Rudra illuminate our intellect and inspire us toward truth, righteous action, and spiritual liberation.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 6 (Ishana Mantra — Upward Face) ॥
ईशानस्स॑र्वविद्या॒ना॒मीश्वरस्सर्व॑भूता॒नां॒ ब्रह्माऽधि॑पति॒र्ब्रह्म॒णोऽधि॑पति॒र्ब्रह्मा॑ शि॒वो मे॑ अस्तु सदाशि॒वोम् ॥ ६ ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Īśānaḥ sarvavidyānām** = He who is the absolute Ruler and Master of all knowledge and spiritual sciences (*Īśāna*)
* **Īśvaraḥ sarvabhūtānām** = He who is the Supreme Lord of all beings and elements
* **Brahmādhipatiḥ** = He who is the Lord of the Vedas (*Brahma*)
* **Brahmaṇo'dhipatiḥ** = He who is the Master of Brahma (the Creator)
* **Brahmā śivo me astu** = May that Supreme Brahman, Lord Shiva, be ever auspicious and benevolent unto me
* **Sadāśivom** = He is the eternal Sadashiva, embodied as the sacred syllable OM.

**Translation:**
He who is the absolute Master of all knowledge and sciences, the Supreme Lord of all living beings, the Sovereign of the Vedas, and the Ruler of Creator Brahma Himself—may that Ishana (Upward-facing), the Supreme Brahman Lord Shiva, be ever auspicious unto me. He is the eternal Sadashiva, the embodiment of OM.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 7 (Hiranyabahu Mantra — Sarvatma Namaskara) ॥
नमो हिरण्यबाहवे हिरण्यवर्णाय हिरण्यरूपाय हिरण्यपतयेऽम्बिकापतय उमापतये पशुपतये॑ नमो॒ नमः ॥ ७ ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Namo hiraṇyabāhave** = Salutations to Him of golden, effulgent arms
* **Hiraṇyavarṇāya** = Who is of golden, radiant complexion (*varṇa*)
* **Hiraṇyarūpāya** = Who is of golden, brilliant form (*rūpa*)
* **Hiraṇyapataye** = Who is the Lord of all wealth, spiritual luster, and abundance (*hiraṇya*)
* **Ambikāpataye** = Who is the Lord and Consort of Mother Ambika
* **Umāpataye** = Who is the Consort of Goddess Uma (Parvati)
* **Paśupataye namo namaḥ** = Salutations again and again to Pashupati, the Lord and Protector of all beings (*paśu*).

**Translation:**
Salutations to Lord Shiva of golden arms, golden luster, and effulgent form! Salutations to the Lord of all wealth and spiritual brilliance, the beloved Consort of Mother Ambika, the Consort of Goddess Uma, and the Supreme Lord Pashupati—the Protector of all souls. Salutations to You again and again.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 8 (Ritam Satyam — Supreme Brahman Stuti) ॥
ऋ॒तग्ं स॒त्यं प॑रं ब्र॒ह्म॒ पु॒रुषं॑ कृष्ण॒पिङ्ग॑लम् । 
ऊ॒र्ध्वरे॑तं-वि॑रूपा॒क्षं॒-वि॒श्वरू॑पाय॒ वै नमो॒ नमः॑ ॥ ८ ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Ritagṁ satyaṃ paraṃ brahma** = He who is the Divine Law (*Ṛta*), the Eternal Truth (*Satya*), and the Supreme Brahman
* **Puruṣaṃ kṛṣṇapiṅgalam** = Who is the Supreme Person of dark-blue (*kṛṣṇa* - Neelakantha) and golden-red (*piṅgala* - matted locks) form
* **Ūrdhvaretam** = Who is the supreme ascetic and master of celibacy/yogic energy (*ūrdhvaretas*)
* **Virūpākṣam** = Who has three (or all-seeing) eyes (*virūpākṣa*)
* **Viśvarūpāya vai namo namaḥ** = Salutations again and again to Him whose form is the entire universe (*Viśvarūpa*).

**Translation:**
Salutations again and again to Lord Shiva—who is the Eternal Truth, the Cosmic Law, and the Supreme Brahman! He is the Supreme Person of dark-blue throat and golden-red locks, the great celibate Yogin (*Urdhvaretas*), the three-eyed Lord (*Virupaksha*), and the One whose manifestation is the entire cosmos (*Vishvarupa*).

---

#### ॥ Mantra 9 (Sarvo Vai Rudrah — Rudra Sarvatmata Mantra) ॥
सर्वो॒ वै रु॒द्रस्तस्मै॑ रु॒द्राय॒ नमो॑ अस्तु॒ । 
पुरु॑षो॒ वै रु॒द्रस्सन्म॒हो नमो॒ नमः॑ ।
विश्वं॑ भू॒तं भुव॑नं चि॒त्रं ब॑हु॒धा जा॒तं जाय॑मानं च॒ यत् । 
सर्वो॒ ह्ये॑ष रु॒द्रस्तस्मै॑ रु॒द्राय॒ नमो॑ अस्तु॒ ॥
कद्रु॒द्राय॒ प्रचे॑तसे मी॒ढुष्ट॑माय॒ तव्य॑से । वो॒चेम॒ शन्त॑मग्ं हृ॒दे । 
सर्वो॒ ह्ये॑ष रु॒द्रस्तस्मै॑ रु॒द्राय॒ नमो॑ अस्तु॒ ॥ ९ ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Sarvo vai rudraḥ** = All this visible universe is indeed Lord Rudra
* **Tasmai rudrāya namo astu** = Salutations be unto that Lord Rudra
* **Puruṣo vai rudraḥ san maho namo namaḥ** = The indwelling Supreme Person is also Rudra; salutations again and again to that Great Lord
* **Viśvaṃ bhūtaṃ bhuvanaṃ citram** = This wondrous universe, all living beings, and all worlds/planes of existence
* **Bahudhā jātaṃ jāyamānaṃ ca yat** = Whatever has been born in manifold ways and whatever is being born
* **Sarvo hyeṣa rudraḥ...** = All this is verily Rudra; salutations be unto that Lord Rudra
* **Kadrudrāya pracetase** = To that praiseworthy, all-wise, and all-knowing Lord Rudra (*pracetas*)
* **Mīḍhuṣṭamāya tavyase** = Who is the most generous bestower of boons (*mīḍhuṣṭama*) and the most powerful (*tavyas*)
* **Vocema śantamagṁ hṛde** = We offer from the depths of our hearts the most peaceful and auspicious words of praise
* **Sarvo hyeṣa rudras-tasmai rudrāya namo astu** = Truly, all this is Rudra; salutations be unto that Lord Rudra.

**Translation:**
All this universe is indeed Lord Rudra; salutations be unto that Lord Rudra! The Supreme Person inhabiting all souls is also Rudra; salutations again and again to that Great Lord. This wondrous cosmos, all living beings, all worlds, and whatever has been born or is being born in manifold forms—all this is verily Rudra. To that all-knowing, most generous, and supreme Lord Rudra, we offer from the depths of our hearts the most peaceful and auspicious hymns of praise. Salutations be unto that all-pervading Lord Rudra.

---

#### ॐ शान्तिः॒ शान्तिः॒ शान्तिः॑ ॥
May there be absolute peace from physical, divine, and internal suffering.

---

### 3. Benefits of Recitation (Phalashruti)

Regular recitation of the **Shri Shivopasana Mantrah** during daily prayer or while performing *Rudrabhisheka* destroys all fear, illness, mental distress, and accumulated karmic impurities. By the grace of Lord Shiva's five-faced *Panchabrahma* manifestations (Sadyojata, Vamadeva, Aghora, Tatpurusha, and Ishana) and His all-pervading *Sarvatma Rudra* form, the seeker is blessed with divine knowledge, inner peace, spiritual strength, and ultimate liberation (*Moksha*).
