## Shri Rama Stotram
**Complete Text, Word-by-Word Meaning, Translation & Significance**

---

### 1. Introduction & Spiritual Significance

The 'Shri Rama Stotram' is a profoundly powerful and auspicious collection of hymns dedicated to Lord Rama, Mother Sita, Brother Lakshmana, the supreme devotee Hanuman, and the primal poet Sage Valmiki. This compilation features some of the most vibrant and revered verses from the 'Shri Ramaraksha Stotram' and traditional Ramayana Dhyana Shlokas.

In the Sanatana tradition, this stotram is not merely an expression of devotion; it is considered an invincible armor (*Kavacha*) that shields the seeker from all crises, fears, and adversities. Chanting it before embarking on a journey, during difficult times, or as part of daily worship invokes the ultimate protection of Lord Rama for the individual and their family.

* **Presiding Deity:** Lord Shri Rama (along with Mother Sita, Lakshmana, and Lord Hanuman)
* **Purpose (Viniyoga):** For the destruction of adversities, liberation from fear, protection during travel, and the attainment of pure, unalloyed devotion to Lord Rama.

---

### 2. Sanskrit Verses, Word-by-Word Meaning & English Translation

#### ॥ Verse 1 ॥
आपदामपहर्तारं दातारं सर्वसम्पदाम् ।
लोकाभिरामं श्रीरामं भूयो भूयो नमाम्यहम् ॥ १॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Āpadāmapahartāraṃ (Āpadām+apahartāraṃ)** = The remover/destroyer of adversities and crises
* **Dātāraṃ sarvasampadām** = The bestower of all wealth, prosperity, and success
* **Lokābhirāmaṃ** = The most beautiful and pleasing in all the worlds
* **Śrīrāmaṃ bhūyo bhūyo namāmyaham** = To that Lord Shri Rama, I bow again and again.

**Translation:**
I bow again and again to Lord Shri Rama, who is the remover of all adversities, the bestower of all wealth and prosperity, and the most beautiful and pleasing Lord in all the worlds.

---

#### ॥ Verse 2 ॥
आर्तानामार्तिहन्तारं भीतानां भयनाशनम् ।
द्विषतां कालदण्डं तं रामचन्द्रं नमाम्यहम् ॥ २॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Ārtānām-ārtihantāraṃ** = The destroyer of the agony of the distressed
* **Bhītānāṃ bhayanāśanam** = The destroyer of the fears of the frightened
* **Dviṣatāṃ kāladaṇḍaṃ** = Who is like the staff of Death (Yamaraja) to the enemies
* **Taṃ rāmacandraṃ namāmyaham** = I bow to that Lord Ramachandra.

**Translation:**
I bow to Lord Ramachandra, who destroys the sorrow of the distressed, eradicates the fears of the terrified, and who acts as the very staff of Time (Death) to His wicked enemies.

---

#### ॥ Verse 3 ॥
नमः कोदण्डहस्ताय सन्धीकृतशराय च ।
खण्डिताखिलदैत्याय रामायापन्निवारिणे ॥ ३॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Namaḥ kodaṇḍahastāya** = Salutations to Him who holds the Kodanda bow in His hand
* **Sandhīkṛta-śarāya ca** = And who has an arrow drawn and fixed on it
* **Khaṇḍitākhila-daityāya** = Who has destroyed all the demons
* **Rāmāyāpan-nivāriṇe** = To Lord Rama, the dispeller of adversities (salutations).

**Translation:**
Salutations to Lord Rama, who holds the great Kodanda bow in His hand with an arrow ever ready on its string, who has annihilated the entire horde of demons, and who is the ultimate dispeller of all calamities.

---

#### ॥ Verse 4 ॥
रामाय रामभद्राय रामचन्द्राय वेधसे ।
रघुनाथाय नाथाय सीतायाः पतये नमः ॥ ४॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Rāmāya rāmabhadrāya rāmacandrāya vedhase** = To Lord Rama, the auspicious Ramabhadra, the moon-like Ramachandra, and the Creator (Vedhas)
* **Raghunāthāya nāthāya** = To the Lord of the Raghu dynasty and the Master of the universe
* **Sītāyāḥ pataye namaḥ** = Salutations to the beloved Husband of Mother Sita.

**Translation:**
Salutations to the omnipresent Rama, the auspicious Ramabhadra, the delightful Ramachandra, the very Creator of the universe, the Lord of the Raghu dynasty, the Master of all, and the beloved Husband of Mother Sita.

---

#### ॥ Verse 5 ॥
अग्रतः पृष्ठतश्चैव पार्श्वतश्च महाबलौ ।
आकर्णपूर्णधन्वानौ रक्षेतां रामलक्ष्मणौ ॥ ५॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Agrataḥ pṛṣṭhataścaiva** = From the front and from behind
* **Pārśvataśca mahābalau** = And from both sides, the enormously mighty ones
* **Ākarṇapūrṇadhanvānau** = With their bowstrings pulled taut up to their ears (ready to strike)
* **Rakṣetāṃ rāmalakṣmaṇau** = May Rama and Lakshmana protect me.

**Translation:**
May the enormously mighty Lord Rama and Brother Lakshmana, with their bowstrings drawn taut up to their ears, always protect me from the front, from behind, and from both sides.

---

#### ॥ Verse 6 ॥
सन्नद्धः कवची खड्गी चापबाणधरो युवा ।
गच्छन् ममाग्रतो नित्यं रामः पातु सलक्ष्मणः ॥ ६॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Sannaddhaḥ kavacī khaḍgī** = Ever ready/prepared, wearing armor, and holding a sword
* **Cāpabāṇadharo yuvā** = The youthful Lord holding a bow and arrows
* **Gacchan mamāgrato nityaṃ** = Always walking ahead of me (on my path)
* **Rāmaḥ pātu salakṣmaṇaḥ** = May Lord Rama, along with Lakshmana, protect me.

**Translation:**
May the ever-ready, youthful Lord Rama, adorned with armor, wielding a sword, and holding His bow and arrows, always walk ahead of me on my path, accompanied by Lakshmana, and protect me.

---

#### ॥ Verse 7 ॥
श्रीराम राम रघुनन्दन राम राम ।
श्रीराम राम भरताग्रज राम राम ।
श्रीराम राम रणकर्कश राम राम ।
श्रीराम राम शरणं भव राम राम ॥ ७॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning & Translation:**
O Shri Rama! O Rama! O Joy of the Raghu dynasty (Raghunandana)!
O Shri Rama! O Rama! O Elder brother of Bharata (Bharatagraja)!
O Shri Rama! O Rama! O Fierce and formidable one in battle (Ranakarkasha)!
O Shri Rama! O Rama! Please be my ultimate refuge, O Rama!

---

#### ॥ Verse 8 ॥
श्रीरामचन्द्रचरणौ मनसा स्मरामि ।
श्रीरामचन्द्रचरणौ वचसा गृणामि ।
श्रीरामचन्द्रचरणौ शिरसा नमामि ।
श्रीरामचन्द्रचरणौ शरणं प्रपद्ये ॥ ८॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning & Translation:**
I remember the lotus feet of Lord Ramachandra with my mind.
I sing the glories of the lotus feet of Lord Ramachandra with my speech/words.
I bow my head in reverence to the lotus feet of Lord Ramachandra.
I take complete refuge in the lotus feet of Lord Ramachandra.

---

#### ॥ Verse 9 ॥
माता रामो मत्पिता रामचन्द्रः ।
स्वामी रामो मत्सखा रामचन्द्रः ।
सर्वस्वं मे रामचन्द्रो दयालुः ।
नान्यं जाने नैव जाने न जाने ॥ ९॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning & Translation:**
Lord Rama is my Mother, and Lord Ramachandra is my Father.
Lord Rama is my Master, and Lord Ramachandra is my Friend.
The compassionate Lord Ramachandra is my everything (my all-in-all).
I know of no other, absolutely no other, I know no one else but Him.

---

#### ॥ Verse 10 ॥
दक्षिणे लक्ष्मणो यस्य वामे तु जनकात्मजा ।
पुरतो मारुतिर्यस्य तं वन्दे रघुनन्दनम् ॥ १०॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Dakṣiṇe lakṣmaṇo yasya** = To whose right is Lakshmana
* **Vāme tu janakātmajā** = And to whose left is the daughter of Janaka (Sita)
* **Purato mārutiryasya** = In front of whom sits Maruti (Hanuman)
* **Taṃ vande raghunandanam** = I worship that joy of the Raghu dynasty (Rama).

**Translation:**
I worship Lord Rama, the jewel of the Raghu dynasty, who has Brother Lakshmana on His right, Mother Sita (daughter of Janaka) on His left, and the supreme devotee Hanuman seated right in front of Him.

---

#### ॥ Verse 11 ॥
लोकाभिरामं रणरङ्गधीरं राजीवनेत्रं रघुवंशनाथम् ।
कारुण्यरूपं करुणाकरं तं श्रीरामचन्द्रं शरणं प्रपद्ये ॥ ११॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Lokābhirāmaṃ raṇaraṅgadhīraṃ** = The most pleasing in the universe, and steadfast/courageous on the battlefield
* **Rājīvanetraṃ raghuvaṃśanātham** = The lotus-eyed one, the Lord of the Raghu dynasty
* **Kāruṇyarūpaṃ karuṇākaraṃ taṃ** = The very embodiment of compassion, and the bestower of mercy
* **Śrīrāmacandraṃ śaraṇaṃ prapadye** = I take refuge in that Lord Ramachandra.

**Translation:**
I take refuge in Lord Ramachandra, who is the most beautiful in the entire universe, steadfast and unwavering on the battlefield, lotus-eyed, the Lord of the Raghu dynasty, the very embodiment of compassion, and the supreme bestower of mercy.

---

#### ॥ Verse 12 (Hanuman Stuti) ॥
मनोजवं मारुततुल्यवेगं जितेन्द्रियं बुद्धिमतां वरिष्ठम् ।
वातात्मजं वानरयूथमुख्यं श्रीरामदूतं शरणं प्रपद्ये ॥ १२॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Manojavaṃ mārutatulyavegaṃ** = Who is as swift as the mind, and as fast as the wind
* **Jitendriyaṃ buddhimatāṃ variṣṭham** = Who has conquered His senses, and is the foremost among the wise/intellectuals
* **Vātātmajaṃ vānarayūthamukhyaṃ** = The son of the Wind God, the chief of the Vanara army
* **Śrīrāmadūtaṃ śaraṇaṃ prapadye** = I take refuge in the supreme messenger of Lord Rama (Hanuman).

**Translation:**
I take refuge in Lord Hanuman, the supreme messenger of Lord Rama, whose speed is as swift as the mind and as fast as the wind, who has absolute mastery over His senses, who is the greatest among the wise, the son of the Wind God, and the commander of the monkey army.

---

#### ॥ Verse 13 (Valmiki Stuti) ॥
कूजन्तं राम रामेति मधुरं मधुराक्षरम् ।
आरुह्य कविताशाखां वन्दे वाल्मीकिकोकिलम् ॥ १३॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Kūjantaṃ rāma rāmeti** = Sweetly cooing/singing "Rama, Rama"
* **Madhuraṃ madhurākṣaram** = The exceedingly sweet and melodious syllables
* **Āruhya kavitāśākhāṃ** = Sitting upon the branch of the tree of poetry
* **Vande vālmīkikokilam** = I worship the cuckoo-bird named Sage Valmiki.

**Translation:**
I bow in reverence to the sage Valmiki, who, sitting like a cuckoo on the branch of the tree of poetry, sweetly and continuously coos the melodious syllables "Rama, Rama."

---

#### ॥ Shanti Patha ॥
ॐ शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः ॥
May there be absolute peace from physical, divine, and internal suffering.

---

### 3. Benefits of Recitation (Phalashruti)

Chanting these perfected verses of the 'Shri Rama Stotram' in daily life protects the seeker from untimely death, severe illnesses, and accidents. Verses 5 and 6 specifically act as an invisible shield (*Kavacha*). When recited before stepping out of the house or during travel, Lord Rama and Lakshmana form an invisible barrier of protection around the devotee. This stotram cultivates profound mental peace, unshakeable confidence, and pure, supreme devotion (*Bhakti*) toward Lord Shri Rama.
