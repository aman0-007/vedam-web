## Shri Sharada Prarthana (Saraswati Stuti)
**Complete Text, Word-by-Word Meaning, Translation & Significance**

---

### 1. Introduction & Spiritual Significance

The 'Shri Sharada Prarthana' (also known as Saraswati Stuti) is a profoundly sweet and highly revered hymn dedicated to Goddess Saraswati, the presiding deity of knowledge, wisdom, intellect, and speech. In this prayer, the Goddess is invoked as 'Sharada' (the divine mother residing in the historic and sacred Sharada Peeth of Kashmir), and the seeker asks for the dispelling of ignorance and the blessing of supreme knowledge.

In Sanatana Dharma, this prayer is traditionally recited by students, scholars, and spiritual seekers before beginning their studies, academic pursuits, or any intellectual endeavor. The hymn beautifully declares that without the grace of knowledge and speech, the entire world would be like the living dead—mute and devoid of consciousness.

* **Presiding Deity:** Mother Sharada (Saraswati / Vagdevi)
* **Purpose (Viniyoga):** For the attainment of true knowledge, enhancement of memory and intellect (*Medha*), purification of speech, and the destruction of ignorance.

---

### 2. Sanskrit Verses, Word-by-Word Meaning & English Translation

#### ॥ Verse 1 ॥
नमस्ते शारदे देवि काश्मीरपुरवासिनि ।
त्वामहं प्रार्थये नित्यं विद्यादानं च देहि मे ॥ १॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Namaste Śārade devi** = Salutations to You, O Goddess Sharada!
* **Kāśmīrapuravāsini** = O You who reside in the city/land of Kashmir (the sacred Sharada Peeth)
* **Tvāmahaṃ prārthaye nityaṃ** = I pray to You constantly / every day
* **Vidyādānaṃ ca dehi me** = Please grant me the charity (gift) of knowledge.

**Translation:**
Salutations to You, O Goddess Sharada, who resides in the sacred land of Kashmir. I pray to You every single day; please bless me with the divine gift of knowledge.

---

#### ॥ Verse 2 ॥
या श्रद्धा धारणा मेधा वाग्देवी विधिवल्लभा ।
भक्तजिह्वाग्रसदना शमादिगुणदायिनी ॥ २॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Yā śraddhā dhāraṇā medhā** = She who is the very embodiment of faith (*shraddha*), retention/memory (*dharana*), and intellect (*medha*)
* **Vāgdevī vidhivallabhā** = The Goddess of Speech (*Vagdevi*) and the beloved consort of Lord Brahma (*Vidhi-vallabha*)
* **Bhakta-jihvāgra-sadanā** = Who resides on the tip (*agra*) of the tongues (*jihva*) of Her devotees
* **Śamādiguṇadāyinī** = Who bestows noble virtues such as inner peace (*shama*) and self-control.

**Translation:**
I bow to Her who is the very embodiment of faith, memory, and brilliant intellect; who is the Goddess of Speech and the beloved consort of Creator Brahma; who constantly dwells on the tips of the tongues of Her devotees, and who bestows noble virtues like inner peace and tranquility.

---

#### ॥ Verse 3 ॥
नमामि यामिनीनाथलेखालङ्कृतकुन्तलाम् ।
भवानीं भवसन्तापनिर्वापणसुधानदीम् ॥ ३॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Namāmi** = I bow down / I salute
* **Yāminīnātha-lekhālaṅkṛta-kuntalām** = To Her whose hair (*kuntala*) is adorned (*alankrita*) with the crescent line (*lekha*) of the moon, the lord of the night (*yamininatha*)
* **Bhavānīṃ** = To the Supreme Mother, Bhavani (the protector of the world)
* **Bhava-santāpa-nirvāpaṇa-sudhānadīm** = Who is like a river of nectar (*sudha-nadim*) that extinguishes/cools (*nirvapana*) the burning sorrows and miseries (*santapa*) of worldly existence (*bhava*).

**Translation:**
I bow to that Supreme Mother, whose beautiful hair is adorned with the crescent moon, and who is like a flowing river of cooling nectar that extinguishes the burning fires of worldly sorrows and miseries.

---

#### ॥ Verse 4 ॥
भद्रकाल्यै नमो नित्यं सरस्वत्यै नमो नमः ।
वेदवेदाङ्गवेदान्तविद्यास्थानेभ्य एव च ॥ ४॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Bhadrakālyai namo nityaṃ** = My eternal salutations to Goddess Bhadrakali (the auspicious power that protects)
* **Sarasvatyai namo namaḥ** = Salutations again and again to Goddess Saraswati
* **Veda-vedāṅga-vedānta-vidyā-sthānebhya eva ca** = And salutations to the Vedas, the Vedangas (auxiliary sciences), the Vedanta (Upanishads), and all the sacred seats/institutions of learning.

**Translation:**
My eternal salutations to the auspicious Goddess Bhadrakali. Salutations again and again to Goddess Saraswati. I also offer my reverent bows to the four Vedas, the six Vedangas, the Vedanta, and all the sacred seats and sources of knowledge.

---

#### ॥ Verse 5 ॥
ब्रह्मस्वरूपा परमा ज्योतिरूपा सनातनी ।
सर्वविद्याधिदेवी या तस्यै वाण्यै नमो नमः ॥ ५॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Brahmasvarūpā paramā** = She who is the Supreme (*parama*) embodiment of the Absolute Truth (*Brahman*)
* **Jyotirūpā sanātanī** = Who is of the form of divine light (*jyoti*) and is eternal/beginningless (*sanatani*)
* **Sarva-vidyādhidevī yā** = She who is the presiding deity of all branches of knowledge
* **Tasyai Vāṇyai namo namaḥ** = To that Goddess of Speech (*Vani*), my salutations again and again.

**Translation:**
She who is the Supreme embodiment of Absolute Brahman, who is eternal and of the form of divine light, and who is the presiding deity of all knowledge in the universe—to that Goddess of Speech (Vani), my salutations again and again.

---

#### ॥ Verse 6 ॥
यया विना जगत्सर्वं शश्वज्जीवन्मृतं भवेत् ।
ज्ञानाधिदेवी या तस्यै सरस्वत्यै नमो नमः ॥ ६॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Yayā vinā jagat-sarvaṃ** = Without whom this entire world
* **Śaśvaj-jīvanmṛtaṃ bhavet** = Would constantly (*shashvat*) be like the living dead (*jivan-mritam*)
* **Jñānādhidevī yā** = She who is the presiding deity of true knowledge
* **Tasyai Sarasvatyai namo namaḥ** = To that Goddess Saraswati, my salutations again and again.

**Translation:**
Without whose grace (of knowledge and expression) this entire world would merely exist as the living dead, to that presiding deity of knowledge, Goddess Saraswati, my salutations again and again.

---

#### ॥ Verse 7 ॥
यया विना जगत्सर्वं मूकमुन्मत्तवत्सदा ।
या देवी वागधिष्ठात्री तस्यै वाण्यै नमो नमः ॥ ७॥
॥ इति श्रीशारदाप्रार्थना समाप्ता ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Yayā vinā jagat-sarvaṃ** = Without whom this entire world
* **Mūkam-unmattavat-sadā** = Would always (*sada*) remain mute/dumb (*mukam*) and behave like a madman (*unmattavat*)
* **Yā devī vāgadhiṣṭhātrī** = That Goddess who is the supreme controller/ruler of speech (*Vak*)
* **Tasyai Vāṇyai namo namaḥ** = To that Goddess of Speech (*Vani*), my salutations again and again.

**Translation:**
Without whose grace this entire world would forever remain mute and devoid of reason, like a madman; to that presiding Goddess of Speech, Mother Vani, my salutations again and again.
(Here ends the Shri Sharada Prarthana)

---

### 3. Benefits of Recitation (Phalashruti)

Regularly reciting the 'Shri Sharada Prarthana' awakens one's hidden talents, sharpens the memory, and expands intellectual capacity. When a student, scholar, or seeker chants this prayer daily before beginning their studies or work, Goddess Saraswati blesses them with purity of speech, brilliant articulation, and effortless success in mastering all scriptures, arts, and sciences.
