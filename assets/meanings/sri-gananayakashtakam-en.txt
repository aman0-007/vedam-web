## Shri Gananayaka Ashtakam
**Complete Text, Word-by-Word Meaning, Translation & Significance**

---

### 1. Introduction & Spiritual Significance

'Shri Gananayaka Ashtakam' is an immensely powerful and sacred hymn consisting of eight verses (Ashtakam) dedicated to Lord Ganesha. It beautifully describes His divine physical form, His radiant ornaments, His immense valor, and His compassionate nature. 

In the Sanatana Dharma tradition, chanting this Ashtakam at the beginning of any auspicious undertaking, daily worship, or study ensures the removal of all obstacles. It invokes Lord Ganesha's blessings for a smooth path, deep wisdom, and ultimate success in all endeavors.

* **Presiding Deity:** Lord Shri Ganesha (Gananayaka - The Lord of the Ganas)
* **Purpose (Viniyoga):** For the destruction of all physical and mental obstacles, attainment of success in all tasks, and to be blessed with knowledge and wealth.

---

### 2. Sanskrit Verses (Roman Transliteration), Word-by-Word Meaning & English Translation

#### ॥ Verse 1 ॥
Ekadantaṃ mahākāyaṃ taptakāñcanasannibham ।
Lambodaraṃ viśālākṣaṃ vande'haṃ gaṇanāyakam ॥ 1॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Ekadantaṃ** = The one with a single tusk
* **Mahākāyaṃ** = Having a massive/huge body
* **Tapta-kāñcana-sannibham** = Shining with a radiant complexion like pure, molten gold
* **Lambodaraṃ** = Having a large belly (capable of digesting the universe's secrets)
* **Viśālākṣaṃ** = Having vast, expansive eyes
* **Vande'haṃ Gaṇanāyakam** = I bow down to (vande'haṃ) the Lord of the Ganas (Gaṇanāyakam)

**Translation:**
I bow down to Lord Gananayaka, who has a single tusk, a massive body, a radiant complexion resembling pure molten gold, a large belly, and vast, beautiful eyes.

---

#### ॥ Verse 2 ॥
Mauñjīkṛṣṇājinadharaṃ nāgayajñopavītinam ।
Bālendusukalāmauliṃ vande'haṃ gaṇanāyakam ॥ 2॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Mauñjī-kṛṣṇājina-dharaṃ** = Wearing a girdle made of Munja grass and the skin of a black deer
* **Nāga-yajñopavītinam** = Wearing a serpent (nāga) as a sacred thread (yajñopavītam)
* **Bālendu-sukalā-mauliṃ** = Whose head (mauli) is adorned with the beautiful crescent of the waxing moon (bālendu)
* **Vande'haṃ Gaṇanāyakam** = I bow down to the Lord of the Ganas

**Translation:**
I bow down to Lord Gananayaka, who wears a girdle of sacred Munja grass and a black deer skin, who wears a serpent as His holy thread, and whose head is beautifully adorned with a crescent moon.

---

#### ॥ Verse 3 ॥
Ambikāhṛdayānandaṃ mātṛbhiḥ pariveṣṭitam ।
Bhaktipriyaṃ madonmattaṃ vande'haṃ gaṇanāyakam ॥ 3॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Ambikā-hṛdaya-ānandaṃ** = He who brings immense joy (ānandaṃ) to the heart of Mother Ambika (Parvati)
* **Mātṛbhiḥ pariveṣṭitam** = Who is surrounded (pariveṣṭitam) by the Divine Mothers (mātṛbhiḥ)
* **Bhakti-priyaṃ** = Who is extremely fond of devotion (bhakti)
* **Mada-unmattaṃ** = Who is delightfully intoxicated in divine bliss
* **Vande'haṃ Gaṇanāyakam** = I bow down to the Lord of the Ganas

**Translation:**
I bow down to Lord Gananayaka, who brings immense delight to the heart of Mother Parvati, who is always surrounded by the Divine Mothers (Ashtamatrikas), who dearly loves the sincere devotion of His devotees, and who remains ever blissfully intoxicated in divine joy.

---

#### ॥ Verse 4 ॥
Citraratnavicitrāṅgaṃ citramālāvibhūṣitam ।
Citrarūpadharaṃ devaṃ vande'haṃ gaṇanāyakam ॥ 4॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Citra-ratna-vicitrāṅgaṃ** = Whose divine limbs (aṅga) are wondrously adorned with vivid, diverse gems (citra ratna)
* **Citra-mālā-vibhūṣitam** = Who is decorated with marvelous and colorful garlands
* **Citra-rūpa-dharaṃ devaṃ** = The deity who assumes various amazing and wondrous forms
* **Vande'haṃ Gaṇanāyakam** = I bow down to the Lord of the Ganas

**Translation:**
I bow down to the divine Lord Gananayaka, whose body is marvelously adorned with vivid and diverse jewels, who wears beautifully wondrous garlands, and who is capable of assuming many amazing divine forms.

---

#### ॥ Verse 5 ॥
Gajavaktraṃ suraśreṣṭhaṃ karṇacāmarabhūṣitam ।
Pāśāṅkuśadharaṃ devaṃ vande'haṃ gaṇanāyakam ॥ 5॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Gaja-vaktraṃ** = Having the face of an elephant
* **Sura-śreṣṭhaṃ** = The supreme and best among all gods
* **Karṇa-cāmara-bhūṣitam** = Adorned with large ears that resemble royal fly-whisks (chāmara)
* **Pāśāṅkuśa-dharaṃ devaṃ** = The deity who holds a noose (pāśa) and a goad/hook (aṅkuśa) in His hands
* **Vande'haṃ Gaṇanāyakam** = I bow down to the Lord of the Ganas

**Translation:**
I bow down to the supreme deity Lord Gananayaka, who has an elephant's face, who is the most exalted among all gods, whose large ears fan out like royal fly-whisks, and who holds a noose and a goad in His divine hands.

---

#### ॥ Verse 6 ॥
Mūṣakottamumāruhya devāsuramahāhave ।
Yoddhukāmaṃ mahāvīryaṃ vande'haṃ gaṇanāyakam ॥ 6॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Mūṣakottamam-āruhya** = Mounted upon His excellent mouse vehicle (mūṣaka)
* **Deva-asura-mahāhave** = In the great battles between the gods and the demons
* **Yoddhu-kāmaṃ** = Eager and ready to fight
* **Mahā-vīryaṃ** = Possessing immense valor and power
* **Vande'haṃ Gaṇanāyakam** = I bow down to the Lord of the Ganas

**Translation:**
I bow down to the immensely valiant Lord Gananayaka, who mounts His supreme mouse vehicle and is ever ready and eager to fight in the great cosmic battles between the gods and the demons.

---

#### ॥ Verse 7 ॥
Yakṣakinnaragandharvaiḥ siddhavidyādharaiḥ sadā ।
Stūyamānaṃ mahābāhuṃ vande'haṃ gaṇanāyakam ॥ 7॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Yakṣa-kinnara-gandharvaiḥ** = By the celestial Yakshas, Kinnaras, and Gandharvas
* **Siddha-vidyādharaiḥ sadā** = And always (sadā) by the Siddhas and Vidyadharas
* **Stūyamānaṃ** = Who is constantly being praised and worshipped
* **Mahā-bāhuṃ** = Having mighty, long arms
* **Vande'haṃ Gaṇanāyakam** = I bow down to the Lord of the Ganas

**Translation:**
I bow down to Lord Gananayaka of mighty arms, whose glories are constantly sung and praised by celestial beings including Yakshas, Kinnaras, Gandharvas, Siddhas, and Vidyadharas.

---

#### ॥ Verse 8 ॥
Sarvavighnaharaṃ devaṃ sarvavighnavivarjitam ।
Sarvasiddhipradātāraṃ vande'haṃ gaṇanāyakam ॥ 8॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Sarva-vighna-haraṃ devaṃ** = The deity who destroys (haraṃ) all obstacles (sarva vighna)
* **Sarva-vighna-vivarjitam** = Who is Himself completely free from/beyond all obstacles
* **Sarva-siddhi-pradātāraṃ** = The ultimate bestower (pradātāraṃ) of all success and supernatural powers (sarva siddhi)
* **Vande'haṃ Gaṇanāyakam** = I bow down to the Lord of the Ganas

**Translation:**
I bow down to Lord Gananayaka, the divine deity who eradicates all obstacles, who is Himself completely beyond any impediments, and who is the supreme bestower of all success, perfection, and spiritual powers.

---

#### ॥ Phalashruti (Verse 9) ॥
Gaṇāṣṭakamidaṃ puṇyaṃ yaḥ paṭhe satataṃ naraḥ ।
Siddhyanti sarvakāryāṇi vidyāvān dhanavān bhavet ॥ 9॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning & Translation:**
* **Gaṇāṣṭakam-idaṃ puṇyaṃ** = This highly auspicious and sacred Ganashtakam (hymn of eight verses)
* **Yaḥ paṭhe satataṃ naraḥ** = The person (naraḥ) who reads/recites (paṭhe) it continuously and regularly (satataṃ)
* **Siddhyanti sarvakāryāṇi** = All their tasks and endeavors are successfully accomplished (siddhyanti)
* **Vidyāvān dhanavān bhavet** = And they become blessed with immense knowledge/wisdom (vidyāvān) and wealth (dhanavān).

**Translation:**
Whoever regularly and with devotion recites this highly sacred and auspicious 'Ganashtakam', will have all their tasks and endeavors successfully fulfilled, and will be blessed with immense wisdom, knowledge, and wealth.

---

### 3. Benefits of Recitation (Phalashruti)

In the Sanatana Dharma tradition, the word 'Shri Ganesha' itself is synonymous with an 'auspicious beginning'. Chanting this sacred Ashtakam regularly, especially before starting any new venture, ritual, or study, yields the following benefits:

* **Removal of Obstacles (Vighna Vinash):** As the Lord is described as "Sarvavighnaharam," reciting this hymn at the beginning of any task automatically dispels all physical, mental, and cosmic hurdles.
* **Wisdom and Concentration (Sadbuddhi):** Lord Ganesha is the master of intellect and learning. Regular recitation enhances focus, clarity of thought, and bestows profound knowledge and academic success (*Vidyavan*).
* **Success and Prosperity (Sarvakarya Siddhi):** By meditating on the Lord's radiant form and His ability to grant all perfections (*Sarva-siddhi-pradataram*), the seeker is blessed with positive energy, wealth (*Dhanavan*), and absolute success in all life endeavors.
