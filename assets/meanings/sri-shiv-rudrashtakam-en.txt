## ॥ Shri Shiva Rudrashtakam Stotram ॥
**Complete Text, Word-by-Word Meaning, Translation & Significance**

---

### 1. Introduction & Spiritual Significance

The 'Shri Shiva Rudrashtakam' is a highly energetic, vibrant, and powerful hymn dedicated to Lord Shiva, composed by the great saint-poet Goswami Tulsidas. It is found in the *Uttara Kanda* of the epic *Ramacharitamanas*. According to the scripture, this hymn was sung by the Guru of Kakabhushundi (who was a Brahmin) to appease Lord Shiva and seek forgiveness for his disciple's offenses. Pleased by this soulful prayer, Lord Shiva immediately manifested and granted his blessings.

In the Sanatana tradition, the Rudrashtakam is considered one of the most unfailing and quickest ways to please Lord Ashutosh (Shiva, who is easily pleased). It presents a breathtakingly beautiful description of both the *Saguna* (manifest, compassionate, beautiful) and *Nirguna* (unmanifest, formless, infinite) aspects of Lord Shiva.

* **Composer:** Goswami Tulsidas
* **Presiding Deity:** Lord Shiva (Rudra)
* **Purpose (Viniyoga):** To seek the immediate grace of Lord Shiva, for liberation from the sufferings of birth and death, and to attain supreme peace and joy in life.

---

### 2. Sanskrit Verses, Word-by-Word Meaning & English Translation

#### ॥ Verse 1 ॥
नमामीशमीशान निर्वाणरूपं ।
विभुं व्यापकं ब्रह्मवेदस्वरूपम् ॥
निजं निर्गुणं निर्विकल्पं निरीहं ।
चिदाकाशमाकाशवासं भजेऽहम् ॥ १॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Namāmīśamīśāna (Namāmi+īśam+īśāna)** = I bow to the Lord (Isham), Ishana (Shiva)
* **Nirvāṇarūpaṃ** = Whose very form is liberation/Moksha (Nirvana)
* **Vibhuṃ vyāpakaṃ** = Who is omnipotent (Vibhu) and all-pervading (Vyapaka)
* **Brahmavedasvarūpam** = Who manifests in the form of Brahman and the Vedas
* **Nijaṃ nirguṇaṃ nirvikalpaṃ nirīhaṃ** = Who is established in His own Self (Nija), beyond the three Gunas (Nirguna), free from change/duality (Nirvikalpa), and desireless (Niriha)
* **Cidākāśamākāśavāsaṃ bhaje'ham** = I worship Him who is the sky of pure consciousness (Chidakasha) and who has the sky as His dwelling/garment (Akashavasam).

**Translation:**
I bow to the Lord of all, Ishana, whose very form is absolute liberation. He is the omnipotent, all-pervading Lord, manifesting as the Supreme Brahman and the Vedas. I worship Him who is established in His own pristine Self, devoid of material qualities, beyond all dualities, and entirely desireless. I worship Him who is the infinite sky of pure consciousness and who dwells in the vast sky itself.

---

#### ॥ Verse 2 ॥
निराकारमोंकारमूलं तुरीयं ।
गिरा ज्ञान गोतीतमीशं गिरीशम् ॥
करालं महाकाल कालं कृपालं ।
गुणागार संसारपारं नतोऽहम् ॥ २॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Nirākāramoṅkāramūlaṃ turīyaṃ** = Who is formless (Nirakara), the root of Omkara (OM), and the fourth state of consciousness (Turiya)
* **Girā jñāna gotītamīśaṃ girīśam** = Who is beyond speech (Gira), knowledge, and the senses (Go-atita); the Lord of the mountains (Girisham)
* **Karālaṃ mahākāla kālaṃ kṛpālaṃ** = Who is fierce (Karala), the death of Time itself (Mahakala), yet supremely merciful (Kripalam)
* **Guṇāgāra saṃsārapāraṃ nato'ham** = The abode of all virtues (Gunagara), who ferries one across the ocean of Samsara; to Him I bow down (nato'ham).

**Translation:**
I bow to the Lord who is formless, the profound root of the sacred syllable 'OM', and the transcendental fourth state (Turiya). I bow to the Lord of the mountains, who is entirely beyond the reach of speech, worldly knowledge, and the senses. I bow to Him who is fierce to the wicked, who is the Supreme Annihilator (Mahakala) of Time itself, yet bounds in mercy for His devotees. I bow to the abode of all virtues, who carries souls across the ocean of worldly existence.

---

#### ॥ Verse 3 ॥
तुषाराद्रि संकाश गौरं गभीरं ।
मनोभूत कोटि प्रभा श्री शरीरम् ॥
स्फुरन्मौलि कल्लोलिनी चारु गङ्गा ।
लसद्भालबालेन्दु कण्ठे भुजङ्गा ॥ ३॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Tuṣārādri saṃkāśa gauraṃ gabhīraṃ** = Who is as white/fair (Gaura) as the snow-clad mountains (Tusharadri) and profoundly deep/majestic
* **Manobhūta koṭi prabhā śrī śarīram** = The radiant beauty (Sri) of whose body equals the brilliance of millions of Kamadevas (Gods of Love)
* **Sphuranmauli kallolinī cāru gaṅgā** = On whose head (Mauli) sparkles the beautiful, rippling river Ganga
* **Lasadbhālabālendu kaṇṭhe bhujaṅgā** = On whose forehead shines the crescent moon (Balendu), and around whose neck slither serpents (Bhujanga).

**Translation:**
I worship the Lord who is as majestic and flawlessly white as the snow-capped Himalayas. The divine radiance of His beautiful body outshines the brilliance of millions of Gods of Love (Kamadeva). The charming and undulating waves of the holy river Ganga sparkle playfully upon His matted hair. A beautiful crescent moon adorns His radiant forehead, and majestic serpents coil gracefully around His neck.

---

#### ॥ Verse 4 ॥
चलत्कुण्डलं भ्रू सुनेत्रं विशालं ।
प्रसन्नाननं नीलकण्ठं दयालम् ॥
मृगाधीशचर्माम्बरं मुण्डमालं ।
प्रियं शङ्करं सर्वनाथं भजामि ॥ ४॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Calatkuṇḍalaṃ bhrū sunetraṃ viśālaṃ** = Whose earrings are swaying, possessing beautiful eyebrows and large, enchanting eyes
* **Prasannānanaṃ nīlakaṇṭhaṃ dayālam** = Whose face is ever cheerful/blissful, whose throat is blue (Nilakantha), and who is extremely compassionate
* **Mṛgādhīśacarmāmbaraṃ muṇḍamālaṃ** = Who wears the hide/skin (Charma) of the king of beasts (lion/tiger) as a garment, and a garland of skulls (Mundamala)
* **Priyaṃ śaṅkaraṃ sarvanāthaṃ bhajāmi** = I worship the beloved Lord Shankara, the Master of all.

**Translation:**
I worship Lord Shankara, whose beautiful earrings sway gently, who possesses exquisitely beautiful eyebrows and large, captivating eyes. His countenance is perpetually cheerful and blissful, His throat is blue (from consuming the worldly poison), and He is an ocean of compassion. Clad in the majestic skin of a tiger and adorned with a garland of skulls, I worship that beloved Lord of all beings.

---

#### ॥ Verse 5 ॥
प्रचण्डं प्रकृष्टं प्रगल्भं परेशं ।
अखण्डं अजं भानुकोटिप्रकाशम् ॥
त्रयः शूल निर्मूलनं शूलपाणिं ।
भजेऽहं भवानीपतिं भावगम्यम् ॥ ५॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Pracaṇḍaṃ prakṛṣṭaṃ pragalbhaṃ pareśaṃ** = Who is fierce/terrible (Prachanda), exalted (Prakrishta), bold/resolute (Pragalbha), and the Supreme Lord (Paresham)
* **Akhaṇḍaṃ ajaṃ bhānukoṭiprakāśam** = Who is indivisible (Akhanda), unborn (Ajam), and radiant like millions of suns
* **Trayaḥ śūla nirmūlanaṃ śūlapāṇiṃ** = Who completely uproots/destroys the three types of miseries (physical, divine, worldly), holding a trident in His hand (Shulapanim)
* **Bhaje'haṃ bhavānīpatiṃ bhāvagamyam** = I worship the husband of Mother Bhavani, who can only be reached/attained through pure love and devotion (Bhavagamyam).

**Translation:**
I worship the Supreme Lord who is terrifying, exalted, resolute, and the highest Sovereign. He is indivisible, unborn, and shines with the blinding radiance of millions of suns. Holding the mighty trident in His hand, He completely uproots the three-fold agonies of human existence. I worship the Lord of Goddess Bhavani, who can be attained only through the purity of heartfelt love and devotion.

---

#### ॥ Verse 6 ॥
कलातीत कल्याण कल्पान्तकारी ।
सदा सज्जनानन्ददाता पुरारी ॥
चिदानन्द सन्दोह मोहापहारी ।
प्रसीद प्रसीद प्रभो मन्मथारी ॥ ६॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Kalātīta kalyāṇa kalpāntakārī** = Who is beyond time/phases (Kalatita), the embodiment of auspiciousness (Kalyana), and the bringer of the end of the cosmic cycle (Kalpantakari)
* **Sadā sajjanānandadātā purārī** = Who always bestows joy upon the virtuous, and is the enemy/destroyer of the demon Tripura (Purari)
* **Cidānanda sandoha mohāpahārī** = Who is a massive repository of pure consciousness and bliss, and the dispeller of delusion (Moha)
* **Prasīda prasīda prabho manmathārī** = Be pleased with me, be pleased with me, O Lord, the enemy of Kamadeva (Manmathari).

**Translation:**
O Lord who is beyond the bounds of time and form, You are the very essence of auspiciousness, and the ultimate destroyer of the universe at the end of the cosmic cycle. You continuously bestow boundless joy upon the righteous, and You are the vanquisher of the demon Tripura. You are the dense ocean of pure consciousness and bliss, removing the dark clouds of worldly delusion. Be pleased with me, be pleased with me, O Lord, the vanquisher of the God of Lust (Kamadeva)!

---

#### ॥ Verse 7 ॥
न यावद् उमानाथ पादारविन्दं ।
भजन्तीह लोके परे वा नराणाम् ॥
न तावत्सुखं शान्ति सन्तापनाशं ।
प्रसीद प्रभो सर्वभूताधिवासम् ॥ ७॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Na yāvad Umānātha pādāravindaṃ** = As long as the lotus feet of the Lord of Uma (Shiva)
* **Bhajantīha loke pare vā narāṇām** = Are not worshipped by men in this world or in the next
* **Na tāvatsukhaṃ śānti santāpanāśaṃ** = Until then, there can be no happiness, peace, or destruction of sufferings
* **Prasīda prabho sarvabhūtādhivāsam** = Therefore, be pleased with me, O Lord who resides within the hearts of all living beings!

**Translation:**
As long as human beings do not devotedly worship the lotus feet of the Lord of Goddess Uma (Shiva), they can never find true happiness, inner peace, or the end of their worldly agonies—neither in this world nor in the next. Therefore, O Supreme Lord who dwells within the hearts of all living beings, please be gracious and pleased with me!

---

#### ॥ Verse 8 ॥
न जानामि योगं जपं नैव पूजां ।
नतोऽहं सदा सर्वदा शम्भु तुभ्यम् ॥
जराजन्म दुःखौघ तातप्यमानं ।
प्रभो पाहि आपन्नमामीश शम्भो ॥ ८॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Na jānāmi yogaṃ japaṃ naiva pūjāṃ** = I know neither Yoga, nor Japa (chanting), nor the proper methods of worship (Puja)
* **Nato'haṃ sadā sarvadā śambhu tubhyam** = I simply bow down to You, always and forever, O Shambhu
* **Jarājanma duḥkhaugha tātpyamānaṃ** = I am burning/tormented by the immense multitude of sorrows associated with old age and the cycle of birth
* **Prabho pāhi āpannāmāmīśa śambho** = O Lord! Please protect (Pahi) this afflicted/distressed soul, I bow to You, O Lord Shambho.

**Translation:**
O Lord! I do not know the practices of Yoga, the chanting of mantras, or the intricate rituals of worship. O Shambhu, I only know how to bow down to You, always and forever. I am constantly burning in the agonizing fire of old age, birth, and the endless miseries of this worldly existence. O Lord! O Isha! O Shambhu! Please rescue and protect this distressed soul, I bow to You.

---

#### ॥ Phalashruti ॥
रुद्राष्टकमिदं प्रोक्तं विप्रेण हरतोषये ।
ये पठन्ति नरा भक्त्या तेषां शम्भुः प्रसीदति ॥
ॐ शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning & Translation:**
This Rudrashtakam was originally uttered by a Vipra (Brahmin) for the appeasement (pleasing) of Lord Hara (Shiva). Those devotees who recite this hymn with deep faith and devotion, Lord Shambhu becomes immensely pleased with them.
(Om Shantih — May there be peace from physical, divine, and internal afflictions).

---

### 3. Benefits of Recitation (Phalashruti)

The 'Shri Shiva Rudrashtakam' is a highly potent prayer that instantly invokes the mercy of Lord Shiva. Regular recitation of this stotram shields the devotee from the deepest worldly agonies, the fear of death, and the sufferings of the birth-and-death cycle. If a seeker is tormented by deep inner turmoil or severe life crises, singing this hymn with a pure, surrendered heart brings profound mental peace. It effectively destroys ego and ignorance, blessing the seeker with the supreme devotion of Lord Shiva in both His manifest (Saguna) and unmanifest (Nirguna) forms.
