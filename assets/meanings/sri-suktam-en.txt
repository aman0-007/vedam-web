## ॥ Sri Suktam ॥
**Complete Text, Word-by-Word Meaning, Translation & Significance**

---

### 1. Introduction & Spiritual Significance

'Sri Suktam' is the most ancient, authentic, and powerful Vedic hymn dedicated to the worship of Goddess Mahalakshmi (Shri). Found in the Khilani (appendix) of the Rig Veda, it consists of 15 core verses (Mantras), followed by verses of *Phalashruti* (benefits of chanting) and *Dhyana* (meditation) in verses 16 to 29.

In the Sanatana tradition, chanting the Sri Suktam with its proper Vedic intonations (*Svaras* - Udatta, Anudatta, and Svarita) and offering oblations (Homa/Yajna) with its mantras is considered the ultimate spiritual remedy to attain wealth, prosperity, and supreme peace. In these verses, the seeker prays to Agni (the Fire God, Jataveda) to invoke Goddess Lakshmi and establish Her permanently in their home. This sacred hymn completely eradicates poverty (Alakshmi), debt, and misfortune.

* **Veda:** Rig Veda (Khilani)
* **Presiding Deity:** Goddess Mahalakshmi (Shri) & Lord Agni (Jataveda)
* **Purpose (Viniyoga):** For the destruction of poverty and misfortune (Alakshmi), freedom from debts, attainment of endless wealth, good health, prosperity, and ultimately, liberation.

### 2. Sanskrit Verses with Svaras, Word-by-Word Meaning & Translation

#### ॥ Mantra 1 ॥
ॐ हिर॑ण्यवर्णां॒ हरि॑णीं सु॒वर्ण॑रज॒तस्र॑जाम् । च॒न्द्रां हि॒रण्म॑यीं ल॒क्ष्मीं जात॑वेदो म॒ आव॑ह ॥ १ ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Hiraṇyavarṇāṃ** = Of golden color
* **Hariṇīṃ** = Beautiful like a doe / The destroyer of sorrows
* **Suvarṇarajatasrajām** = Adorned with garlands of gold and silver
* **Candrāṃ hiraṇmayīṃ Lakṣmīṃ** = Goddess Lakshmi, who is radiant like the moon and made of gold
* **Jātavedo ma āvaha** = O Agni (Jataveda)! Invoke/bring Her to me.

**Translation:**
O Agni! Invoke for me that Goddess Lakshmi, who is of golden complexion, beautiful like a doe, and the destroyer of all sorrows. She who is adorned with garlands of gold and silver, who is radiant like the moon, and who is the embodiment of pure gold.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 2 ॥
तां म॒ आव॑ह॒ जात॑वेदो ल॒क्ष्मीमन॑पगा॒मिनी᳚म् । यस्यां॒ हिर॑ण्यं वि॒न्देयं॒ गामश्वं॒ पुरु॑षान॒हम् ॥ २ ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Tāṃ ma āvaha Jātavedo** = O Agni! Invoke Her for me
* **Lakṣmīmanapagāminīm** = That Goddess Lakshmi who never departs (who is stable and eternal)
* **Yasyāṃ hiraṇyaṃ vindeyaṃ** = By whose arrival I may obtain abundant gold/wealth
* **Gāmaśvaṃ puruṣānaham** = Cows, horses, and men (progeny, servants, and friends).

**Translation:**
O Agni! Invoke for me that unfailing, ever-abiding Goddess Lakshmi. By Her grace and arrival, may I obtain abundant gold, cows, horses, and a great retinue of family and followers.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 3 ॥
अ॒श्व॒पू॒र्वा र॑थम॒ध्यां ह॒स्तिना᳚द-प्र॒बोधि॑नीम् । श्रियं॑ दे॒वीमुप॑ह्वये॒ श्रीर्मा᳚ दे॒वीर्जु॑षताम् ॥ ३ ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Aśvapūrvā rathamadhyāṃ** = Who has horses in the front and is seated in the middle of the chariot
* **Hastināda-prabodhinīm** = Who is awakened and delighted by the trumpeting of elephants
* **Śriyaṃ devīmupahvaye** = I invoke that Supreme Goddess Shri (Lakshmi)
* **Śrīrmā devīrjuṣatām** = May that Goddess Shri be pleased with me and bless me.

**Translation:**
I invoke the Supreme Goddess Shri, whose chariot is drawn by horses in the front, who is majestically seated in the center, and whose arrival is heralded by the joyous trumpeting of elephants. May that Goddess Shri be pleased with me and reside in my home.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 4 ॥
कां॒ सो॒स्मि॒तां हि॑रण्यप्रा॒कारा॑मा॒र्द्रां ज्वल॑न्तीं तृ॒प्तां त॒र्पय॑न्तीम् । प॒द्मे॒ स्थि॒तां प॒द्मव॑र्णां॒ तामि॒होप॑ह्वये॒ श्रियम्॥ ४ ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Kāṃ sosmitāṃ** = Who is the embodiment of Brahman ('Ka') and bears a sweet smile
* **Hiraṇyaprākārāmārdrāṃ** = Enveloped in a fortress of gold, and moist with deep compassion (Ardra)
* **Jvalantīṃ tṛptāṃ tarpayantīm** = Radiant, self-fulfilled/satisfied, and satisfying all Her devotees
* **Padme sthitāṃ padmavarṇāṃ** = Seated upon a lotus, and having the beautiful hue of a lotus
* **Tāmihopahvaye Śriyam** = I invoke that Goddess Shri here.

**Translation:**
I invoke that Goddess Shri, who is the very embodiment of the Supreme Brahman, bearing a sweet smile. She is enveloped in golden radiance, Her heart melts with compassion, and She is fiercely radiant. She is entirely self-fulfilled and fulfills the desires of all Her devotees. She is seated upon a lotus and bears the beautiful pink hue of a lotus.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 5 ॥
च॒न्द्रां प्र॑भा॒सां य॒शसा॒ ज्वल॑न्तीं॒ श्रियं॑ लो॒के दे॒वजु॑ष्टामुदा॒राम् । तां प॒द्मिनी॑मीं॒ शर॑णम॒हं प्रप॑द्येऽल॒क्ष्मीर्मे॑ नश्यतां॒ त्वां वृ॑णे ॥ ५ ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Candrāṃ prabhāsāṃ yaśasā jvalantīṃ** = Radiant like the moon, and shining brilliantly with Her own fame
* **Śriyaṃ loke devajuṣṭāmudārām** = The Goddess Shri, who is worshipped by the gods in this world and is exceptionally generous
* **Tāṃ padminīmīṃ śaraṇamahaṃ prapadye** = I take refuge in that Lotus-Goddess (Padmini)
* **Alakṣmīrme naśyatāṃ tvāṃ vṛṇe** = May my poverty (Alakshmi) be destroyed; for this, I seek You.

**Translation:**
I take refuge in the Lotus-Goddess, who is as cooling and radiant as the moon, who blazes with Her own glorious fame, who is adored by the gods, and who is supremely generous. O Mother! I seek Your refuge so that my poverty and misfortune (Alakshmi) may be utterly destroyed.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 6 ॥
आ॒दि॒त्यव॑र्णे॒ तप॒सोऽधि॑जा॒तो वन॒स्पति॒स्तव॑ वृ॒क्षोऽथ बि॒ल्वः । तस्य॒ फला॑नि॒ तप॒सानु॑दन्तु मा॒यान्त॑रा॒याश्च॑ बा॒ह्या अ॑ल॒क्ष्मीः ॥ ६ ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Ādityavarṇe** = O Goddess of sun-like radiant complexion!
* **Tapaso'dhijāto vanaspatistava vṛkṣo'tha bilvaḥ** = From Your austerity (Tapas), the lord of trees, the Bilva tree, was born
* **Tasya phalāni tapasānudantu** = May the fruits of that tree, by Your grace/austerity, drive away
* **Māyāntarāyāśca bāhyā Alakṣmīḥ** = My inner ignorance (Maya) and outer poverty (Alakshmi).

**Translation:**
O Goddess who shines with the radiance of the Sun! It was through Your divine austerity that the king of trees, the Bilva tree, was born. May the sacred fruits of that Bilva tree, by Your grace, entirely destroy my inner darkness and ignorance (Maya) as well as my external poverty and misfortune (Alakshmi).

---

#### ॥ Mantra 7 ॥
उपै॑तु॒ मां दे॑वस॒खः की॒र्तिश्च॒ मणि॑ना स॒ह । प्रा॒दु॒र्भू॒तोऽस्मि॑ राष्ट्रे॒ऽस्मिन् की॒र्तिमृ॑द्धिं द॒दातु॑ मे ॥ ७ ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Upaitu māṃ devasakhaḥ** = May the friend of the gods (Kubera, the Lord of Wealth) come to me
* **Kīrtiśca maṇinā saha** = Along with fame (Kirti) and the wish-fulfilling jewel (Chintamani)
* **Prādurbhūto'smi rāṣṭre'smin** = I am born in this realm/world
* **Kīrtimṛddhiṃ dadātu me** = Therefore, grant me fame and abundant prosperity.

**Translation:**
O Mother! Since I am born in this realm, please bestow Your grace upon me. May Kubera (the friend of Lord Shiva and keeper of wealth) come to me, bringing along fame and the wish-fulfilling jewel (Chintamani). Please grant me immense glory and prosperity.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 8 ॥
क्षुत्पि॑पा॒साम॑लां ज्ये॒ष्ठाम॑ल॒क्ष्मीं ना॑शया॒म्यहम् । अभू॑ति॒मस॑मृद्धिं॒ च सर्वां॒ निर्णु॑द मे॒ गृहा᳚त् ॥ ८ ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Kṣutpipāsāmalāṃ** = Tainted by the impurities of hunger and thirst
* **Jyeṣṭhāmalakṣmīṃ nāśayāmyaham** = I destroy Jyeshtha (the elder sister of Lakshmi), which is poverty/misfortune
* **Abhūtimasamṛddhiṃ ca sarvāṃ** = Lack of wealth, failure, and all forms of poverty
* **Nirṇuda me gṛhāt** = Drive them entirely out of my home.

**Translation:**
Through my devotion, I destroy the entity of poverty (Alakshmi/Jyeshtha), who is characterized by the impurities of hunger and thirst. O Mother! Please banish all forms of misfortune, failure, and poverty from my home forever.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 9 ॥
ग॒न्ध॒द्वा॒रां दु॑राध॒र्षां॒ नि॒त्यपु॑ष्टां करी॒षिणी᳚म् । ई॒श्वरींग्॑ सर्व॑भूता॒नां॒ तामि॒होप॑ह्वये॒ श्रियम्॥ ९ ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Gandhadvārām** = Who is perceived through the door of fragrance (where there is purity)
* **Durādharṣāṃ** = Who is invincible and unapproachable by evil
* **Nityapuṣṭāṃ karīṣiṇīm** = Who is eternally nourished and abundant in cattle/wealth (Karishinim)
* **Īśvarīṃ sarvabhūtānāṃ** = The Supreme Sovereign of all living beings
* **Tāmihopahvaye Śriyam** = I invoke that Goddess Shri here.

**Translation:**
I invoke the Supreme Goddess Shri, who is perceived through fragrance and purity, who is invincible, who is eternally fully nourished, who is abundantly rich in cattle and agriculture, and who is the absolute Sovereign of all living beings in the universe.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 10 ॥
मन॑सः॒ काम॒माकू॑तिं वा॒चः स॒त्यम॑शीमहि । प॒शू॒नां रू॒पमन्न॑स्य॒ मयि॒ श्रीः श्र॑यतां॒ यशः॑ ॥ १० ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Manasaḥ kāmamākūtiṃ** = The desires and the holy intentions of the mind
* **Vācaḥ satyamaśīmahi** = And the truth of speech, may we attain
* **Paśūnāṃ rūpamannasya** = Beautiful forms of cattle and abundance of food
* **Mayi Śrīḥ śrayatāṃ yaśaḥ** = May Goddess Shri and fame take permanent refuge in me.

**Translation:**
O Mother! May my mind's desires and pure intentions be fulfilled, and may my words always manifest as truth. May I attain an abundance of cattle and plentiful food. May Goddess Shri and glorious fame always reside within me (and my home).

---

#### ॥ Mantra 11 ॥
क॒र्दमे॑न प्र॑जाभू॒ता॒ म॒यि॒ सम्भ॑व क॒र्दम । श्रियं॑ वा॒सय॑ मे कु॒ले मा॒तरं॑ पद्म॒मालि॑नीम् ॥ ११ ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Kardamena prajābhūtā** = She (Lakshmi) appeared through sage Kardama (her mind-born son)
* **Mayi sambhava Kardama** = O Kardama! Please reside in me (my home)
* **Śriyaṃ vāsaya me kule** = And cause Goddess Shri to dwell permanently in my lineage
* **Mātaraṃ padmamālinīm** = The Divine Mother who is adorned with a garland of lotuses.

**Translation:**
Goddess Lakshmi appeared through Sage Kardama. Therefore, O Sage Kardama, please reside in my home, and establish your Mother, Goddess Shri—who is beautifully adorned with a garland of lotuses—permanently in my lineage and household.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 12 ॥
आपः॑ सृ॒जन्तु॑ स्नि॒ग्धा॒नि॒ चि॒क्ली॒त व॑स मे॒ गृहे । नि च॑ दे॒वीं मा॒तरं॒ श्रियं॑ वा॒सय॑ मे कु॒ले ॥ १२ ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Āpaḥ sṛjantu snigdhāni** = Let the water gods create unctuous/auspicious and beautiful things
* **Ciklīta vasa me gṛhe** = O Chiklita (another son of Lakshmi)! Reside in my home
* **Ni ca devīṃ mātaraṃ Śriyaṃ** = And the Divine Mother, Goddess Shri
* **Vāsaya me kule** = Cause Her to reside in my lineage forever.

**Translation:**
May the waters create pure, soothing, and beautiful things. O Sage Chiklita (the son of Goddess Lakshmi), please come and reside in my home, and bring along your Divine Mother, Goddess Shri, to dwell in my family forever.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 13 ॥
आ॒र्द्रां पु॒ष्करि॑णीं पु॒ष्टिं॒ पि॒ङ्ग॒लां प॑द्ममा॒लिनीम् । च॒न्द्रां हि॒रण्म॑यीं ल॒क्ष्मीं जात॑वेदो म॒ आव॑ह ॥ १३ ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Ārdrāṃ puṣkariṇīṃ** = Who is moist with compassion (Ardra), and holds a lotus
* **Puṣṭiṃ piṅgalāṃ padmamālinīm** = Who grants nourishment, is of a golden/yellow hue, and wears a lotus garland
* **Candrāṃ hiraṇmayīṃ Lakṣmīṃ** = Radiant like the moon, the golden Goddess Lakshmi
* **Jātavedo ma āvaha** = O Agni! Invoke Her for me.

**Translation:**
O Agni! Invoke for me that golden Goddess Lakshmi, who is deeply compassionate, who dwells in a lotus pool, who nourishes the entire world, whose complexion is a beautiful golden hue, who wears a garland of lotuses, and who is as radiant and cooling as the moon.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 14 ॥
आ॒र्द्रां यः॒ करि॑णीं य॒ष्टिं॒ सु॒व॒र्णां हे॑ममा॒लिनीम् । सू॒र्यां हि॒रण्म॑यीं ल॒क्ष्मीं॒ जात॑वेदो म॒ आव॑ह ॥ १४ ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Ārdrāṃ yaḥ kariṇīṃ yaṣṭiṃ** = Who is compassionate, bestowing abundance, and holding the scepter of authority
* **Suvarṇāṃ hemamālinīm** = Who is of beautiful golden color, adorned with a golden garland
* **Sūryāṃ hiraṇmayīṃ Lakṣmīṃ** = Radiant and blazing like the sun, the golden Goddess Lakshmi
* **Jātavedo ma āvaha** = O Agni! Invoke Her for me.

**Translation:**
O Agni! Invoke for me that golden Goddess Lakshmi, who is full of compassion, who holds the scepter of supreme authority, whose complexion is like pure gold, who is adorned with a golden garland, and who shines with the brilliant, blazing radiance of the sun.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 15 ॥
तां म॒ आव॑ह॒ जात॑वेदो ल॒क्ष्मीमन॑पगा॒मिनी᳚म् । यस्यां॒ हिर॑ण्यं॒ प्रभू॑तं॒ गावो॑ दा॒स्योऽश्वा᳚न्, वि॒न्देयं॒ पुरु॑षान॒हम् ॥ १५ ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Tāṃ ma āvaha Jātavedo** = O Agni! Invoke Her for me
* **Lakṣmīmanapagāminīm** = That Goddess Lakshmi who never departs (is stable)
* **Yasyāṃ hiraṇyaṃ prabhūtaṃ** = By whose arrival I may obtain abundant gold/wealth
* **Gāvo dāsyo'śvān** = Cows, maid-servants, horses
* **Vindeyaṃ puruṣānaham** = And I may attain men (superior progeny and followers).

**Translation:**
O Agni! Invoke for me that ever-abiding Goddess Lakshmi, who will never leave my home. By Her divine grace, may I be blessed with abundant gold, cows, maid-servants, horses, and a multitude of excellent descendants and followers.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 16 (Start of Phalashruti) ॥
यः शुचिः॒ प्रय॑तो भू॒त्वा जु॒हुया॑दाज्य॒मन्व॑हम् । श्रियः॑ प॒ञ्चद॑शर्चं॒ च श्री॒कामः॑ सत॒तं ज॑पेत् ॥ १६ ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning & Translation:**
A person who desires wealth and prosperity (*Shrikamah*) should become pure in body and mind (*shuchi*), practice self-control (*prayato*), and offer oblations of ghee (*ajyam*) into the sacred fire daily (*anvaham*), while constantly chanting these fifteen verses (*panchadasharcham*) dedicated to Goddess Shri.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 17 ॥
प॒द्मा॒न॒ने प॑द्म ऊ॒रू॒ प॒द्माक्षी॑ पद्म॒सम्भ॑वे । त्वं मां᳚ भ॒जस्व॑ प॒द्मा॒क्षी॒ ये॒न सौ᳚ख्यं ल॒भाम्य॑हम् ॥ १७ ॥

**Translation:**
O Goddess whose face is like a lotus! O Goddess whose thighs are like lotus petals! O Lotus-eyed One! O You who are born from a lotus! Please bestow Your loving grace upon me, so that I may attain all forms of happiness in this life.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 18 ॥
अश्व॑दा॒यी गो॑दा॒यी॒ ध॒नदा॑यी म॒हाध॑ने । धनं मे॒ जुष॑तां दे॒वि॒ स॒र्वका॑मांश्च॒ देहि॑ मे ॥ १८ ॥

**Translation:**
O Mother of immense wealth! You are the bestower of horses, cows, and riches. O Divine Goddess! May wealth always delight in me, and may You gracefully fulfill all my heartfelt desires.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 19 ॥
पुत्रपौ॒त्र ध॑नं धा॒न्यं ह॒स्त्यश्वा॑दिग॒वे र॑थम् । प्र॒जा॒नां भ॑वसि मा॒ता आ॒युष्म॑न्तं क॒रोतु॑ माम् ॥ १९ ॥

**Translation:**
O Mother! You grant sons, grandsons, wealth, grains, elephants, horses, cows, and chariots. You are the eternal Mother of all created beings; please bless me with a long, healthy, and prosperous life.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 20 ॥
धन॑म॒ग्निर्ध॑नं वा॒युर्ध॑नं॒ सूर्यो॑ धनं॒ वसुः॑ । धन॒मिन्द्रो॒ बृह॒स्पति॒र्वरु॑णं॒ धन॒मश्नु॑ ते ॥ २० ॥

**Translation:**
By the grace of Goddess Lakshmi, Agni (Fire) becomes wealth, Vayu (Air) becomes wealth, Surya (Sun) becomes wealth, and the Vasus become wealth. Indra, Brihaspati, and Varuna—all the gods become forms of wealth and bring auspiciousness to the devotee.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 21 ॥
वैन॑तेय॒ सोमं॑ पिब॒ सोमं॑ पिबतु वृत्र॒हा । सोमं॒ धन॑स्य सो॒मिनो॒ मह्यं॒ ददा॑तु सो॒मिनः॑ ॥ २१ ॥

**Translation:**
O Vainateya (Garuda)! Drink the Soma juice. Let Indra (the slayer of the demon Vritra) also drink the Soma. May all the gods who partake in the Soma bestow abundant wealth upon me, the performer of the Soma sacrifice.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 22 ॥
न क्रोधो न च॑ मात्स॒र्यं न॒ लोभो॑ नाशु॒भा म॑तिः । भव॑न्ति॒ कृत॑पुण्या॒नां भ॒क्तानां श्रीसू᳚क्तं ज॒पेत्स॑दा ॥ २२ ॥

**Translation:**
Those highly virtuous and devoted individuals who constantly chant the 'Sri Suktam' are never troubled by anger, jealousy, greed, or impure thoughts. Their minds remain eternally pure and peaceful.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 23 ॥
वर्ष᳚न्तु॒ ते वि॑भाव॒रि॒ दि॒वो अ॑भ्रस्य॒ विद्यु॑तः । रोह᳚न्तु॒ सर्व॑बी॒जा॒न्य॒व ब्र॑ह्म द्वि॒षो ज॑हि ॥ २३ ॥

**Translation:**
O Radiant Mother (Vibhavari)! May the clouds in the sky shower their rains accompanied by lightning for us. May all seeds sprout and grow properly. And O Mother, please destroy those who harbor enmity against Brahman (the truth/righteousness).

---

#### ॥ Mantra 24 ॥
पद्म॑प्रिये पद्मिनि पद्म॒हस्ते पद्मा॑लये पद्मदलाय॑ताक्षि । विश्व॑प्रिये॒ विष्णु मनो॑ऽनुकू॒ले त्वत्पा॑दप॒द्मं मयि॒ सन्नि॑धस्त्व ॥ २४ ॥

**Translation:**
O Goddess who loves lotuses, who is a lotus Herself, who holds a lotus in Her hand, who dwells in a lotus, and whose eyes are as wide and beautiful as lotus petals! O Beloved of the universe, who always acts according to the mind of Lord Vishnu! Please establish Your lotus feet forever in my heart.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 25 ॥
या सा पद्मा॑सन॒स्था विपुलकटितटी पद्म॒पत्रा॑यता॒क्षी । गम्भीरा व॑र्तना॒भिः स्तनभर नमिता शुभ्र वस्त्रो॑त्तरी॒या ।
लक्ष्मीर्दि॒व्यै-र्गजेन्द्रैर्म॒णिगण खचितै-स्स्नापिता हे॑मकु॒म्भैः । नि॒त्यं सा प॑द्मह॒स्ता मम वस॑तु गृ॒हे सर्व॒माङ्गल्य॑युक्ता ॥ २५ ॥

**Translation:**
May that Goddess Lakshmi always reside in my home—She who is seated on a lotus, who has a broad waist and eyes like lotus petals; whose navel is deep and circular, who is slightly bent by the weight of her breasts, and who is adorned in pristine white garments. She is continuously bathed by celestial elephants using golden pitchers studded with jewels. She holds a lotus in Her hand and is endowed with all universal auspiciousness.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 26 ॥
ल॒क्ष्मीं क्षीरसमुद्र राजतनयां श्री॒रंगधामे॑श्वरीम् । दा॒सीभूतसमस्त देव व॒नितां लो॒कैक॒ दीपां॑कुराम् ।
श्रीमन्मन्दकटाक्षलब्ध विभव ब्र॒ह्मेन्द्रगङ्गा॑धराम् । त्वां त्रै॒लोक्य॒ कुटु॑म्बिनीं स॒रसिजां वन्दे॒ मुकु॑न्दप्रियाम् ॥ २६ ॥

**Translation:**
I bow to Goddess Lakshmi, the daughter of the King of the Milky Ocean, the Sovereign of Srirangam (Lord Vishnu's abode). All celestial goddesses serve Her as maidservants. She is the sole guiding light of the entire world. Brahma, Indra, and Gangadhara (Shiva) obtained their majestic powers and wealth merely by Her gentle, compassionate glance. I bow to Her, who considers the three worlds as Her family, who dwells in the lotus, and who is the beloved of Lord Mukunda (Vishnu).

---

#### ॥ Mantra 27 ॥
सि॒द्ध॒ल॒क्ष्मी-र्मो॑क्षल॒क्ष्मी॒-र्ज॒यल॑क्ष्मीस्स॒रस्व॑ती । श्रीलक्ष्मीर्व॑रल॒क्ष्मी॒श्च॒ प्र॒सन्ना म॑म स॒र्वदा ।
वरांकुशौ पाशमभी॑तिमु॒द्रां॒ क॒रैर्वहन्तीं क॑मला॒सनस्थाम् । बालार्क कोटि प्रति॑भां त्रि॒णे॒त्रां॒ भ॒जेहमाद्यां ज॑गदी॒श्वरीं त्वाम् ॥ २७ ॥

**Translation:**
May Siddha Lakshmi, Moksha Lakshmi, Jaya Lakshmi, Saraswati, Sri Lakshmi, and Vara Lakshmi always remain pleased with me. I worship the primordial Supreme Goddess of the Universe, who holds the boon-giving mudra, an elephant goad, a noose, and the fear-dispelling mudra in Her four hands. She is seated on a lotus, possesses the radiant brilliance of millions of rising suns, and is three-eyed.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 28 ॥
स॒र्व॒म॒ङ्ग॒ल॒मा॒ङ्गल्ये॑ शि॒वे स॒र्वार्थ॑ साधिके । शर॑ण्ये त्र्यम्ब॑के दे॒वि॒ ना॒राय॑णि न॒मोऽस्तु॑ ते ॥ २८ ॥

**Translation:**
O Auspiciousness of all that is auspicious! O Consort of Shiva, who fulfills all objectives (Dharma, Artha, Kama, Moksha)! O Bestower of refuge, O Three-eyed Goddess! Salutations to You again and again, O Narayani.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 29 (Mahalakshmi Gayatri) ॥
म॒हा॒ल॒क्ष्मी च॑ वि॒द्महे॑ विष्णुप॒त्नी च॑ धीमहि । तन्नो॑ लक्ष्मीः प्रचो॒दया᳚त् ॥ २९ ॥

**Translation:**
We realize Goddess Mahalakshmi; we meditate upon the beloved consort of Lord Vishnu. May that Goddess Lakshmi illuminate our intellects and inspire us toward the righteous path.

---

#### ॐ शान्तिः॒ शान्तिः॒ शान्तिः॑ ॥
May there be absolute peace from physical, divine, and internal suffering.

---

### 3. Benefits of Recitation (Phalashruti Summary)

Chanting these sacred mantras of the 'Sri Suktam' daily with purity of mind and body ensures that poverty and misfortune never enter one's home. Offering oblations (Homa) into the fire with cow's ghee using these 15 verses bestows the grace of *Ashta Lakshmi* (the eight forms of wealth). It destroys debts, diseases, family strife, and ignorance, ultimately granting the devotee an abundance of wealth, fame, and supreme liberation (Moksha).
