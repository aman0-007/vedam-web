## ॥ Shri Vishnu Suktam ॥
**Complete Text, Word-by-Word Meaning, Translation & Significance**

---

### 1. Introduction & Spiritual Significance

'Shri Vishnu Suktam' is a highly energetic and powerful Vedic hymn found in both the Rig Veda and the Yajur Veda, dedicated to the Supreme Lord Mahavishnu. This Suktam glorifies the 'Trivikrama' (Vamana avatar) form of Lord Vishnu, who measured the entire cosmic universe with just three mighty strides.

In the Sanatana tradition, this Suktam is chanted with precise Vedic intonations during Satyanarayana Puja, Vishnu Yajna, and other auspicious ceremonies. It contains a profoundly beautiful description of Vishnu's *Parama Padam* (the supreme abode of liberation). Chanting this hymn daily destroys all sins, bestows immense spiritual and material wealth, and ultimately leads the seeker to the supreme abode of Lord Vishnu, which the wise sages constantly behold.

* **Source:** Rig Veda (Mandalas 1 and 7) and Krishna Yajur Veda (Taittiriya Samhita/Aranyaka)
* **Presiding Deity:** Lord Shri Mahavishnu
* **Purpose (Viniyoga):** For the attainment of worldly success and spiritual perfection, protection from distress, and ultimate liberation (Vishnu Sayujya).

---

### 2. Sanskrit Verses with Svaras, Word-by-Word Meaning & Translation

#### ॥ Mantra 1 ॥
ॐ विष्णो॒र्नुकं॑ वी॒र्या॑णि॒ प्रोवो॑चं॒ यः पार्थि॑वानि विम॒मे रजाग्ं॑सि॒ यो अस्क॑भाय॒दुत्त॑रग्ं
स॒धस्थं॑ विचक्रमा॒णस्त्रे॒धोरु॑गा॒यः । विष्णो॑र॒राट॑मसि॒ विष्णोः᳚ पृ॒ष्ठम॑सि॒ विष्णोः॒
श्नप्त्रे᳚स्थो॒ विष्णोः॒ स्स्यूर॑सि॒ विष्णो᳚र्ध्रुवम॑सि वैष्ण॒वम॑सि॒ विष्ण॑वे त्वा ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Om viṣṇornukaṃ vīryāṇi provocaṃ** = I now proclaim the great heroic deeds (vīryāṇi) of Lord Vishnu
* **Yaḥ pārthivāni vimame rajāgṃsi** = Who measured out (vimame) the earthly regions and all the worlds (rajāgṃsi)
* **Yo askabhāyaduttaragṃ sadhasthaṃ** = Who propped up and stabilized the upper dwelling places (the heavens)
* **Vicakramāṇastredhorugāyaḥ** = Striding out in three ways (tredhā) with vast steps (urugāyaḥ)
* **Viṣṇorarāṭamasi... viṣṇave tvā** = (Yajur Vedic mantra for the sacrificial grass/altar) O Kusha grass! You are the forehead of Vishnu, the back of Vishnu, the chin (śnaptre) of Vishnu, the seam/joint (ssyūra) of Vishnu, the steadfast center of Vishnu. You are the embodiment of Vishnu; to Vishnu I offer you.

**Translation:**
I loudly proclaim the magnificent, heroic deeds of Lord Mahavishnu, who measured the earthly regions and all the worlds with His vast strides. He firmly stabilized the upper heavens and paced the three worlds with three massive steps. (For the ritual altar) O sacred grass, you represent the forehead, back, face, and the eternal form of Lord Vishnu. For the pleasure of Lord Vishnu, I establish and dedicate you.

#### ॥ Mantra 2 ॥
तद॑स्य प्रि॒यम॒भिपाथो॑ अश्याम् । नरो॒ यत्र॑ देव॒यवो॒ मद॑न्ति । उ॒रु॒क्र॒मस्य॒ स हि
बन्धु॑रि॒त्था । विष्णोः᳚ प॒दे प॑र॒मे मध्व॒ उथ्सः॑ ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Tadasya priyamabhipātho aśyām** = May I attain (aśyām) that beloved abode (pātho) of His
* **Naro yatra devayavo madanti** = Where men devoted to the Divine rejoice in bliss (madanti)
* **Urukramasya sa hi bandhuritthā** = For that wide-striding Lord (urukrama) is truly our kin/friend
* **Viṣṇoḥ pade parame madhva uthsaḥ** = In the supreme step/abode (parame pade) of Vishnu, there is a wellspring (uthsaḥ) of honey/nectar (madhva).

**Translation:**
May I attain that supremely beloved abode of Lord Vishnu (Vaikuntha), where devotees dedicated to the Divine rejoice in eternal bliss. That wide-striding Lord Trivikrama is indeed our truest friend and relative. In that highest, supreme abode of Lord Vishnu, there is an eternal, flowing wellspring of sweet nectar and absolute joy.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 3 ॥
प्रतद्विष्णु॑स्स्तवते वी॒र्या॑य । मृ॒गो न भी॒मः कु॑च॒रो गि॑रि॒ष्ठाः । यस्यो॒रुषु॑ त्रि॒षु
वि॒क्रम॑णेषु । अधि॑क्षि॒यन्ति॒ भुव॑नानि॒ विश्वा᳚ ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Pratadviṣṇusstavate vīryāya** = Therefore, Lord Vishnu is praised for His heroic might
* **Mṛgo na bhīmaḥ kuçaro giriṣṭhāḥ** = Like a fierce (bhīmaḥ) roaming lion (mṛgo) dwelling in the mountains
* **Yasyoruṣu triṣu vikramaṇeṣu** = Within whose three vast (uruṣu) strides (vikramaṇeṣu)
* **Adhikṣiyanti bhuvanāni viśvā** = All the worlds and beings (viśvā bhuvanāni) safely reside.

**Translation:**
We praise the mighty valor of Lord Vishnu, who is as formidable and invincible as a fierce lion roaming freely through the mountains. Within His three massive strides alone, this entire cosmic universe and all its realms are completely encompassed and securely reside.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 4 ॥
प॒रो मात्र॑या त॒नुवा॑ वृधान । न ते॑ महि॒त्वमन्व॑श्नुवन्ति ॥ उ॒भे ते॑ विद्म॒ रज॑सी पृथि॒व्या
विष्णो॑ देव॒त्वम् । प॒र॒मस्य॑ विथ्से ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Paro mātrayā tanuvā vṛdhāna** = Growing (vṛdhāna) beyond all measures and limits with Your body (tanuvā)
* **Na te mahitvamanvaśnuvanti** = No one can fully attain or comprehend Your greatness (mahitvam)
* **Ubhe te vidma rajasī pṛthivyā** = We mortals only know the two realms (earth and the sky)
* **Viṣṇo devatvam paramasya vithse** = O Vishnu, You alone know (vithse) the supreme divinity (of Yourself).

**Translation:**
O Supreme Lord Vishnu! Your divine body and glory expand far beyond all human measures and boundaries. No one can ever fully comprehend or measure the true extent of Your greatness. We ignorant mortals can only perceive these two realms of earth and sky; but O Vishnu, only You truly know the magnitude of Your own supreme, transcendental divinity.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 5 ॥
विच॑क्रमे पृथि॒वीमे॒ष ए॒ताम् । क्षेत्रा॑य॒ विष्णु॒र्मनु॑षे दश॒स्यन् । ध्रु॒वासो॑ अस्य की॒रयो॒
जना॑सः । ऊ॒रु॒क्षि॒तिग्ं सु॒जनि॑माचकार ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Vicakrame pṛthivīmeṣa etām** = He (Vishnu) strode over this earth
* **Kṣetrāya viṣṇurmanuṣe daśasyan** = To bestow (daśasyan) a dwelling place (kṣetrāya) for mankind
* **Dhruvāso asya kīrayo janāsaḥ** = His praisers/devotees (kīrayo janāsaḥ) remain eternally secure and stable (dhruvāso)
* **Ūrukṣitigṃ sujanimācakāra** = By His noble incarnation (sujanima), He created a vast and safe abode (ūrukṣitigṃ) for all.

**Translation:**
Lord Mahavishnu strode across this entire earth with the desire to provide a safe dwelling place for mankind. Those devoted people who sing His praises remain forever stable, protected, and secure. Through His auspicious incarnation as Vamana, He gracefully provided a vast and secure refuge for all living beings.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 6 ॥
त्रिर्दे॒वः पृ॑थि॒वीमे॒ष ए॒ताम् । विच॑क्रमे श॒तर्च॑सं महि॒त्वा । प्रविष्णु॑रस्तु
त॒वस॒स्तवी॑यान् । त्वे॒षग्ग् ह्य॑स्य॒ स्थवि॑रस्य॒ नाम॑ ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning:**
* **Trirdevaḥ pṛthivīmeṣa etām vicakrame** = This radiant Lord strode over this earth three times (with three steps)
* **Śatarcasaṃ mahitvā** = With His hundred-fold (infinite) radiant greatness (mahitvā)
* **Praviṣṇurastu tavasastavīyān** = May Lord Vishnu be the most powerful (tavīyān) among the mighty
* **Tveṣagg hyasya sthavirasya nāma** = For the name of this ancient/eternal (sthavirasya) Lord is intensely brilliant (tveṣam).

**Translation:**
This luminous Lord Vishnu, through His infinite and hundred-fold radiant greatness, measured this entire earth with just three strides. May Lord Mahavishnu be the mightiest among all powerful beings. The name and glory of this eternal, ancient Deity are exceptionally brilliant and resplendent.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 7 ॥
अतो॑ दे॒वा अ॑वन्तुनो॒ यतो॒ विष्णु॑-र्विचक्र॒मे । पृ॒थि॒व्यास्स॒प्त धाम॑भिः । इ॒दं
विष्णु-र्विच॑क्रमे त्रे॒धा निद॑धे प॒दम् । समू॑ढमस्य पाग्ंसु॒रे । त्रीणि॑ प॒दा विच॑क्रमे॒
विष्णु॑र्गो॒पा अदा᳚भ्यः । ततो॒ धर्मा॑॑णि धा॒रयन्॑ । विष्णो॒: कर्मा॑णि पश्यत॒ यतो᳚ व्र॒तानि॑
पस्प॒शे । इन्द्र॑स्य॒ युज्य॒स्सखा᳚ ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning & Translation:**
* **Ato devā avantuno yato viṣṇurvicakrame | Pṛthivyāssapta dhāmabhiḥ |** = May the gods protect us from that supreme place from where Lord Vishnu strode across the seven realms of the earth.
* **Idaṃ viṣṇurvicakrame tredhā nidadhe padam | Samūḍhamasya pāgṃsure |** = Lord Vishnu traversed this universe and placed His foot in three ways. The entire cosmos is gathered and contained within the sacred dust (pāgṃsure) of His feet.
* **Trīṇi padā vicakrame viṣṇurgopā adābhyaḥ | Tato dharmāṇi dhārayan |** = Vishnu, the invincible and undeceivable protector (gopā), took three great strides, thereby establishing and upholding all cosmic laws and Dharmas.
* **Viṣṇo: karmāṇi paśyata yato vratāni paspaśe | Indrasya yujyassakhā ||** = Behold the magnificent deeds of Lord Vishnu, through which He observes and fulfills the sacred vows of His devotees. He is the most worthy and intimate friend of Indra.

---

#### ॥ Mantra 8 ॥
तद्विष्णोः᳚ पर॒मं प॒दंग्ं सदा॑ पश्यन्ति सू॒रयः॑ । दि॒वीव॒ चक्षु॒रात॑तम् । तद्विप्रा॑सो
विप॒न्यवो॑ जागृ॒वाग्ं स॒स्समि॑न्धते । विष्णो॒-र्यत्प॑र॒मं प॒दम् । पर्या᳚प्त्या अन॑न्तरायाय॒
सर्व॑स्तोमोऽति रा॒त्र उ॑त्त॒म मह॑र्भवति॒ सर्व॒स्याप्त्यै॒ सर्व॑स्य॒ जित्यै॒ सर्व॑मे॒व तेना᳚प्नोति॒
सर्वं॑ जयति ॥

**Word-by-Word Meaning & Translation:**
* **Tadviṣṇoḥ paramaṃ padagṃ sadā paśyanti sūrayaḥ | Divīva cakṣurātatam |** = The wise and enlightened sages continuously behold that supreme abode (Parama Padam) of Lord Vishnu, just as clearly as an eye extended across the sky sees the Sun.
* **Tadviprāso vipanyavo jāgṛvāgṃ sassamindhate | Viṣṇo-ryatparamaṃ padam |** = That supreme abode of Vishnu is illuminated and realized within the hearts of the wise (viprāso), those who sing praises (vipanyavo), and the ever-wakeful, vigilant seekers.
* **Paryāptyā anantarāyāya sarvastomo'ti rātra uttama maharbhavati sarvasyāptyai sarvasya jityai sarvameva tenāpnoti sarvaṃ jayati ||** = (This is the Yajur Vedic Phalashruti): The chanting of this Suktam is for total completeness and the removal of all obstacles. It yields the supreme merit of the great 'Sarvastoma Atiratra' sacrifice. Through this, one attains everything and conquers everything. The seeker undoubtedly achieves all desires and emerges victorious over all hurdles.

---

#### ॐ शान्तिः॒ शान्तिः॒ शान्तिः॑ ॥
May there be absolute peace from physical, divine, and internal suffering.

---

### 3. Benefits of Recitation (Phalashruti Summary)

In the Vedic tradition, chanting the 'Shri Vishnu Suktam' is considered immensely auspicious. Singing the glories of Lord Vishnu's Trivikrama (Vamana) avatar liberates the seeker from all forms of fear and obstacles. For devotees yearning to attain Vishnu's 'Parama Padam' (the ultimate state of liberation or Moksha), this Suktam serves as a direct pathway. Its daily recitation brings inexhaustible wealth, a secure home, profound inner peace, and ultimate victory in all spheres of life.
